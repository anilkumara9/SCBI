## 1. The load-bearing flaw: 1-NN statistic is structurally confounded by duplicate triples

**Finding.** The 1-NN leave-one-out statistic, as registered, cannot distinguish "relational task information" from "triple-identity / entity-set encoding" — and the bench's duplicate-triple structure guarantees a spurious signal.

**Evidence (all computed from the registered bench builder, this review):**

- `TRIPLES_INDICES` contains 15 entries but only **10 unique triples** (indices 10–14 repeat 0–4).
- `QUADS_INDICES` contains 15 entries but only **5 unique quads**, each repeated **3 times**.
- Across the 60-item bench: **50/60 items (83.3%) have at least one duplicate triple/quad** in the bench (verified by execution against `benchmark_exp091.py`).
- The label is a **deterministic function of the triple**: every item records `"ent": A` where `A = VOCAB[iA]`, the first index of the triple/quad. Verified: duplicate triples **always** share the label (no exceptions in 60 items).
- The premise (the embedded state text) **always mentions the label entity** ("Premise: {A} outranks {B}..." or "Premise: {C} is lower than {B}...").

**Consequence.** Any layer embedding that encodes the entity set — which every transformer layer does to some degree — enables 1-NN to match duplicate triples (3/3 or 4/4 entity overlap strictly exceeds any partial overlap). The duplicate always carries the same label. A pure entity-set oracle therefore achieves **≥83.3% accuracy** against a 10% chance baseline, without any relational information whatsoever. The registered CONTINUE branch requires only `(a_l − q95_l) ≥ 10pp` with Bonferroni significance; the confound alone supplies ~70pp of margin. **CONTINUE is structurally biased to trigger at every layer, for reasons unrelated to the hypothesis.**

**Why the permutation null cannot save it.** The stratified null permutes labels within (domain × phrasing) at the item level. This breaks the triple→label association that the observed statistic exploits. For the null to calibrate the confound, it would need to preserve P(label | triple) — but the label is a deterministic function of the triple, so any null preserving P(label | triple) is degenerate (permuting within triple-groups permutes identical labels). **No valid null exists for this statistic on this bench.** The test is uncalibratable, not merely underpowered.

**Why this is load-bearing, not a caveat.** The programmatic purpose of EXP092 is to adjudicate the fork: bridge power = misplaced *task* (relational) information vs construction artifact. A CONTINUE driven by triple-identity encoding does not adjudicate the fork — triple identity does not explain the bridge's +10pp relational rescue. It would misdirect the program ("redirect readout to l*") on the basis of a surface feature. The draft's Caveat 4 ("detects information about *which* entity is correct, not about the relational reasoning") does not cover this: the confound is not "which entity" (task-adjacent) but "which triple" (bench-structural). The KILL branch is effectively unreachable, so the experiment cannot falsify — it can only misfire.

## 2. Findings on the seven review points

### (1) Precise question and decision naming
The question (§1) is operationally well-posed. The decision (CONTINUE vs KILL the "misplaced information" program) is correctly named for the fork. **However**, the registered statistic does not test the stated question: it tests triple-identity predictability, not relational task information. The question-to-statistic mapping is broken (see §1 above).

### (2) Cheapest falsifying test and cost
The test is cheap ($0 CPU, ~1.5h; the cost breakdown is credible and consistent with EXP091's measured runtime). But a confounded test is not a falsifying test at any price. The "cheapest" claim is moot.

### (3) Math license, 10pp bar, Bonferroni, layer-20 exclusion
- **Fano grounding: SOUND.** Verified: at chance (P_e = 1−1/K), the Fano bound is tight (H(P_e) + P_e·log(K−1) = log K for K=10); above-chance accuracy certifies I(h_l;y) > 0. The draft correctly notes the converse fails (Caveat 3).
- **10pp bar: PRE-REGISTERED, rationale implicit.** The dossier states it as the quantitative prediction; it matches the bridge's observed effect size (+10pp, EXP077), which is the program's de facto meaningful-signal magnitude. Acceptable as a pre-registered practical-significance gate, though the anchor should be stated explicitly.
- **Bonferroni (α_B = 0.05/24 ≈ 2.083e-3): CORRECT.** Breaking point (no layer significant → KILL) is the right falsification logic.
- **Permutation granularity (1,000/layer): THIN BUT ADEQUATE.** Minimum achievable p = 1/1001 ≈ 9.99e-4 < α_B, so significance is detectable. *Observation (not load-bearing):* 2,500+ permutations would give more headroom near the threshold.
- **Layer-20 exclusion from CONTINUE: CORRECT.** EXP091 read layer 20 with a different instrument (cosine scoring); a layer-20 1-NN signal implicates the instrument, not misplacement. The §7.1 totality note states this clearly.

### (4) Bench contract (Law #9 reuse)
The Law #9 reuse is procedurally legitimate (deterministic builder, archive hash pin, no new construction). **But** the reused bench's duplicate-triple structure (50/60 items) is incompatible with the 1-NN statistic for the stated question. Law #9 compliance does not cure a statistic–bench mismatch. The readout change (per-layer 1-NN vs cosine) does not demand new bench *validation* — it demands a bench *without* the duplicate structure, which Law #9 forbids constructing. This is the core tension the draft does not resolve.

No guard defects.

### (6) Hypothesis shift vs the dossier's Law #15 block
**No silent shift.** Verified point-by-point against `research/innovation/SPRINT_2026-09-25_SUMMIT.md` Candidate 1: Q1 (question) verbatim; Q2 (decision) faithful; Q3 (cheapest test + $0/1.5h) faithful, with the dossier-§6-mandated S2 baseline correctly included; Q4 (Fano license, ≥10pp prediction, Bonferroni breaking point) faithful. The succession banner correctly states EXP091 is NOT superseded. **The flaw originates in the dossier's Law #15 block itself** (it specifies the same 1-NN approach without considering the duplicate structure) — but the draft is where it becomes actionable, and this review binds the draft.

## 3. Why SIGN-WITH-FIXES is not viable

A fix would need to (a) preserve the Law #15 question, (b) respect Law #9 (no new bench), and (c) de-confound the statistic. No such fix exists:

- **Triple-level permutation** is degenerate (label is deterministic given the triple; permuting within triple-groups permutes identical labels).
- **Excluding duplicates** leaves N too small (3-hop quads have zero unique instances; 2-hop has 5 unique triples per domain) and violates the registered §6 bench contract.
- **Redefining the label** to a relational property is impossible without new bench construction (Law #9).
- **Baselining against a duplicate-oracle** (require a_l to exceed set-overlap-oracle accuracy by 10pp) is post-hoc and does not restore the Fano-based "task information" interpretation.

The Candidate 1 design needs fundamental rethinking, not a patch. Recommended directions for the redesign: (i) a bench without duplicate triples (new number, Law #9 fresh construction with a no-duplicate invariant asserted at build time); (ii) a label requiring relational discrimination (e.g., pairwise ranking judgments rather than entity identification); or (iii) a statistic conditioned on the triple (e.g., within-triple phrasing discrimination, though label variance is absent).

---

## 4. Binding ruling

**REJECT.** EXP092 as drafted may not proceed to signing. The 1-NN leave-one-out statistic is structurally confounded by the bench's duplicate triples (50/60 items; label deterministic given the triple), the stratified permutation null cannot calibrate for this confound, and the CONTINUE branch is biased to trigger on bench structure rather than task information. The experiment cannot adjudicate the bridge-artifact vs misplaced-information fork as designed.

This rejection does not touch: EXP091's ADOPTED KILL (LOG-361/362), the LOG-204 bridge demotion, or any other program result. It rejects only this pre-registration.
