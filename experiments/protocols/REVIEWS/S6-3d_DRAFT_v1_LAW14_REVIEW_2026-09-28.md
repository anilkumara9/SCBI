## 2. The two re-opened questions — binding adjudications

EXP102's verdict (LOG-4455) leaves the τ-principledness and no-peeking questions re-opened for this re-gating, "since any other re-gating re-opens the τ-principledness and no-peeking questions this verdict leaves untouched." This review answers both. Both HOLD.

### 2a. No-peeking — HOLDS (attacked hardest)

**The attack.** Max was chosen *because* mean collapsed at L20 (1/60) and *because* max fired at L11 (16/60) — is that outcome-tuning with extra steps? And is the "conservative-first" ordering now a polite way of trying the other thing?

**What I verified:**
1. **τ is byte-identical to the adjudicated constant.** `τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i)` appears in the S6-3d draft in the identical byte sequence as in S6-3c v2 (verified by byte-level grep on both files — the `2·` middle-dot form matches exactly). No numeric threshold moved.
2. **No L20-max attention masses exist anywhere in the program record.** Searched `research/` and `experiments/` for L20-max mass quantities (`mass_L20_max`, `L20_max`, `l20max`, `L20-max` mass columns): zero hits. The three gate CSVs carry exactly what the draft's §0.2 claims: `mass` (L11 mean, EXP100), `mass_max`/`mass_mean` (L11, EXP101), `mass_L20_mean` (EXP102). The draft's bright-line factual claim is verified true, not asserted. No L20-max-gated run has ever been executed (§0.3).
3. **The max-at-L20 choice comes from the license record, twice over.** LOG-4443's endorsed conservative-first convention (confirmed in LOG-4450's log summary: "conservative-first is LOG-4443's endorsed discrete-ordering convention") pre-named max-at-L20 as the S6-3d candidate; EXP102's binding verdict breaking point (iv) names "max-at-L20" as "the surviving externally-computed attention-gate candidate requiring fresh pre-registration and Law #14 review." The choice is the verdict's own prescription applied to the convention's second discrete step — not smuggled in.
4. **Firing observations are not calibration measurements.** "Max fired 16/60 at L11" and "mean collapsed 1/60 at L20" are *firing* observations. No S6-3d parameter is a function of them: τ is byte-identical, the aggregation is a discrete pre-named choice, the firing rule is closed-form. There is no tuning surface.

**The honest residual (the draft states it in §6 risk #6; I confirm it).** The *research direction* is outcome-informed — had mean fired at L20, max-at-L20 would not be the next move. That is not peeking. Peeking is outcome-dependence of *design parameters*; the license standard is the absence of a tuning surface, not the chooser's ignorance (LOG-4443's binding adjudication, re-applied by LOG-4450 §2a). The draft states the distinction and asks Law #14 to verify it explicitly — verified.

**Adjudication (binding): motivation-by-observation is distinguished from calibration-by-observation. The no-peeking bright line HOLDS.** The draft's §0.2 bright-line statement is verified true: no parameter is a function of any EXP094–102 measurement.

### 2b. τ-principledness — HOLDS (F5 carried, aggregation change does not re-open it)

The F5 binding adjudication (LOG-4436) licensed τ = 2×-uniform as a fail-safe pre-registered heuristic under three conditions: (i) no peeking surface, (ii) fail-safe miscalibration, (iii) the verdict never hinges on the constant. All three re-verified for the L20-max read (the draft's §6 risk #7 states them explicitly): (i) the constant is unchanged, closed-form, byte-identical, and §2a holds; (ii) too selective → step 2b PIVOT, too permissive → step 2c vacuous-gate flag, and the gate alone cannot manufacture the step-5 conjunction; (iii) miscalibration yields PIVOT-untestable or a weak KILL, never a false CONTINUE. The aggregation change moves the *head-selection*, not the constant — the principledness fight about the number stays settled.

**Adjudication (binding): τ remains licensable as a fail-safe pre-registered heuristic under the L20-max read. The "why this aggregation" question is answered by §2a's license record, not by re-litigating the constant.**
