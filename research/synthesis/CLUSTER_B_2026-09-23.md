## 1. Evidence audit (track 8 lead) — input to synthesis §A

Method: every SCPM result in the §2 evidence base is audited for leakage,
oracle information, benchmark-construction artifacts, weak controls,
multiple-comparison problems, underpowered designs, and invalid
interpretations. Survivors become constraints; non-survivors are marked and
excluded from the synthesis's foundations.

### 1.1 Results that survive as L1 evidence (with stated limits)

**R1. Autonomous per-instance candidate selection, EXP023/EXP024 [FACT/OBSERVATION].**
Autonomous (frozen E_CF, label-free) selection: +7.0pp on Seed 84 (N=100,
CI [+0.0300,+0.1200], 87.5% of oracle headroom), +6.0pp on Seed 168 (75.0%
headroom recovery); pooled b=14, c=1, exact McNemar p=0.000488. Candidate
selectivity vs random: +4.0pp (experiment_report.md:1588).
[INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] Verdict on "frozen counterfactual evaluation
selects beneficial coordinates better than random": **Supported** — two fresh
seeds, pre-registered confirmatory splits, exact test, CI strictly positive.
Evidentiary level: **L1 only**. It does not show a new strategy (L2): the
procedure is generate-candidates → evaluate → select, i.e. Family C
(inference-time search), already populated by ToT, Self-Refine, PPLM.
**Limits the synthesis must respect:** (a) these results predate the
forced-baseline law — no matched-compute comparison against best-of-N,
debate/MoA, or a bigger frozen model exists; (b) the treatment uses multiple
forward passes per instance vs single-pass greedy baseline — compute parity
was not controlled; (c) BENCH-002's error structure ("distractor bias" as
the dominant failure mode) was not audited by this cluster for
construction artifacts — a benchmark whose errors are distractor-driven is
maximally amenable to contrast-direction interventions, so the effect size
may not generalize; (d) the "AUTONOMOUS SCBI CONFIRMED"
[THEOREM/OBSERVATION] label in the corpus is challenged in §6 (selection
bias) — until that challenge is discharged, cite R1 as **Supported (L1,
selection-bias audit pending)**, not as confirmed.

**R2. Output-bridge rescues [FACT/OBSERVATION].** EXP065: +16.67pp (10/19,
p=0.0020). EXP066: +13.33pp (8/26, p=0.0078). EXP077 official GPU: +10pp
(b=6, c=0, p=0.03125). Local smoke (EXP077 bench): +23.33pp (b=14, c=0,
p=0.000122). EXP070 C7: +16.67pp (p=0.001953). EXP064: +5.0pp (3/3, ceiling).
[INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] Verdict on "label-informed output-space linear
directions rescue decision errors": **Supported** — replicated across
160M/410M, L10/L20, five benchmarks. **What it licenses:** an existence
proof — the errors are at least partly readout-accessible; the causal power
observed in this program lives output-side. **What it does NOT license:**
any autonomous-discovery claim (the bridges are label-informed: constructed
from error instances / unembedding rows — per G1 report §2, the EXP077
bridge was reconstructed from the bench's 60 items plus unembedding rows),
any concept-geometry mechanism (EXP078: the bridge's projected energy in the
concept subspace is 0.054399 < 0.10 — its causal power lies mostly OUTSIDE
the tested concept subspace), or any L2/L3 claim — this is pure **L1**.
**Validity notes the synthesis must carry:** (i) EXP077's official +10pp
(p=0.03125) does **not** survive a multiple-comparison correction across
its ~8 conditions (Bonferroni α≈0.006) — it is a suggestive positive
control, not a significant one; (ii) EXP066's p=0.0078 is borderline under
Bonferroni (0.05/6=0.0083) — passes, barely; the corpus reports
uncorrected p-values, so all bridge claims should be read as
**one-sided confirmatory, uncorrected**; (iii) the smoke-vs-official gap
(+23.33pp local vs +10pp official GPU on the same bench) is a
reproducibility red flag on the *magnitude* — the direction replicates,
the size does not; do not cite a specific pp number as a stable quantity.

**R3. The static-geometry null series (boundary claim I1) [FACT/OBSERVATION].**
Static B_agg injection: ΔM=0, p=1.0, 0/19 (EXP065), 0/26 (EXP066), 0/60-class
(EXP077 radial/cone/offset/replication arms). Raw cross-vocabulary cosine
≈0.7 coexists with zero causal transfer. [INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] Verdict
on I1 ("raw cross-vocabulary geometric similarity ≈0.7 does not yield

conditional variants survive.

## 2. Strongest prior-art equivalence attacks (track 4 lead) — input to synthesis §B

For each: exactly where the equivalence holds, exactly where it breaks.
Citations below are from the program's verified literature audit
(`research/literature/audit_2026-09-23.md`, all URLs verified 2026-09-22/23
via public web search) or from fresh verification in this dispatch
(marked). Nothing is invented; unverifiable items are tagged UNVERIFIED.

### 2.1 The tested static mechanism — equivalence is total

**CAA (Rimsky et al., 2023 / ACL 2024; arXiv:2312.06681) and ActAdd (Turner
et al., 2023; arXiv:2308.10248).** [FACT] Operator-level identity:
v = h̄₊ − h̄₋, h ← h + c·v, frozen backbone, inference-time application.
SCBI's B_agg is a mean contrast direction applied additively; aggregation
over instances and vocabulary mapping are preprocessing — they do not
change the applied operator. **Equivalence holds** at the mathematical,
algorithmic, and subsumption levels (SCBI Stage A ⊆ diff-in-means
steering). **It breaks** only where SCBI *claims* but has not tested:
per-instance construction, self-evaluation, selection. A claim is not a
mechanism. **Attack strength: maximal.** The tested mechanism is N0.

**Supporting fire (each independently weakens any novelty reading):**
- *Jorgensen et al. (2023), mean-centring (arXiv:2312.03813):* SCBI omitted
  anisotropy correction — its ~0.7 cosine likely inflates the bias
  direction. A methodological *deficit* vs prior art, not parity.
- *Ethayarajh (EMNLP 2019; arXiv:1909.00512):* anisotropy makes raw cosine
  ≈0.7 weak evidence — the exact control SCBI omitted.
- *Tan et al. (NeurIPS 2024; arXiv:2407.12404), Braun et al. (2025;
  arXiv:2505.22637):* SCBI's nulls are *predicted* by the steering
  literature (unsteerable behaviors, incoherent directions) — not
  discoveries.
- *Bansal et al. (NeurIPS 2021; arXiv:2106.07682), model stitching:*
  alignment ≠ causal transfer is already established — I1 is a
  re-derivation in a narrower setting.
- *Smith et al. (ICLR 2017; arXiv:1702.03859), Mikolov et al. (2013;
  arXiv:1309.4168):* the Procrustes/linear-map recipe is 2013/2017 math;
  SCBI's instance was defective (rank-2 unembedding fit → hidden-state
  directions).

### 2.2 The claimed dynamic loop — the equivalence attacks that actually threaten it

**PPLM (Dathathri et al., ICLR 2020; arXiv:1912.02164) — closest
dynamic-intervention prior.** [FACT] Equivalence holds at: frozen LM,
per-instance inference-time updates to hidden activations ΔH_t (θ never
trained), evaluator-guided intervention, KL-regularized re-forward.
**It breaks at:** (i) PPLM's evaluator is *external* (a label-trained
attribute classifier), SCBI's claimed E is internal self-consistency;
(ii) PPLM's search object is the hidden-state *trajectory*, SCBI's is a
*basis*; (iii) PPLM uses gradient steps with backward passes, SCBI proposes
generate–evaluate–select. **Attack:** the "per-instance inference-time
representation intervention of a frozen model guided by an evaluator" slot
is occupied since 2020. SCBI's remaining distinction (internal evaluator +
basis-valued object) is a [CONJECTURE] with zero empirical support —
and EXP068 is the first test, not a result.

**∇-Reasoner (Wang et al., ICLR 2026; arXiv:2603.04948) — the output-side
occupant.** [FACT — verified by this dispatch via public web search]
Test-time gradient descent on token *logits* (Differentiable Textual
Optimization): dual to KL-regularized RL, per-sample; 80.4% on MATH-500
(Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, up from 71.2% greedy); matches GRPO at 10–40% fewer
model calls than Best-of-N. **Equivalence holds at:** frozen model,
inference-time, iterative refinement of the model's own output
representations, no weight updates. **It breaks at:** gradient-based (needs
backward passes + a reward model) vs SCBI's proposed gradient-free
self-consistency; logit-space vs basis-space. **Attack:** the program's own
evidence says causal power lives output-side (§1, R2/A2 + EXP078) — and
that room already has a 2026 occupant that *works*, with better
compute-efficiency than the sampling baselines. Any SCBI output-side loop
must beat ∇-Reasoner at matched compute (standing law) — and must explain
why gradient-free self-consistency would beat gradient-based optimization
with a reward model. The burden is now on SCBI, not the field.

**Self-Refine (Madaan et al., NeurIPS 2023; arXiv:2303.17651) — the
internal-evaluator prior.** [FACT] Equivalence holds at: frozen model,
generate → self-critique → refine loop at inference, no training, internal
evaluator. **It breaks at:** search object is text y, not bases B;
refinement, not selection. **Attack (the sharp one):** Self-Refine's
documented limitations — gains plateau after 2–3 iterations, self-critique
has blind spots — are a direct falsifier-risk for SCBI's unvalidated E.
An internal evaluator that cannot see its own errors cannot select better
bases. Until EXP068's ρ-gate (or equivalent) validates E *with transfer*,
the dynamic loop's hinge is exactly the component the literature says is
weak. This attack targets H1/H2's load-bearing assumption, not a
strawman.

**Tree of Thoughts (Yao et al., NeurIPS 2023; arXiv:2305.10601) and
Self-Consistency (Wang et al., ICLR 2023; arXiv:2203.11171).** [FACT]
ToT's generate → self-evaluate → select/backtrack over thoughts is
algorithmically isomorphic to the proposed G/E/S over bases; the only
change is the search object (y → B). **Attack:** domain shift alone does
not clear N1. The loop is a known combination; the "self-consistent" in
SCBI's name borrows Wang et al.'s term while the mechanism (frozen
counterfactual evaluation) differs from agreement-marginalization —
terminological proximity without mechanistic identity, which the
synthesis must not trade on.

**Activation-LQR (arXiv:2604.19018, ~Apr 2026; "Local Linearity of LLMs
Enables Activation Steering via Model-Based Linear Optimal Control") —
the closed-loop occupant.** [FACT — verified by this dispatch via public
web search] Layer-wise Jacobians linearize transformer dynamics (LTV);
LQR feedback controllers drive activations toward semantic setpoints in
*closed loop*, with formal tracking-error bounds, no fine-tuning, no
parameter updates. **Equivalence holds at:** frozen model, dynamic
(closed-loop, feedback) activation control at inference, Δθ=0.
**It breaks at:** setpoints are externally specified (feature direction
v_k given), not per-instance invented; control-theoretic, not
self-evaluative. **Attack:** this is the field's existence proof that
*dynamic closed-loop representation control of a frozen model works with
guarantees* — it occupies H1's slot ("dynamic representation control")
with stronger validation than anything in SCBI. Two consequences:
(a) it validates the adaptive-loop *bet* (closed-loop > open-loop
steering — consistent with the Sept-2026 sweep note); (b) it sets the bar:
SCBI's loop must beat or clearly differ from A-LQR at matched compute, or
its H1 candidacy is "closed-loop control, but open-loop-evaluated and
unbounded" — strictly weaker than the art.

**STARS (Yang et al.; arXiv:2605.26733 — "STAbility-driven Recurrent
Scaling" for Looped LMs).** [FACT — verified by this dispatch] A
*training* framework (Jacobian Spectral Radius Regularization + random
loop sampling) that stabilizes test-time latent recurrence; documents the
"gain-then-collapse" pathology of naive looped inference.
**Equivalence: none** — it trains (violates Δθ=0), so it is not an
equivalence threat but a *constraint*: it predicts that frozen iterative
latent loops degrade without stabilization. **Falsifiable prediction for
EXP068-class loops:** non-monotonic gains with iteration depth unless a
stabilizer is present. If the program ever iterates a loop, this is the
prior it must answer.

**DEER (Yang et al., 2025; arXiv:2504.15895 — Dynamic Early Exit in
Reasoning Models).** [FACT — verified by this dispatch] Training-free,
confidence-gated early exit at reasoning switch points; 19–80% shorter
CoTs, +0.3–5.0% accuracy, 11 models / 10 benchmarks. **Equivalence:
none** — it allocates compute (exit/stop), it does not construct or select
representations. **Relevance:** it is L2 evidence ("changes the
computational strategy" — per-instance compute allocation) achieved by the
field *without* any representation construction. It is therefore a forced
baseline with teeth: any SCBI claim to L2 must show a strategy change that
DEER-style routing cannot already deliver at matched compute.

**Debate / MoA (Irving et al., 2018; Wang et al., 2024).**
**Equivalence: none** — pure output-space multi-call methods, no
representation intervention. **Relevance:** standing-law forced baselines.
SCBI's claimed advantage is *internal* computation (fewer tokens, latent);
that advantage is unmeasured until a matched-compute comparison exists.

**Steering Vector Fields (Feb 2026, per Sept-2026 field sweep note).**
UNVERIFIED by this dispatch (no primary source retrieved; cited per the
program's internal sweep note as validating the adaptive-loop bet).
**Constraint:** cluster C / the literature program must verify and place
it before the synthesis finalizes; do not cite its content.

### 2.3 Net assessment for §B

[INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] The tested static mechanism has no novelty
defense (N0, §2.1). The claimed dynamic loop's *structure* (generate →
evaluate → select at inference, frozen model) is a known combination
(Family C + PPLM); its *distinguishing components* (internal
self-consistency evaluator over per-instance bases) are conjectures under
their first test (EXP068). Meanwhile the field has moved: output-side
dynamic methods (∇-Reasoner), closed-loop control with guarantees
(Activation-LQR), and training-free adaptive routing (DEER) each occupy
part of the space SCBI aspires to — all with stronger validation. The
strongest honest statement for §B: **SCBI's tested mechanism is
prior art; its claimed mechanism is a conjecture in a field that has
meanwhile produced working instances of the adjacent possible.** The
novelty boundary (§3) is therefore narrow and precisely locatable.

## 3. Prior-art map + novelty boundary (tracks 4 + 8) — input to synthesis §D

### 3.1 Map (families; SCBI's position marked)

- **Family A — diff-in-means steering (static):** ActAdd, CAA, RepE-control,
  ITI, Arditi refusal-direction, Subramani latent vectors, Jorgensen
  mean-centring. *SCBI-tested lives here (N0).*
- **Family B — linear cross-space alignment:** Mikolov (2013), Smith
  (2017), model stitching, DAS. *SCBI Stage-A mapping lived here
  (defective instance).*
- **Family C — inference-time search + self-evaluation:** self-consistency,
  ToT, Self-Refine, LLM-Monkeys, rStar-Math (MCTS+PRM), PPLM
  (gradient-guided hidden-state search, external evaluator). *SCBI-claimed
  loop lives here (conjectured sub-slot: internal evaluator × basis-valued
  object).*
- **Family D — output-side dynamic optimization (frozen):** ∇-Reasoner
  (test-time gradient descent on logits, reward-guided). *Adjacent;
  occupied 2026.*
- **Family E — closed-loop representation control (frozen):**
  Activation-LQR (LTV linearization + LQR feedback, formal bounds).
  *Adjacent; occupied 2026.*
- **Family F — adaptive compute routing (frozen, training-free):** DEER
  (confidence-gated early exit). *Forced baseline; L2 already demonstrated
  by the field.*
- **Family G — frozen-backbone representation editing with learning:**
  ReFT/LoReFT, DAS. *Excluded from core SCBI by Law #6 (learned
  parameters), shares the premise.*
- **Family H — test-time weight adaptation:** TTT and descendants.
  *Excluded by the Δθ=0 boundary.*

[INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] No SCBI component sits outside known families.
The only unoccupied sub-slot is **C′: per-instance basis-valued search
with an internal self-consistency evaluator, transfer-validated, Δθ=0.**

### 3.2 Exactly where the novelty boundary would have to be

A result crosses from N1 to a genuine novelty claim **iff** it
simultaneously demonstrates, pre-registered:

1. **Per-instance construction** — the applied intervention varies
   non-trivially across instances (falsifier: if selected bases collapse
   to a near-constant direction across instances, it is steering with
   extra steps — Family A, not C′).
2. **Transfer-validated internal evaluator** — E's scores track rescue on a
   *held-out distribution* (vocabulary/task), not just the gate
   distribution (the EXP068 §4b transfer-probe lesson: E-validity that
   doesn't transfer is an instrument verdict, not a loop verdict).
3. **Matched-compute superiority over Family C/D/E/F baselines** — beats
   ToT-style search, best-of-N, ∇-Reasoner, A-LQR-style closed-loop, DEER,
   debate/MoA at equal forward-pass budget (standing law; no exceptions).
4. **Conditional computation (the track-3 bar, §4)** — the loop's gain
   depends on intermediate computed quantities within the episode:
   randomizing E's measurements (or the feedback signal) must kill the
   gain. Open-loop selection from precomputed candidates does not cross.
5. **Specificity** — the selected intervention passes a specificity-gap
   test against matched structural controls (the EXP059 lesson, F6):
   Δ_valid − Δ_control > 0, pre-registered.

[INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] Conditions 1–5 are individually necessary and
jointly sufficient for the boundary crossing. Note condition 4 is the
P1 mechanism-level bar operationalized: it is what makes the computation
*new* rather than *more*.

### 3.3 The experiment that would cross it

EXP068 (signed) tests conditions 1–3 partially (ρ-gate + §4b transfer
probe + kill criteria F1–F5) but does **not** test condition 4
(conditionality) or 5 (specificity). [HYPOTHESIS/PROPOSITION] The
boundary-crossing experiment is therefore **EXP068 + a conditionality
probe**: after the loop runs, re-run with E's per-instance measurements
replaced by randomly permuted measurements (same marginal distribution,
broken instance-linkage). Pre-registered decision rule: if ΔM_loop −
ΔM_permuted-E ≤ 0 (McNemar, pre-registered α), the loop's gain does not
depend on its computed quantities — verdict **Not supported** for L2
(it is open-loop selection); the program's L2 bet moves to the output-side
room (§6 idea) or stands down. If the gain survives only with true E and
beats the Family C/D/E/F battery at matched compute, with a positive
specificity gap — the boundary is crossed. Either outcome is first-class
science; the probe is cheap (a re-run arm, no new bundle).

## 4. Neuroscience input (track 3)

Charter duty: analogies for temporary computational structures, never
forced; every analogy [INTERPRETATION] or [SPECULATION] until a falsifiable
prediction holds; name the biological function an inference-time structure
must implement to count as more than steering, and the experiment that
would show it.

### 4.1 The one load-bearing analogy: working memory

[INTERPRETATION] The strongest biological precedent for Δθ=0 temporary
computation is **working memory**: prefrontal persistent activity
maintains and manipulates task-relevant representations with no synaptic
change. This is an existence proof that temporary computational structures
can be cognitively powerful — it is, in fact, the substrate of fluid
intelligence. It therefore *licenses the aspiration* behind H2 (ephemeral
computational workspace) while setting its bar: a working-memory-like
structure must support (a) maintenance over delays with interference and
decay, (b) manipulation (not just storage), (c) capacity limits, and
(d) content-addressability. SCBI's tested structures exhibit **none** of
(a)–(d). [INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] Verdict on "SCBI's tested structures
are working-memory-like": **Not supported**. The analogy constrains H2:
any H2 candidate must pre-register which of (a)–(d) it implements and the
measurement for each — otherwise "ephemeral workspace" is a metaphor, not
a mechanism.

### 4.2 Executive control / biased competition — and what EXP077 already confirmed

[INTERPRETATION] Top-down executive control (biased-competition model)
modulates downstream processing *conditionally* — gated by task context —
without rewiring. Structurally this resembles additive steering, but the
biological version is **conditional and routed**, never an unconditional
global bias. EXP077's licensed kill (unconditional 30° cone, α=1 offset
null; gated/conditional variants survive) is exactly what this analogy
predicts: unconditional bias is not how control works. [SPECULATION] The
surviving gated/conditional variants are the biologically plausible
direction — but plausibility is not evidence; they remain untested.

### 4.3 What is NOT analogous (warnings against forced mapping)

- **Hippocampal replay** [INTERPRETATION]: replay itself is activity
  without weight change, but its *function* is consolidation — eventual
  weight change. Under strict Δθ=0 it is not a precedent; do not cite it
  as one.
- **Synaptic tagging / plasticity** [INTERPRETATION]: explicitly weight
  change; excluded by Law #6. Mentioned only to block the analogy.
- **Hierarchical prefrontal control** [SPECULATION]: rostral–caudal
  abstraction gradients are a tempting template for hierarchical basis
  construction (abstract basis constrains concrete intervention), but
  there is no SCBI evidence and no pre-registered test. Held as
  [CONJECTURE/OPEN], not support.

### 4.4 The biological function that would count as "more than steering"

[INFERENCE/PROPOSITION] (track 3 duty, stated as a falsifiable bar):
an inference-time structure counts as more than steering iff it implements
**conditional routing contingent on intermediate computed results** —
i.e., the intervention applied at computational stage t+1 is a non-trivial
function f of a quantity *measured* at stage t within the same inference
episode (feedback), and f is load-bearing. **The experiment:** run the loop
twice — once with true intermediate measurements, once with the
measurements randomly permuted across instances (same marginals, broken
linkage). Pre-registered rule: if the gain survives permuted feedback,
the structure is open-loop bias (steering, Family A/C) — verdict
**Not supported** for "more than steering." If the gain requires true
feedback, it implements conditional computation — the minimal mechanism
that is *not* steering. This is the same conditionality probe as §3.3,
now grounded: biology's vote is that *gating by computed state* is the
difference between control and bias.

## 5. Discovery criterion (track 12) — constrains roadmap Stage 3

Charter duty: define "discovery" operationally *before* any experiment
claims it. All four conditions are necessary; all concern **L3**
(qualitatively new capability) — no silent crossing from L1.

**D1 — Novelty-to-model (behavioral).** The artifact A (procedure,
algorithm, hypothesis, structure) is not in the frozen model's elicitable
repertoire without the discovery procedure P. Operational test:
*elicitation control* — the same frozen model, given the task directly at
matched compute (direct prompting, best-of-N, debate/MoA), fails to
produce A at comparable quality/rate. If a matched-compute baseline
elicits A, the credit belongs to sampling, not discovery. (Anti-
"stochastic parrot with a verifier" clause.)

**D2 — Independent oracle.** A's correctness/value is verified by a
process causally independent of the model's own judgment: a formal proof
checker, execution against held-out tests, a physical experiment, or a
different model family whose validity is independently established. The
model's self-evaluation can never be the oracle (Self-Refine blind-spot
lesson; Brown et al. verifier ceiling). If the oracle is *used during*
discovery (as PPLM's attribute classifier is), D1's elicitation control
must grant baselines the same oracle — otherwise the discovery is
attributable to the oracle, not the model.

**D3 — Outside the training manifold.** A is not memorized retrieval.
Pre-registered proxies (at least one): (a) *temporal novelty* — A
concerns facts/procedures established after the documented training
cutoff; (b) *combinatorial novelty* — A's joint element-combination is
absent from training (corpus check required); (c) *canary ablation* —
P run on a model trained with A-adjacent data removed still finds A
(reserved for high-stakes claims). Without one, the claim is "novel
combination," not discovery.

**D4 — Capability bite.** A enables decisions/actions the model could not
otherwise take — measured as new task success under D1's elicitation
control, not as score gains on already-solved tasks (L1 ≠ L3).

**Credit assignment.** Discovery credit accrues to the *model* only to the
extent P is task-general. If P is human-designed scaffolding specific to
the discovery, the discovery belongs to the human+model system. Every
discovery claim must report P's scaffolding-specificity alongside D1–D4.

[INFERENCE/INTERPRETATION] **Stage-3 gate:** no "discovery" claim may
advance without D1–D4 pre-registered and satisfied. Current program
status: no SCBI result satisfies even D1 — nothing has been shown to lie
outside the model's elicitable repertoire. Stage 3 is therefore
*ungated aspiration*, not a plan, until an L2 mechanism exists.

### CHALLENGE (track 8) — selection-bias audit of "AUTONOMOUS SCBI CONFIRMED"

**Target (live corpus claim):** `reports/experiment_report.md`
[THEOREM/OBSERVATION] — "EXP023 demonstrates autonomous SCBI on a fresh
unseen BENCH-002-NL split: frozen counterfactual candidate selection
followed by contrastive intervention produced statistically significant
Top-1 headroom (b=14, c=1, p=0.000488, CI [+0.0400,+0.0950])"; "H_mech is

**Hidden assumption:** the development audit (N=20) that froze the full
configuration (G4, E_CF, O5, L=8, r=2, α=0.25) is treated as valid model
selection, and the Seed-84 p-value is reported *without selection
adjustment*. With a configuration grid searched on N=20, the winner's
curse applies: the selected config is the maximum over the grid, and its
confirmatory p-value is optimistic by the selection multiplicity — which
the corpus never quantifies (Laws #9, #13).

**Falsification (cheapest):** enumerate the dev-audit config grid from
archived logs; compute the selection-adjusted p-value (Bonferroni over
the grid actually searched, or max-T permutation). **Kill criterion:** if
selection-adjusted p ≥ 0.05, "CONFIRMED" is revoked — the result is
downgraded to *exploratory*, and any L1 claim built on it requires a fresh
confirmatory replication with the config frozen before data contact. If
adjusted p < 0.05, the claim survives this challenge. If the grid size is
not archived, the challenge still bites: unquantified selection
multiplicity means the confirmatory status is unsupported by its own
documentation.
**Cost:** $0 GPU — pure re-analysis of archived logs.
**Evidentiary level:** L1. **Verdict sought:** Supported (survives
adjustment) / Not supported (revoked to exploratory).

### IDEA (tracks 4 + 8 + 3) — Dynamic per-instance readout-space search (DPRS)

**Question the corpus suggests but never tested:** every causal rescue in
this program is output-side (R2/A2: bridges +10–16.67pp; EXP078: bridge
energy outside the concept subspace; G1: QK story dead; five dead geometry
rooms). Yet all output-side work is *static, label-informed* bridges —
existence proofs, not mechanisms. The corpus never tested a **dynamic
per-instance loop in readout space**. EXP068 tests the loop on the
geometry side; the evidence says the geometry side is where causality
*isn't*.

**Mechanism (mathematics, not metaphor):** per instance x, let C(x) be the
model's own top-k confusion set from frozen logits (label-free).
- **G:** candidate readout perturbations {δ_j}: sparse logit biases on
  top-2 confusion pairs (±β on ordered pairs), |{δ_j}| ≤ 8, pre-registered.
- **E:** frozen self-consistency over perturbed rollouts evaluated on a
  held-out paraphrase x′ of x (paraphrase from the frozen model; E never
  sees the test decision).
- **S:** δ* = argmax E. **T:** apply δ* to x. Δθ=0 throughout.
The loop's decisions are conditional on per-instance computed quantities
(the §4 bar): E-scores measured within the episode.

**Why it matters:** it is the only room the negatives leave open; it
moves the Family-C loop to the space where causality was actually
observed; it directly tests whether inference-time computation can be
*more than steering* via the conditionality criterion (§4.4).

**Cheapest falsifying experiment:** archived EXP077 bench (N=60,
Pythia-410m/L20). Primary endpoint: McNemar ΔM vs baseline; key
comparison: DPRS ΔM vs the static bridge ΔM at matched forward-pass
budget, plus a matched-compute best-of-K random-perturbation control.
**Kill criterion (pre-registered):** if DPRS ΔM ≤ 0 (p ≥ 0.05), **or** if
DPRS does not beat the random-perturbation control, the idea is dead —
the output-side room contains only label-informed existence proofs, not a
discoverable dynamic mechanism. **Free-tier cost:** ~60 × 8 × 2 forward
passes on Pythia-410m — minutes on 2×T4.
**Novelty audit:** N1 — Family-C loop in readout space; adjacent art:
∇-Reasoner (gradient-based logit refinement, external reward model —
DPRS differs: gradient-free, internal evaluator, discrete candidates),
PPLM (per-instance hidden-state updates), Self-Refine (output loop).
Cited with evidence; no N2 claim.
**H1–H5 relevance:** tests H1 (dynamic representation control) in its
last surviving space; a positive result is **L2** evidence (new strategy
for allocating inference computation); L3 requires D1–D4 (§5).
**Non-duplication check:** EXP068's signed loop is geometry-side with an
E-validity gate; DPRS is readout-side with a conditionality probe —
distinct search spaces, distinct falsifiers (verified against
`reports/adversarial_review_exp068_prereg_2026-09-23.md` §0–1).
