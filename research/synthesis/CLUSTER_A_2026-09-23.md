## B. Strongest arguments SCPM is NOT fundamentally novel

*Track 7 attacks; Cluster B maps. Equivalence claims below are
**[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]` pending Cluster B's audit — the synthesis must
not cite them as verified.*

### B1. The static mechanism is operator-equivalent to CAA / Activation Addition

The tested static family is h ← h + α·B_agg, where B_agg is a normalized mean of
normalized contrast-pair directions. Contrastive Activation Addition (Rimsky et al.,
ACL 2024) and Activation Addition (Turner et al., 2023) are the same operator:
additive constant-vector interventions derived from contrast pairs. The program's own
paper draft concedes this (N1 overall, N0 for the tested mechanism) after a
24-record adversarially reviewed prior-art audit. **This is the killing attack**: the
entire EXP059–EXP066 static program is a Known Combination; no methods claim built on
it can survive, and the dynamic-Procrustes conditions cannot rescue it because the
operator was unsound (A2).

### B2. Per-instance Procrustes rotation is test-time coordinate adaptation — a searched cluster, not a new principle

Even a *sound* R(x) would be "fit a coordinate transform from premise embeddings at
test time, apply to a steering direction" — squarely inside the literature README's
search clusters C (coordinate transformations, adaptive bases, subspace adaptation)
and D (hidden-state intervention, latent intervention). The per-instance adaptivity is
SCPM's only candidate formulation distinction over CAA, and it is **[CONJECTURE]** /
unvalidated — it has never produced a discriminating positive. Cluster B to verify
exact priors (candidates: test-time activation optimization literature).

### B3. G/E/S/T is search-based inference + self-consistency with an unsupervised selector

Candidate generation + self-supervised evaluation + selection + injection is the
literature README's cluster E (inference-time search, candidate generation and
selection, adaptive computation) composed with cluster F (self-consistency
decoding). Best-of-K with an unsupervised selector is a Known Combination. The
2026-09-11 ChatGPT-review response pre-registered the exact "no new mechanism" test —
a forward-pass matched sampling baseline beating or matching the loop (falsification
criterion #3) — and the program has *never run it*: EXP070, designed as the first
real ceiling test, returned UNINFORMATIVE_PROBE. A novelty claim for the loop with
zero discriminating evidence is theory preservation, not science.

### B4. Self-Refine maps onto G/E/S/T structurally

Self-Refine (iterative generate→critique→refine with the same frozen model, Δθ=0)
is G/E/S/T with the "basis" in token space and the model itself as evaluator. The
activation-space vs. token-space distinction is a formulation difference of unknown
significance — N1 at best, significance **[OPEN]**. PPLM likewise established
test-time activation steering with Δθ=0 (via an external attribute model +
backprop); SCPM's "no gradients, no external model" is a design choice, not a new
principle, until an experiment shows the choice buys a capability PPLM lacks.

### B5. The dissociation "discovery" was predicted by prior art

The paper draft's own prior-art statement: the steering literature predicts geometric
alignability without causal transfer (Tan et al., NeurIPS 2024; Braun et al., 2025);
the contribution is a negative result in a specific controlled form with the
operator-unsoundness confound surgically removed. Negative results are first-class
science — and they are not novelty. The "probes ≠ causal interchangeability" critique
is standard in mechanistic interpretability since 2023.

### B6. The field has moved past open-loop additive steering

The September-2026 field sweep (per diary/sprint): Steering Vector Fields (Feb 2026)
and Activation-LQR (Apr 2026) move steering to vector fields and closed-loop feedback
control with tracking-error bounds; static CAA is the field's *ablation baseline*.
A program testing CAA-equivalent mechanisms in late 2026 is behind the frontier. The
sweep's direction-changing read — that our G/E/S/T is open-loop search with no error
bounds and A-LQR may be the formulation the loop should have been — is the strongest
external evidence that SCPM's mechanism family is not where the frontier is.

### B7. No capability claim exists at all, let alone a novel-mechanism claim

Every positive number in the corpus is level-1, and none beat the forced baselines
(debate/MoA, STARS, DEER, ∇-Reasoner, bigger frozen model at matched compute) —
because those comparisons were never run. A capability result without them is a
measurement, not a claim (standing law). There is therefore no *capability* for a
novelty claim to attach to.

### Track-7 verdict on "SCPM is fundamentally novel"

- Static family (EXP059–066 + EXP077 static rooms): **Refuted** as novel (operator
  equivalence B1 + killed mechanisms A4 + five dead rooms A5).
- Per-instance G/E/S/T loop formulation: **Inconclusive** (never discriminatingly
  tested; E-validity [CONJECTURE]).
- Per P2/P5, the synthesis is licensed to conclude SCPM is a dead end as a mechanism
  program. The one live thread — the loop's formulation distinction — survives only as
  an untested [HYPOTHESIS], and Cluster B's audit may yet find its exact prior.

**Where the attack breaks (honesty):** if E-validity were demonstrated — a label-free
evaluator selecting activation-space candidates above chance-matched baselines at
matched compute — the loop would be an N2-candidate formulation distinction
(self-consistency selects over *outputs*; the loop would select over *activation
operators* — a real structural difference). That experiment has never been run.

*What the negatives leave open. Highest licensed claim: level-1 with paths to
level-2 tests. No level-3 evidence anywhere — stated explicitly.*

### C1. The output-side room is real, unfalsified, and a different direction family

Four independent bridge rescues, always c=0 (A3); EXP078 localization puts the causal
power outside the concept subspace (A7); five experiments converge on "the
transferable direction lives output-side." **[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: the
negatives killed *concept-geometry steering*, not *output-coupled steering*. What is
genuinely new here is not the bridge (logit steering is old) but the *program the
corpus has not run*: inventing output-coupled directions **without label access**
(C-A donor transfer, pending the LOG-144 entity-similarity-leak review). If donor
geometry rescues held-out items under strict Law #7 compliance, the mechanism is
"cross-item donor transfer" — not steering, not CAA. Level: [HYPOTHESIS]; the
discriminating experiment is designed but unrun.

### C2. The G1 per-head table is a new instrument, not just a null result

**[FACT]** / `[OBSERVATION]`: 48 downstream heads measured weight-only, zero forward
passes — B_agg beats the bridge on QK energy in 35/48 heads; cross-head correlation
0.27 (the two directions are visible to *different* heads, not scaled versions of one
profile). **[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: this is the program's first
*measurement-pruned* search-space reduction derived from the model's own weights
rather than from geometry hypotheses. It enables a search the static program never
tried: restrict the loop's candidates to head-subspaces measured to carry the
direction. Caveat (honest): G1 weakened the original premise — the bridge is
mid-null on QK — so the pruning hypothesis must be restated around readout-coupled
(OV/unembedding) heads, not QK-visible heads. Level: method (level-1 enabler).

### C3. The boundary I1 is a diagnostic instrument

**[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: the dissociation is not just a result but a
*test* — any future mechanism can be run through "raw geometric similarity vs. causal
transfer" to separate geometric structure from causal structure. The program's most
defensible asset is a measurement tool. A successor mechanism that *passes* the
boundary test (high similarity AND causal transfer under a sound operator) would be,
by construction, doing something no tested mechanism does.

### C4. Feedback control reframes the loop as a different kind of computation

**[HYPOTHESIS]**: Activation-LQR (Apr 2026, per the field sweep) shows layer-wise
transformer dynamics are locally linear and controllable in closed loop with
setpoints and tracking-error bounds. Reframing G/E/S/T from open-loop candidate
search to closed-loop feedback control changes the *kind* of computation from
feedforward search to feedback regulation — this is where level-2 evidence (changes
the computational strategy) could first appear. The discriminating question is
mechanism-level, not benchmark-level: does closed-loop beat matched-compute open-loop
best-of-K? **[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: this is the most credible path in the
corpus from level 1 to level 2, because the difference (feedback vs. feedforward) is
architectural, not incremental.

### C5. The bottleneck is the verifier, not the generator — and no verifier has been built

**[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: EXP070's design, the oracle-vs-autonomous gap,
and the ChatGPT-review falsification criteria all point at *selection* as the unsolved
piece; candidate generation is cheap. A frozen-model verifier that counterfactually
tests candidate interventions without labels (verifier/controller separation, from
the mentor's alternative list) is a genuinely open architectural question — the
corpus contains no such component (E-validity [CONJECTURE]). The minimal-new-mechanism
answer to the spec's mandatory question: **"SCPM + a working verifier" is a different
kind of inference system than "SCPM + a better direction."** Level: [HYPOTHESIS].

### C6. The static program's comprehensiveness is a map, not just a graveyard

Five dead rooms under narrow, honest licenses (A5) + the G1 kill (A4) + the
Procrustes unsoundness proof (A2) jointly say *where the mechanism isn't* with unusual
precision for a young program. **[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: a successor that
avoids all five rooms (no unconditional static geometry, no QK-subspace operators, no
unsound cross-space fits) starts from a constrained design space — which is exactly
what makes C1, C4, C5 worth pursuing rather than random. Gated/conditional variants
survived EXP077's narrow kill; multi-layer coordination is untested.

## E-input — candidate architectures (foundations view; for the inventor cluster)

*Sketches only — Cluster C owns full development and the H1–H5 boxes. Each carries a
compressed support/kill pair; the synthesizer must expand them per spec §5. None is
assumed correct.*

**E1. Closed-loop activation controller (A-LQR lineage).** Mechanism: at inference
time compute the target layer's Jacobian, define a semantic setpoint (bridge
direction or correct-answer mean activation), derive LQR feedback gains, inject
closed-loop corrections across generation steps — feedback, not open-loop search.
Foundations rationale: first formulation available to this program with tracking-error
bounds; changes the computation kind (feedforward→feedback). *Support:* closed-loop
beats matched-FLOP open-loop best-of-K on decision-change endpoints. *Kill:* no gain
at matched compute, or Jacobian cost dominates the budget.

**E2. Donor-bridge transfer (C-A lineage).** Mechanism: construct steering directions
from *other items'* unembedding geometry (donor items), restricted to the
output-coupled direction family; zero target-option information at eval time.
Rationale: EXP078 — causal power lives output-side. *Support:* donor bridges rescue
held-out items with c≈0 while the entity-similarity-leak control does not.
*Kill:* donor ΔM ≤ 0 while self-bridge rescues, or the effect collapses to the
similarity control (then it is logit steering, not transfer).

**E3. Measurement-pruned head-targeted search (J-lens lineage).** Mechanism: use
G1's weight-only per-head table to restrict loop candidates to
span{top-k readout-coupled head projections of the bridge}; search only
measurement-pruned subspaces. Rationale: blind cone search died (EXP077); this is
search-space reduction from the model's own weights. Honest caveat: restate around
readout-coupled heads — G1 refuted the QK-visibility premise. *Support:*
head-restricted search beats full-space search at matched candidate budget.
*Kill:* no gain over the static full bridge (ΔM ≤ 0, McNemar p ≥ 0.05).

**E4. Verifier/controller separation.** Mechanism: two-role frozen architecture —
generator proposes candidate interventions; a separate verifier role counterfactually
tests each (e.g., margin shift on a held-out view of the item) and accepts/rejects.
Rationale: selection is the evidenced bottleneck (C5); no verifier exists in the
corpus. *Support:* verifier-selected candidates beat random selection at matched
forward-pass budget (the 2026-09-11 falsification criterion #1/#2, already
pre-registered). *Kill:* verifier selection ≈ random selection.

**E5. Ephemeral workspace with counterfactual revision (H2-flavored).** Mechanism:
recurrent inference maintaining temporary workspace state C_t across passes — write
a hypothesis, test it counterfactually with the model's own forward passes, revise
or purge. Δθ=0; only (B_t, z_t, C_t, M_t) evolve. Rationale: the canonical
objective's state tuple is entirely unexplored by current SCPM. *Support:*
workspace-equipped inference solves hidden-structure tasks where single-pass +
fixed steering stay at chance. *Kill:* no gain over matched-compute best-of-K on
the same tasks.

**E6. Head-routing program synthesis (H3/H4-flavored).** Mechanism: per-item
*routing programs* — which attention heads to amplify/suppress — treating heads as
modules (G1's cross-head correlation 0.27 suggests head-level modularity), instead
of additive directions. Rationale: all additive-direction rooms are dead; routing
is the untested degree of freedom. *Support:* per-item routing masks beat the best
additive baseline at matched compute. *Kill:* routing masks ≈ random masks.

*Rough H1–H5 mapping for the synthesizer: E1→H1 (dynamic representation control),
E5→H2 (ephemeral workspace), E6→H3/H4 (program synthesis / adaptive routing),
E2→H5-candidate (donor transfer), E3/E4 are enabling mechanisms.*

## I-input — operational definition of "superintelligent augmentation" (track 5)

*Proposal for synthesis §I. Mechanism-level, falsifiable, no hype. "Superintelligent"
is a measurement protocol below, not an adjective.*

**Definition.** A frozen model M (Δθ=0, SHA-256-verified pre/post) is
*superintelligently augmented* by an inference-time procedure P iff all of the
following hold on a pre-registered task family T:

1. **Capability delta at matched compute.** M+P exceeds every forced baseline at ≤
   matched inference FLOPs — unaugmented M, debate/MoA, STARS, DEER, ∇-Reasoner, a
   bigger frozen model — on decision-change endpoints with pre-registered statistics.
2. **Strategy change (level 2).** P's internal compute allocation depends on the
   input: per-item compute spend correlates with item difficulty/uncertainty at
   fixed total budget; ablating the adaptivity (fixed-budget P) destroys the gain.
3. **New capability (level 3).** There exists a task family T_new (hidden-structure /
   novel-task; track 9's duty: negative control + forced baselines + stated level) on
   which M and all forced baselines perform at chance while M+P performs
   significantly above chance, **and** the capability transfers to a second task
   family not used in P's development (Stage-2 gate).
4. **Mechanism ablation.** Removing P's claimed novel component (verifier, controller,
   workspace) at matched compute destroys the capability. No ablation, no mechanism
   claim.
5. **No label leakage.** P's construction uses no test labels, target answers, or
   candidate-option tokens (Law #7); verified by construction audit.

**Kill criterion.** If M+P's only demonstrations are score gains on benchmarks where
a forced baseline at matched compute matches or exceeds it, "superintelligent
augmentation" is **Refuted** for P; the claim downgrades to level 1 ("can improve
inference") or to a measurement.

**Grades (bound to the three evidentiary levels):** (a) *inference improvement*
(level 1 — score gain at matched compute); (b) *strategy change* (level 2 —
adaptive compute allocation verified); (c) *new capability* (level 3 —
chance-to-above-chance on hidden-structure tasks with transfer). The term
"superintelligent augmentation" is reserved for (c); (a) and (b) are named as what
they are.

**[INFERENCE]** / `[INTERPRETATION]`: this definition rules out the entire current
corpus — the bridge rescues are level-1 at best (option-informed construction fails
condition 5 for autonomous claims; forced baselines never run fails condition 1 as a
claim). That is the definition working as intended: it is a bar, not a celebration.
