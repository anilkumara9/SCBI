**Candidate:** EXP107 — template-decorrelated follow-up to EXP106 (H3/LFH)

**Lane:** LOG-4535 (drafting only)
**Status:** DRAFT — unsigned. Independent Law #14 review is a **separate lane** and is **not** this lane's work. The EXP107 designation is reserved in this draft; formal minting of the experiment number is the CEO launch decision's act (founder-authorized lanes only). No execution.

**Parent result under test:** EXP106 scored **60/60** (p = 8.67×10⁻¹⁹) → registered CONTINUE, BUT the executor's adversarial dissection (LOG-4534; `research/experiments/EXP106_LFH_REPORT_2026-09-29.md` §11) proved the "transfer" is fully explained by **surface-template detection**: the phrasing label is deterministically generated by the template ("outranks" vs "is lower than"), and on 31/60 test items where the train entity-prior disagreed with the template, the head followed the template on all 31. The hypothesis's intended reading — "the output-side room has an autonomous (non-artifact) instantiation" — is NOT supported.

## 1. Hypothesis (one falsifiable sentence) [HYPOTHESIS]

A logistic head (L2, C=1.0) trained on the template-correlated EXP092-B bench — the head that scored 60/60 on the template-correlated EXP091 bench in EXP106 — retains **≥ 40/60** accuracy on a template-decorrelated 60-item bench (four balanced 15-item cells crossing phrasing × template word-family), with byte-identical block-off — i.e., the EXP106 transfer is a target-position readout surviving meaning-preserving rephrasing, not a surface-template artifact.

**Falsifier:** ≤ 35/60 on the decorrelated bench.

## 2. Exact question [PROPOSITION]

Does the EXP106 60/60 survive template decorrelation — is it a target-position readout, or pure template detection?

**Decision changed:** **CONTINUE** (genuine position-readout signal; the GPU head-training proposal licensed by EXP106's CONTINUE stands, now with template-control evidence — the proposal itself must still be template-controlled and remains founder-gated) vs **KILL the EXP106 transfer reading** (the 60/60 was pure template artifact; the GPU proposal licensed by EXP106's CONTINUE is VOID; any new GPU proposal must be re-licensed against a template-decorrelated design).

**Primary metric (registered before any run):** accuracy of the retrained head on the 60-item decorrelated bench. One-sided binomial test vs chance (p=0.5).

**Worth-it gate:** exact question above; decision changed above; cheapest test = 60 fresh CPU extraction passes + one logistic fit (~30 min, $0); mathematical license = linear-probe theory (a linear head extracts only what is in the embedding — decorrelation removes the word-template cue, so surviving accuracy must come from a non-template feature) + binomial bars; quantitative prediction = pure template artifact ⇒ ≈30/60 on balanced 4×15 cells (point prediction; ±5 of embedding-shift noise absorbed by the ≤35/60 KILL bar), genuine position readout ⇒ ~60/60; breaking point = the 36–39/60 HELD zone.

## 3. Method

### 3.1 Frozen backbone [ASSUMPTION / Law #6]

- Backbone weights are **frozen**: Δθ = 0. Backbone parameter hash recorded pre-extraction, post-extraction, and final; expected pin `ec276abe3902fab0166ce56c00de84c2c737c80af4f9c41a9cbe94e4ec38e0ed` (LOG-331). Any mismatch → RUN-INVALID, quarantine, no metric may be read (Law #6, Law #13).
- The head is an *external, detachable* readout: it never modifies, adapts, or conditions the backbone.

### 3.2 Train set — reuse of the EXP106 train artifacts (no new train extraction)

- Train embeddings: `experiments/runs/EXP106_lfh/out/exp106_train_embeddings.npz` — (60, 1024) float32, ids + phrasing records.
- **Reuse-integrity check:** SHA-256 of the embeddings array must equal `4e6229bea083570f17e3368326d9848c9a8db5dc25c7efc75f0e1a2960b5683c`; SHA-256 of the id array must equal `4668fc45716d46b408c778c93d2d844ad9b2c5bafb33458ec30a4b673ed44474` (verified at drafting, LOG-4535). Any mismatch → RUN-INVALID.
- Train labels: phrasing from the EXP092-B bench rebuilt via `reference_implementation.build_bench()` (bench pin `9be81626…` verified in EXP106). Balance 30 A-first / 30 C-first.
- Head: `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(penalty='l2', C=1.0)`, all other params default — the EXP106 spec verbatim (lbfgs, deterministic on fixed data). No tuning of any kind. Retrain executes in the EXP106 environment (sklearn 1.9.1, `~/.venv-exp077`, torch 2.14.0+cpu) so the sanity gate tests pipeline determinism, not cross-version optimizer drift.
- **Pipeline-continuity sanity gate (pre-primary):** the retrained head must reproduce **60/60** on the original EXP091 bench (rebuilt by the EXP106 executor's `build_test_premises` verbatim). Failure → RUN-INVALID (pipeline drift — the head under test is not the EXP106 head).

### 3.3 Test bench — template-decorrelated (the kill instrument)

- Builder: `experiments/runs/EXP107_templatectrl/build_exp107_bench.py` (LOG-4535). Deterministic; no RNG; no model calls; closed vocabulary (only "outranks" / "is lower than").
- Rephrasing rule (meaning-preserving): reverse the premise sentence order AND swap the verb family, re-anchoring subject/object so the ranking (A > B > C [> D]) and the target's sentence position (first for A-first, last for C-first) are preserved. Full rule table in the builder module docstring.
- Flip selection (deterministic, no RNG): within each phrasing group (30 items sorted by item ID), flip items at even sorted positions → 15 flipped + 15 original per phrasing.
- Bench: 60 items, four balanced 15-item cells — (A-first, original-words), (A-first, flipped-words), (C-first, original-words), (C-first, flipped-words). Item IDs `exp107_ctrl_*` / `exp107_flip_*`, each recording `derived_from` (the EXP091 source id).
- **Registered bench pin:** SHA-256 of the canonical serialization = `e91b302f41f641c74e56957e526efcd4866bac62d134e157defe0a4cdd49e4f5` (verified at drafting, LOG-4535). Mismatch at execution → RUN-INVALID.
- Extraction input: the registered `premise` field; the builder asserts `premise == prompt[:prompt.index(' Question:')]` and `prompt.startswith('Premise:')` for all 60 items — the EXP106 `state_text_of` contract holds byte-identically.
- Test embeddings: fresh 60-pass extraction under the pinned EXP106 spec — frozen Pythia-410m, `hidden_states[20]`, final token `[0, -1, :]`, float32, CPU, `device_map="cpu"`, `model.eval()`, `requires_grad_(False)`, `torch.no_grad()`. One layer, one rule, identical to the EXP106 train procedure.

### 3.4 Disjointness (Law #7)

- **Overlap tripwire:** item-ID intersection between train (`exp092_*`) and test (`exp107_*`) must be **0**. Overlap > 0 → RUN-INVALID. (0 by construction; checked anyway.)
- No test label, target, or metric may touch the training objective. No EXP107 test information may inform any model-selection choice — all choices are pinned above or inherited verbatim from EXP106 (Law #7).
- The builder's label-semantics assertion (target = `tuple[0]` in the first premise sentence ⟺ A-first) is verified at bench-build time and re-verified by the executor.

### 3.5 Block-off byte-identical discipline [FACT — adopted from EXP106]

- **Disable switch:** the trained head is detached, leaving the bare frozen backbone.
- **Byte-identical check:** two independent fresh extraction passes of the decorrelated bench must be bit-for-bit identical (pipeline determinism), and the scored embeddings must be byte-identical to a fresh backbone-only pass. **Any mismatch → INVALID**: the verdict is void — the head is then not a pure additive readout.
- The block-off check must **pass before any verdict is admissible** (a CONTINUE or KILL with a failed block-off is void).

### 3.6 Registered predictions under the rival readings

- **Pure template-artifact reading** (head = "outranks-words → A-first" detector): original cells 30/30 correct, flipped cells 0/30 → **≈30/60** (point prediction; ±5 of embedding-shift noise absorbed by the ≤35/60 KILL bar).
- **Genuine position-readout reading:** ≈ 60/60 (both cells).
- A collapsed score therefore kills the EXP106 transfer reading under *either* a word-template cue *or* a subject-position cue (the flip also moves the target from sentence-subject to sentence-object — registered honest limitation, §7).

## 4. Decision bars (registered)

- **≥ 40/60** → **CONTINUE.** One-sided binomial p ≈ **0.0067** vs chance. With byte-identical block-off passed and the sanity gate passed: genuine position-readout signal surviving template decorrelation. The GPU head-training proposal licensed by EXP106's CONTINUE stands, now with template-control evidence; the proposal itself must still be template-controlled and remains founder-gated (proposal only — no execution licensed).
- **≤ 35/60** → **KILL the EXP106 transfer reading.** The 60/60 was pure template artifact. The GPU proposal licensed by EXP106's CONTINUE is **VOID**; any new GPU proposal must be re-licensed against a template-decorrelated design. No rescue runs.
- **36–39/60 (in-between zone)** → **HELD / INCONCLUSIVE.** Neither CONTINUE nor KILL is licensed. No decision is taken; no re-run, no larger-n rescue (an unlicensed larger-n re-test would be a gate tweak — barred). The observation is recorded as indeterminate only.

## 7. What this does NOT claim

- A CONTINUE is a **genuine position-readout signal**, not superhuman cognition, not consciousness, not generality beyond this bench, and not evidence about internal mechanisms of cognition (the head is a linear extractor — it reports what is present in the embedding, nothing more).
- A KILL kills **only the EXP106 transfer reading** ("autonomous output-side instantiation"). It does not claim the frozen backbone contains no decision-relevant information anywhere, nor that no other bench could show transfer. It does **not** re-litigate EXP106's registered CONTINUE — the EXP106 bars were binding and the verdict stands as registered; this experiment tests the *reading* of that verdict.
- Neither verdict licenses GPU **execution** — a proposal at most, founder-gated, and any proposal must be template-controlled.
- The flipped paraphrases are human-constructed; no claim of natural-distribution coverage. A collapsed score kills the transfer reading under either a word-template or a subject-position cue; the test does **not** identify which surface cue the head used (registered limitation, §3.6).
- Novelty remains **N1 — Known Combination**.
