## 1. Exact question

[HYPOTHESIS] Does the frozen Pythia-410m layer propagator possess a distinguished
maximally-amplified direction v_1 — the leading right singular vector of the
frozen L20→L24 residual-stream Jacobian product — whose transient gain dwarfs
the gain along random directions? Quantified by the gain ratio

    R = σ_max(M) / σ̄(M),

where M = ∂x_{24,last}/∂x_{20,last} is the final-token block (1024×1024) of the
frozen 4-block propagator Jacobian, σ_max(M) its leading singular value
(estimated by randomized power iteration, never materializing M), and σ̄(M) =
sqrt(Tr(MᵀM)/1024) its root-mean-square singular value (Hutchinson estimator).
The decision statistic is the **median R over 10 bench prompts** (per the
2026-09-25 pilot design).

## 3. Cheapest test

**Randomized power iteration + Hutchinson on autograd Jacobians, CPU, ~1h, $0.**
Exact specification:

- **Layer set:** the 4 full transformer blocks L20–L23 (the L20→L24 propagator).
  This is the injection-to-readout span of every prior injection experiment
  (EXP077 injected at L20, read at L24), so v_1 is compared apples-to-apples
  with the dead semantic directions. [ASSUMPTION] recorded: span-placement risk

- **Jacobian:** M = ∂x_{24,last}/∂x_{20,last}, final-token block of the frozen
  propagator, fixed 10 prompts from the verbatim EXP107 bench. Law #7 clean —
  prompts only, no labels anywhere. Other positions enter as constants (the
  standard final-token-map linearization); the cross-position question is
  flagged for the pre-reg.
- **Power iteration:** v^{(k+1)} = MᵀMv^{(k)}/‖MᵀMv^{(k)}‖ via autograd
  JVPs/VJPs only (M never materialized). Convergence: relative change in the
  Rayleigh quotient < 1e-6 over 5 consecutive iterates AND ≥ 20 iterations
  minimum. R is read **only** from converged iterates (Law #9 analog).
  Pilot requires ≥ 8/10 prompts converged; fewer → apparatus-void (§6).
- **Random-direction null:** Hutchinson trace estimator, K=64
  Rademacher/isotropic draws per prompt on the **same M with the same JVP
  budget** → σ̄. Plus a matched-budget direct arm (K=64 random unit vectors,
  median ‖Mv‖) as cross-check against estimator bias — this is also the bridge
  to the downstream injection design (v_1 vs matched-norm random injection).
- **Budget:** ~30–50 JVPs/prompt × 10 prompts ≈ 300–500 JVPs, ~1 CPU-hour, $0.
  No full Jacobian, no labels, no training.

## 4. Mathematical license
