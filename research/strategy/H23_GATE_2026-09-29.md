## The six worth-it items

### 1. Exact question

[OBSERVATION] The trained-head decodability line holds three bound CONTINUEs:
EXP111 (H5) 24/30 with G=0.0000 on bench 1; EXP120 (H15) 25/30 qualified with
G=−0.2000 on bench 2; EXP122 (H16) 27/30 = 0.9000 with G=−0.0667 and
|Pearson|=0.0322 position-decorrelation on bench 3. On all three benches the
target and distractor entities came from **different domain sets per cell**
(bench 3: animals vs fruits), so a head that reads only "which domain set is
on top" produces the same CONTINUE pattern as a true entity-identity readout.

**[HYPOTHESIS]** The trained head's signal is a **true entity-identity readout**,
tested by the pinned H5/H16 head protocol on a newly constructed bench 5 in
which **every premise is single-domain (all animals)** and the label encodes
which entity — the target's entity-subset membership — with the EXP122
construction discipline carried over.

### 2. Decision changed

- **CONTINUE (identity-readout interpretation):** test accuracy **≥ 20/30**
  (one-sided binomial p ≤ 0.0494) **AND** **G < 0.30** → the head extracts
  entity identity within a single domain; the three-bench CONTINUE gains a
  mechanistic foothold — the signal is not exhausted by domain-set detection.
  Strengthens the boundary characterization; licenses nothing downstream
  (no steering, no capability — §Summit below).
- **KILL (identity interpretation):** test accuracy **≤ 18/30** (p ≥ 0.1808)
  **OR** **G ≥ 0.30** → the identity reading is dead. The three
  [OBSERVATION]s **stand** (Law #8 — nothing retroactively un-happens) but
  their CONTINUE is **reclassified as a domain-set-detector correlation**
  under Law #11's two-layer tagging; any entity-level use of the signal is
  retired. This is the finding the design session names as the actionable
  outcome ("kills entity-level use of the signal").
- **HELD:** **19/30** with **G < 0.30** → underpowered zone; no rerun, no
  larger-N rescue (the EXP106/EXP111 no-rescue discipline).

The design session's H23 section registers exactly these bars ("carried from
the H15/H16 discipline"). No PIVOT outcome.

only** (the H16 §3.3 extraction discipline — there is no bench-5 archive, so

random_state=20260929)`; `train_test_split(test_size=0.5, seed 20260929,

Note: the assignment brief's "120 logit forwards" is corrected here — the

### 4. Mathematical license

- EXP122: 27/30 = 0.9000, G=−0.0667, |Pearson|=0.0322 position-decorrelated —
  the bar precedent for the pinned protocol at n=30.
- EXP120: 25/30 (exact one-sided p=0.0001624), G=−0.2000 — qualified CONTINUE.
- EXP111: 24/30 (one-sided p≈0.0014), G=0.0000 — CONTINUE.
- The decoy-structure confound was never separated: benches 1/2/3 all used
  distinct domain sets per cell, so domain-set membership of the target was

### 5. Quantitative prediction and bar calibration

Under **entity-identity readout**: ≥ 20/30 with G < 0.30 — the head decodes
the target's subset membership with the verb-family signature absent.
Under **coarse domain-set detection**: collapse toward ~15/30 — the domain
cue is informationally dead by construction, so a pure domain detector has
nothing to read (≤ 18/30 KILL band, one-sided p ≥ 0.1808).
Under **word-family/template detection**: G ≥ 0.30 (kill-test takes
precedence over any overall score, per the H5/H16 pin).

**Bar-calibration adjudication:** the assignment brief asked whether the
42–57/60 CONTINUE bars (H6's honesty-logit apparatus, EXP112/EXP125) transfer.
They do **not** — wrong apparatus and wrong n. Those bars belong to an n=60
full-bench fidelity metric on the zero-generation logit readout; the head
protocol's signed bars are at **n=30 test items** (stratified 30/30 split of
60 bench items): CONTINUE ≥ 20/30 (p ≤ 0.0494), HELD = 19/30, KILL ≤ 18/30
(p ≥ 0.1808), with G ≥ 0.30 killing regardless of score. H14's bars are
McNemar paired bars — likewise not transferable. The **EXP122 bench-3
precedent (27/30, G=−0.0667)** is the correct bar calibration. No
cross-bench effect-size comparisons are licensed (the H15 lesson).
