## 1. Exact question

Does a planning artifact — the EXP113-cleared T2-P2 planner's choice
between two phrasing variants of the decision prompt, with the plan text
**never entering the prompt** — steer the decision outcome, measured by
paired (per-item) comparison of the EXP112 logit readout on the
plan-chosen phrasing versus a random-chosen phrasing, under a paired
McNemar test on correctness? [HYPOTHESIS]

## 2. Decision changed (pre-registered bars)

McNemar **one-sided** (direction: plan-chosen arm correct) on the
discordant items, p-value and discordant count both executor-computed
(defect class 1). Minimum discordant floor d ≥ 10 for any CONTINUE.

- **CONTINUE:** p < 0.05 (one-sided) AND d ≥ 10 → the hypothesis
  "plan-informed instrument selection moves decisions vs random,
  label-free" continues. Licenses plan-as-instrument as a candidate
  adaptive-loop component. The honest caveat rides with it: this is
  **steering-adjacent** (the plan chooses the measurement instrument),
  not full autonomous steering; it belongs to the label-free
  None-class (§2.1 — no training, no labels). This would be the
  program's first label-free plan→decision causal signal.
- **KILL:** p ≥ 0.05 AND d ≥ 40 → retires the instrument-selection
  channel: plan-informed choice moves nothing under this apparatus.
  d ≥ 40 is the registered 80%-power floor (see §4; executor-computed).
- **HELD:** p ≥ 0.05 with d < 40 → underpowered; licenses nothing,
  retires nothing. No rescue.
- **RUN-INVALID:** any §5 breaking-point miss (apparatus-void,
  UNTESTED — hypothesis gets no verdict, no number minted).

Notes: (i) a reverse-direction asymmetry (planner systematically picks
the worse phrasing) is an unlicensed descriptive observation, not a
verdict; (ii) this gate refines the design session's "two-sided"
sketch to one-sided, matching the directional quantitative prediction
in §4 — deviation recorded, subject to Law #14 review of the pre-reg.

## 3. Cheapest test

- 60 choice-arm forwards: EXP112 pinned readout on the planner-chosen
  variant per item. The random arm (seed-pinned) is **derived from the

- Total: **60 generations + 120 forwards, $0 CPU.**

The paired McNemar runs per item on
(readout[plan-chosen variant] vs readout[random-chosen variant]),
discordant on correctness. Under H0 (the planner's choice carries no
information), the plan-chosen arm is another random pick → symmetry.

## 4. Mathematical license

- **EXP113's T2 clearance (bound CONTINUE, LOG-4584):** few-shot P2 and
  priming P5 clear 80% on both task shapes on this bench line; the
  planner channel works — EXP119 Phase-0a 10/10 and EXP121 Phase-0a
  10/10 on the same bench. The choice-generation is a T2-P2 generation
  plus a mechanical extraction; elicitation is licensed.
- **EXP112's readout (bound HELD, 41/60):** the decision channel works;
  baseline reproduction 60/60 archived (EXP121).
- **Paired McNemar with executor-computed p:** exact one-sided
  binomial test on the discordant pairs — the correct paired test for
  two decision rules on the same items; no asymptotic approximation.
- **Power floor (executor-computed, registered here):** at one-sided
  α = 0.05, power ≥ 0.80 for true discordant asymmetry q = 0.70
  requires d ≥ 40 (power(q=0.70): d=38 → 0.774, d=40 → 0.807,
  d=44 → 0.861 — computed exactly for this memo). Hence the KILL bar
  d ≥ 40; below it a p ≥ 0.05 is underpowered → HELD.
- **Why phrasing-selection is a valid planning instrument:** the
  planner's choice is a deterministic function of the generated plan
  text (plan-conditioned), and the plan text never enters the decision
  prompt. The token-asymmetry confound that fenced EXP119/EXP121 is
  **sidestepped by construction, not by disclosure** — there is no
  plan-token arm whose effect could be attributed to extra context
  tokens, because no plan token reaches the readout.
- **Quantitative prediction:** under planning-steers-decisions, the
  discordant pairs skew toward plan-chosen-correct (q > 0.5, targeted
  q ≈ 0.70 for power); under planning-is-inert, the discordant split
  is symmetric (q = 0.5) and the choice-gate (a) below fires or the
  test lands HELD/KILL.

## 5. Breaking points (conjunctive — any miss → apparatus-void,
## UNTESTED, no number minted)

- **(a) Choice-information gate:** each phrasing chosen ≥ 20% of the
  time (≥ 12/60). A constant choice is a decision-inert control, not
  a treatment.
- **(b) Phase-0 phrasing-discriminability:** on a pinned 10-item probe,
  the two phrasing variants must yield **different** EXP112 readouts
  on ≥ 3/10 items. If the variants are decision-indistinguishable,
  the planner's choice cannot move decisions.
- **(c) Per-arm G1 guard as a CONSTRUCTION-TIME filter:** both
  phrasing variants must pass H6's channel-level G1 (M ≥ 0.01) on the
  Phase-0 probe, symmetric across variants. This is the EXP121
  class-6 defect made structurally impossible: G1 validity is a
  property of the **prompt variants** (fixed, pinned strings), not a
  post-hoc per-item screen on a treatment arm. Collapsing items are
  pre-registered as invalid; per-item G1 degeneracy in the full run
  on the pinned prompts fires **RUN-INVALID** (apparatus drift), never
  item-dropping.
- **(d) Δθ=0 pins:** LOG-331 backbone hash asserted before the first
  generation and after the last forward; mismatch → RUN-INVALID.
- **(e) Raw records:** all 60 choice-generations and 120 forward
  records persisted and hash-pinned **before** any verdict (EXP119
  lesson); p-value executor-computed, never hand-written
  (defect class 1).
- Additionally: planner choice-valid ≥ 8/10 on the Phase-0a probe
  (T2-P2 parse rule, EXP113 §3.4); both variant tails byte-identical
  to the QUESTION_SUFFIX tail (the identity surface is asserted
  absent, not assumed).

## 6. Dodge of the three H11 failure modes

- **EXP117 (generation channel dead):** zero entity-name generation
  anywhere. The only generation is the EXP113-cleared T2-P2 planner;
  the decision readout is EXP112's pinned logit channel only. A
  degenerated planner is caught by the Phase-0a choice-valid gate
  before any metric is read.
- **EXP119 (tokenization identity):** plan text never enters any
  decision prompt; both phrasing variants are byte-verbatim fixed
  strings ending in the identical `" Question: Who is higher in
  rank? Answer:"` tail. The [-3:] identity surface does not exist by
  construction; asserted byte-identical at Phase-0.
- **EXP121 (plan-arm G1 mass collapse):** the readout prompts are the
  pinned baseline prompts — G1 behavior is the EXP112-archived
  channel's own (60/60 reproduction in EXP121). Plan tokens cannot
  push mass off the final position because they are never in the
  context. Per-item G1 is screened at construction time on the two
  prompt variants, symmetrically — the selection-biased dropout that
  killed EXP121 has no treatment arm to act on.

**Own failure signature:** if the planner's phrasing choice carries no
information — choice constant (gate (a) fires) or label-decorrelated —
the paired comparison degenerates to random-vs-random; McNemar sees
symmetry and the lane lands HELD (or KILL if d ≥ 40 by fluke of
volume, which the power floor guards against inflating). An apparatus-
void is honest here: it means the instrument was never a treatment.
