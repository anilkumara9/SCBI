## 4. Mathematical license

- **EXP113 [OBSERVATION]:** T1-P4 cleared at **58/60** parseable
  (≥48/60 bar; 2 empties scored UNPARSEABLE, far below the 10%
  RUN-INVALID threshold). The clearance is real on the single-letter
  T1 shape; the entity-name shape is the registered unknown — this
  is exactly the untested protocol the EXP116 review flagged.
- **EXP107 bench discipline [FACT]:** 60 items, four 15-item cells,
  meaning-preserving template decorrelation (the template cue is
  killed by construction).
- **EXP121 G1-collapse data [OBSERVATION]:** 22/60 plan-arm items
  collapsed below the 0.01 mass bar (0.000142–0.008571) while the
  baseline arm never dropped below 0.068 — the decision channel
  degrades when the context is perturbed. A generation protocol that
  perturbs the decoding distribution (repetition penalty) inherits
  this risk.
- **Breaking mechanism [INTERPRETATION]:** `repetition_penalty=1.2`
  down-weights tokens already present in the input context — which
  includes the entity-name tokens in the premise. Directionally this
  biases *against* emitting the entity name. On the T1 shape the
  target letter `A` also appears in the prompt and still emitted
  58/60, so the penalty is mild (a nudge, not a block) — but on the
  entity-name shape the nudge compounds the two attractors EXP117
  observed: the **enumeration attractor** (`\n\n1. Jupiter\n\n2.
  Mars...`, 0/10 parseable under exact-match) and the
  **continuation attractor** (`Mercury is the highest-ranked`).
  EXP113's own T2 data point shows the mechanism in the extreme:
  P4=0/60 on outlines — the penalty derails structured generation
  into off-distribution text. The prediction is therefore genuinely
  directional: either the penalty is mild enough that the name
  survives (clearance band), or it tips outputs into the
  listing/continuation attractors and the elicitation gate fires.

## 6. Breaking point — CHECKED, not tripped

Two breaking conditions were registered; both were tested by this
lane (tokenizer-only, $0 CPU, real Pythia-410m snapshot tokenizer,
vocab 50254):

1. **Multi-token entities breaking mechanical parsing (the H18
   pattern):** all 10 EXP107-bench entity names (Mars, Venus,
   Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Iron, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Steel)
   encode as **exactly one token** with leading space (e.g.
   `ĠMars` → [13648], `ĠJupiter` → [34434]). Zero multi-token
   entities. Moreover, the H20 parse rule is a *text-level*
   exact/substring match on the generation — multi-token status
   could not break it the way it broke EXP112's logit-slot
   readout. **Not tripped.**
2. **P4's pinned definition not transferring to the EXP107 template
   shape:** the pinned piece is the decoding hyperparameters
   (`repetition_penalty=1.2`, `no_repeat_ngram_size=3`, greedy,
   `max_new_tokens=4`) — these are template-independent and transfer
   exactly; every entity name fits within the 4-token generation cap
   (all are 1 token). The template swap (T1-P1 single-letter template
   → EXP107 entity-name `{PROMPT}`) is the experiment's *registered
   unknown*, not an apparatus void — this is the licensed "new
   hypothesis under a named-but-untested protocol" reading of the
   design session, compliant with Law #4. **Not tripped.**
