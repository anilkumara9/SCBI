## 1. Exact question

[HYPOTHESIS] In the frozen Pythia-410m, does *state-feedback re-targeting*
of a perturbation — computing the second step from the measured trajectory
response to the first — move the residual stream further **per unit norm**
than any open-loop injection spending the same norm budget?

The causal construct is the *feedback gain*: the claim is that information
about the measured response d₁ = x(δ₁) − x(0) has nonzero causal value when
fed back into δ₂, i.e., the loop's displacement is not reducible to its norm
budget. Stated plainly because the frozen deterministic model admits no
temporal loop: "closed-loop" here means **sequential sense-act forwards
within one evaluation** — forward with δ₁, measure d₁ at the readout site,
compute δ₂ = δ₁ + correction(d₁) under a correction rule frozen in the
pre-reg (no adaptive tuning of the tuner — Law #4), forward again, measure
d₂. The second perturbation is a function of measured state, not a
pre-registered constant. That is the whole difference from every dead lane,
and it is the only difference this family is allowed to claim.

## 3. Cheapest test

**Two-step sense-act vs norm-matched open-loop, CPU, ~2h, $0.** Exact
specification:

- **Arms (all norm-budget-matched, total budget B pinned in the pre-reg):**
  - *Closed-loop 2-step:* δ₁ at L20 (direction class pinned — v₁ from
    EXP127 or isotropic; the pre-reg picks one and justifies it), measure
    d₁ at x_{24,last}, δ₂ = δ₁ + η·correction(d₁) with the correction rule
    frozen (e.g., residual-targeting along the measured displacement, or a
    Newton-type step on a registered scalar objective), measure d₂.
    ‖δ₁‖ + ‖correction‖ = B.
  - *Open-loop sum:* δ₁ + δ₂ with both directions pre-registered (no
    feedback), same total norm B.
  - *Single shot:* δ along the best pre-registered direction, ‖δ‖ = B.
- **Primary metric:** displacement-per-norm = ‖d‖ / B per arm, paired per
  item over the 10-prompt EXP107 decision set (same set as EXP127/EXP128,
  so the three dynamics measurements are directly comparable).
- **Bar:** closed-loop ≥ 2× the best open-loop arm → CONTINUE; ≤ 1.2× →
  KILL. The norm-matching is the proof that direction-adaptation ≠ dose —
  without it, the family is a dose tweak and never launches.
- **Cost:** 10 items × 3 arms × 2 forwards ≈ 60 forwards + sensing
  overhead, ~2h CPU, $0. No training, no labels, Δθ=0.

## 4. Mathematical license

- [THEOREM] (Newton-type iteration): near a root of a locally smooth map,
  Newton iteration converges quadratically where fixed-point iteration
  converges linearly — *if* the residual map is locally smooth. The pilot
  is the test of whether that "if" holds in the residual stream, and
  EXP128's result is now part of the "if": at ε=1e-3 the map is *not*
  locally linear in the finite-difference sense (1/12 within 25%), so the
  pre-reg may not invoke this theorem at that scale without its own
  linearity gate.
- [OBSERVATION] (EXP127, LOG-4698, bound): median R = 8.240640 at L20→L24 —
  the amplifier exists *somewhere*; the loop's correction has a direction
  worth chasing. EXP127's apparatus (exact JVPs via autograd) is the
  licensed sensing instrument — it passed Law #14 and never touched finite
  differences.
- [OBSERVATION] (frontier): Activation-LQR is the working closed-loop form
  (u_t = −Kx_t, tracking-error bounds, no training) — the existence proof
  that feedback control, not bigger open-loop pushes, is the frontier's
  answer. This family is its $0 shadow.
- **Why the S5-2 4.87% bound does not fence this:** S5-2 bounds per-arm
  *decision effects* along *semantic/static* directions. CLP's metric is
  displacement-per-norm along *dynamics-sensed* directions with a decision
  statistic recorded only as observation. The bound constrains claims from
  old directions, not measurements on a new channel — provided Law #14
  agrees the channel is new (§7 tripwire).

## 5. Quantitative prediction (band, from program records only)

- **Null (loop adds nothing):** closed-loop / best-open-loop per-norm
  ratio ≤ 1.2 — the correction is dead weight; any displacement came from
  the norm budget, and EXP095's dose kill absorbs the result.
- **Ambiguous:** ratio ∈ (1.2, 2) → HELD.
- **CLP hypothesis (feedback has causal value):** ratio ≥ 2 — the same
  norm, steered by measured state, moves the residual at least twice as
  far. The 2× bar is set above measurement noise and above any
  second-order dose artifact; it is the smallest ratio that cannot be
  explained by "the correction happened to add norm in a good direction"
  (the open-loop sum arm controls exactly that).
- **Saturation branch:** d₂ ≈ d₁ under any frozen correction rule —
  predicts KILL via the threshold mechanism EXP128's [OBSERVATION]
  (S(ε/2) ≈ 1.5–2.4× S(ε), saturating/threshold-like response) suggested.

## 6. Breaking point (conjunctive)

(a) **Δθ=0** — backbone LOG-331 pin before and after; any mismatch →
    apparatus-void.
(b) **Norm-budget matching** — every arm spends exactly B; any arm with a
    different budget → apparatus-void (the ratio would measure the budget,
    not the feedback). This is the dose-fence made mechanical.
(c) **ε-discipline (learned from EXP128)** — the loop's sensing step may
    NOT assume the finite-difference linear regime at ε=1e-3 that EXP128's
    breaking point killed (1/12 within 25%). The pre-reg must pick one of:
    (i) exact-JVP sensing via autograd (licensed by EXP127's apparatus);
    (ii) nonlinear-by-design feedback — the correction uses only measured
    displacements, never a linear model, so EXP128's linearity failure is
    irrelevant by construction; or (iii) its own ε-descent linearity gate
    (descend ε until ≥10/12 within 25%, with a floor; miss → apparatus-void).
    Assuming the ε=1e-3 linear regime without one of these → apparatus-void.
(d) **Frozen correction rule** — the rule mapping d₁ → correction is pinned
    in the pre-reg; any per-item tuning of the tuner → apparatus-void
    (Law #4).
(e) **No training, no weight mutation** — pure forwards; no optimizer, no
    backward pass into weights.

Any miss → **apparatus-void, hypothesis UNTESTED** (Law #9 ordering:
guards → budget audit → forwards → decision).

**Not a resurrection of TGA or DSF.** TGA (EXP127, CONTINUE'd) is one-shot

## 8. Why it differs from killed work, and what would kill it (difficulty directive)

**Why it differs.** The honest admission first: CLP *is* a perturbation
instrument — it still injects at fixed layers, and no one should pretend
otherwise. What it is not: it does not push a pre-registered semantic
direction and hope (EXP077/EXP094/EXP093); it does not buy displacement
with norm (EXP095 — fenced by budget matching); it does not read content,
decode snapshots, or compose plan text (EXP115/116/123/124, H22, H23).
The injection direction becomes a *function of measured trajectory
response* — the same move that separates LQR from open-loop control, and
the same move no killed lane ever made. If that function has no causal
value, the ratio kills the family on $0 — the test is designed to execute
its own hypothesis, not to protect it.

**What would kill it.** (1) Its own ratio: ≤ 1.2× → KILL the
adaptive-pursuit premise on $0. (2) Saturation: the correction buys nothing
because the dynamics are threshold-like (EXP128's [OBSERVATION] made
flesh) → KILL. (3) Law #14's channel-vs-dose ruling: adaptation = dose →
never launches. (4) ε-discipline failure → apparatus-void. (5) A cheaper
reading: if the open-loop sum arm matches the closed-loop arm, the
"feedback" was just a second push — KILL, and the pre-reg's correction
rule is recorded as dose in disguise.
