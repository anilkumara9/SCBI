## 1. Exact question

[HYPOTHESIS] Does the frozen Pythia-410m's dynamical sensitivity field have
localized structure (soft spots) or is it uniform across layers — i.e., does
the computation concentrate layer-wise sensitivity in a way a dynamics-first
intervention could exploit?

Formalized as the finite-difference gain field:

    S_l = E_items,u [ ‖ x_{24,last}(x_l + δ) − x_{24,last}(x_l) ‖ / ‖δ‖ ]

where x_l is the residual stream at block-l output (l = 0…23, x_0 = embedding
output), δ = ε·u with u a random isotropic unit vector (1024-dim, drawn
fresh per item/layer from a seeded generator), ε the fixed perturbation
scale, and x_{24,last} the final-token residual at the readout-layer input
(the same readout site every prior injection experiment used). S_l is the
finite-difference estimator of the layer-to-readout propagator norm
‖∂x_{24,last}/∂x_l‖ — a direct measurement of *sensitivity*, the third
quantity the program never measured (information ✓ via decodability,
influence ✓ via injection, sensitivity ✗).

## 3. Cheapest test

**Finite-difference gain field, CPU, ~1–2h, $0.** Exact specification:

- **Layer grid:** all 24 block outputs x_0…x_23 (x_0 = post-embedding).
- **Decision set:** the 10 verbatim EXP107-bench prompts (canonical-order
  first 10 — the same decision set as EXP127, so the two measurements are
  directly comparable).
- **δ definition:** δ = ε·u, u ~ isotropic unit vector in ℝ^1024, seeded
  generator (seed pinned in the pre-reg), ε fixed across ALL layers, items,
  and draws — the same perturbation budget per layer (breaking point (c)).
  [ASSUMPTION] recorded: isotropic δ is task-agnostic by construction — it
  carries no semantic content, so the field cannot confound with content
  (Law #7 clean by construction; no labels anywhere).
- **Draws:** K = 4 isotropic draws per (item, layer); S_l averaged over the
  40 (item × draw) samples.
- **Trajectory definition:** final-token residual at x_{24,last} only — the
  decision-relevant trajectory. Full-sequence divergence is barred (it
  confounds with other positions' content; the pre-reg records this).
- **Cost:** 10 items × 24 layers × 4 draws × 2 forwards = 1920 forwards,
  ≈ 1–2h CPU, $0. No autograd, no training, no labels.
- **Linearity check (breaking point (b)):** on a pinned subset (3 items ×
  4 layers), repeat with ε/2; require |S(ε/2) − S(ε)|/S(ε) ≤ 0.25 on ≥ 80%
  of check points — the finite difference is in the linear regime. Miss →
  apparatus-void.

- [THEOREM] (finite-difference / propagator norm): S_l as defined is the
  model-free estimator of ‖∂x_{24,last}/∂x_l‖, the layer-to-readout gain —
  the same object EXP127 measured via JVPs for the single span l=20, now

- [OBSERVATION] (EXP127, LOG-4698, bound): median R = 8.240640 at the

  layer-wise. EXP127's prompt-invariance note (R ∈ [8.00, 8.53] across
  prompts) is the license for the 10-prompt decision set here.
- [THEOREM-adjacent] (Kreiss intuition): in non-normal systems, sensitivity
  need not be layer-uniform — transient amplification concentrates where
  the dynamics are most non-normal. DSF is the test of whether EXP086's
  He≈0.77 is load-bearing (structured field) or decorative (uniform field).
- **Why the S5-2 4.87% bound does not fence this:** S5-2 bounds *per-arm
  decision effects* ≥ 4.87% along *semantic/static* directions. DSF mints a
  *gain-norm field*, not a decision effect: no decision metric is read, no
  arm carries task signal (isotropic δ), and the field is a measurement of
  the propagator, not an intervention claim. The bound constrains what may
  be *claimed from old directions*, not what may be *measured on new ones*.
- **Why G1's QK-subspace KILL does not touch this:** G1 falsified a
  mechanism *account* (QK visibility ≠ rescue) on QK/OV projector energies

- **Why EXP094's L11 inertness and EXP077's cone kills do not touch this:**
  both are zero-*flip* / zero-*displacement* results along *semantic*
  directions. DSF measures norm-divergence along *random* directions and
