## 1. Exact question

[HYPOTHESIS] On the frozen Pythia-410m, at matched sampling budget, does
*margin-verified best-of-N selection* beat greedy decoding by a registered
margin on the relational bench class?

The causal construct is **verifier-selection gain**: the claim is that the
frozen model's own margin — the registered cosine decision statistic —
carries nonzero selection value, i.e., picking the max-margin sample from N
draws improves the bench score beyond what the budget alone buys. Stated
plainly: this family does not steer, inject, decode snapshots, or compose
plan text. It *samples* (generation as measurement, not as elicitation of
content) and *selects* by a frozen, untrained statistic. That is the whole
construct, and it is the only thing this family is allowed to claim.

## 3. Cheapest test

**Two-stage, budget-controlled, CPU, ~3–4h, $0.** Exact specification:

- **Bench:** 60 items from the relational bench class (the EXP107
  template-decorrelated bench family — the same bench lineage EXP127/128/129
  used, so the four dynamics/scaling measurements sit on comparable ground).
- **Stage 1 — verifier calibration (the cheap falsifier, ~30 min):**
  greedy sample per item (60 generations); compute the registered margin
  statistic per item; correlate margin with correctness (labels used for
  evaluation only — Law #7 clean, no leakage into any generator). Pinned
  correlation threshold in the pre-reg. Fail → RUN-INVALID, Stage 2 never
  licensed. This is the falsification test the dossier buried in its
  breaking point — foregrounded here as a stage gate, because it burns ~6%
  of the budget and can kill the family before the expensive sampling.
- **Stage 2 — scaling (only if Stage 1 passes):** per item, N=16 samples
  (960 generations) + greedy (60). Arms: *margin-selected* best-of-N,
  *random-selected* best-of-N (same 16 samples, uniform pick — the budget
  control), *greedy*. Primary metric: accuracy per arm; McNemar paired
  best-of-N(margin) vs greedy. The random-selection arm isolates
  verifier-selection from budget — if margin-select ≈ random-select, the
  gain is tokens, not verification.
- **Elicitation:** an EVALID-cleared protocol (P2 few-shot or P5 priming
  from EXP113/H7, 60/60 on the T1 single-token-answer shape) — pinned in
  the pre-reg; EXP109's elicitation-failure gate (n_valid < threshold →
  RUN-INVALID before any metric is read) carried verbatim.
- **Cost:** 60 + 960 + re-parse overhead ≈ 1,100 generations, ~3–4h CPU,
  $0. No training, no labels in the loop, Δθ=0.

## 4. Mathematical license

- [THEOREM] (best-of-N dominance): best-of-N with a *calibrated* verifier
  dominates greedy in expectation — the pilot tests whether our margin *is*
  a calibrated verifier. The theorem is the license; the calibration check
  is the test of the theorem's precondition.
- [OBSERVATION] (frontier, program records): LLM-Monkeys log-linear
  coverage; self-consistency +17.9pp; ∇-Reasoner 71.2→80.4% on MATH-500
  (verified, LOG-185 record); Meta-Reasoner +9–12% at −28–35% time. The
  frontier's numbers live on this axis — this family is the program's
  honest $0 entry to it.
- [OBSERVATION] (EXP113/H7, LOG-4585, apparatus CONTINUE): protocols P2
  (few-shot) and P5 (priming) clear ≥48/60 on both elicitation task shapes
  (60/60 on T1) — generation on this backbone is *validatable*, so the
  generation channel is not dead as an apparatus. This is the
  construct-validity gate defect class 8 demands, and it exists.
- **Why the S5-2 4.87% bound does not fence this:** S5-2 bounds per-arm
  *decision effects* of *static injections* along *semantic directions*.
  TTS injects nothing; it samples and selects. The bound constrains claims
  from old instruments, not measurements on a new channel — provided Law
  #14 agrees the channel is new (§7 tripwire).

## 5. Quantitative prediction (band, from program records only)

- **Null (verifier adds nothing / curve flat):** Δ < +4pp at N=16 —
  margin-selection is noise; the verifier is miscalibrated or the bench
  class is saturated.
- **Budget account (the honest confound):** margin-select ≈ random-select
  (both ≥ greedy +4pp) — the gain is tokens, not verification; verdict
  HELD with the budget account recorded, not CONTINUE.
- **TTS hypothesis (verifier-selection gain):** best-of-N(margin) ≥ greedy
  +10pp (paired p ≤ 0.05) AND beats random-selection by the pinned margin —
  the verifier carries selection value. The +10pp bar is coherent at n=60
  (+10pp ≈ 6 items; (6,0)→p=0.03125 — the H4 review's lesson that +8pp was
  meaningless at n=20 is honored by the larger n).
- **Calibration failure:** margin uncorrelated with correctness on the
  greedy arm → RUN-INVALID at Stage 1 (the informative outcome either way —
  it characterizes the margin, not just the method).

## 6. Breaking point (conjunctive)

(a) **Δθ=0** — backbone LOG-331 pin before and after; any mismatch →
    apparatus-void.
(b) **Elicitation validity** — an EVALID-cleared protocol (P2 or P5) pinned;
    EXP109's elicitation-failure gate (n_valid < threshold) fires →
    RUN-INVALID before any metric is read (Law #9 ordering: guards →
    elicitation audit → metrics → decision).
(c) **Verifier-calibration pre-gate** — Stage 1 correlation threshold met;
    miss → RUN-INVALID, Stage 2 never runs.
(d) **Budget control** — the random-selection arm is present and its
    outcome is reported regardless of the margin arm's result; a pre-reg
    without it → apparatus-void (the verdict would measure the budget).
(e) **No training, no weight mutation** — pure forwards; the verifier is
    the registered statistic, never a trained head (Law #7).

Any miss → **apparatus-void or RUN-INVALID as stated, hypothesis UNTESTED**
(no silent downgrade to a weaker claim).

G1's QK-subspace KILL (a mechanism account, not a selection channel). The

**The EXP126 threat, named.** EXP126 reclassified the decodability
CONTINUEs as domain-set-detector correlations: what looked like entity
reading was set-structure reading. The same threat applies to the margin
verifier: if the margin on the relational bench is a domain-set correlate,
best-of-N by margin selects domain-conforming completions — the bench
score rises while cognition doesn't. The Stage-1 calibration check
(margin vs correctness on the greedy arm) catches a *miscalibrated*
verifier but NOT a verifier calibrated *through* the spurious correlate.
Therefore the pre-reg must (i) run the calibration check on the
relational bench itself (no cross-bench inheritance), (ii) disclose the
domain-set threat as a named limitation, and (iii) tag any CONTINUE as
"margin-verified best-of-N beats greedy on this bench class" —
search-and-selection, never "the model reasons better."

**Not a resurrection of TGA, DSF, or CLP.** TGA (EXP127, CONTINUE'd)
measured the propagator's leading direction; DSF (EXP128, RUN-INVALID)
mapped a sensitivity field; CLP (EXP129, KILLed) tested feedback gain.
TTS's construct (sampling + verifier selection) is disjoint from all
three — none is re-gated, none is revived.

## 8. Why it differs from killed work, and what would kill it (difficulty directive)

**Why it differs.** The honest admission first: TTS *is* the frontier's
game, and the dossier ranked it last for a reason — it is the least
original family in the sprint. What it is not: it is not another
decodability number (no probe, no head, no readout as steering); it is
not another injection (nothing is added to any activation); it is not
plan composition (no text near the prompt is doing the work); it is not
EXP056 repeated (registered, powered, verifier-designed); it is not H4
(planner-executor) wearing a new name. The new quantity is
*verifier-selection gain at matched budget* — unmeasured in the program's
history — and the design carries its own executioners: the Stage-1
calibration gate, the random-selection control, and the elicitation-
failure gate. If the margin is noise, Stage 1 kills the family for ~30
minutes of CPU. If the gain is budget, the random-selection arm kills the
verifier story. The test is designed to execute its own hypothesis.

**What would kill it.** (1) Stage 1: margin uncorrelated with correctness
→ RUN-INVALID, family unlaunched-in-practice. (2) Its own flat curve:
Δ < +4pp at N=16 → KILL the axis for this bench class — stop proposing
TTS pilots here. (3) The budget account: margin-select ≈ random-select →
the verifier adds nothing; no CONTINUE. (4) Law #14's generation-channel
ruling: construct invalid → never launches. (5) The EXP126 threat
realized: the margin is a domain-set correlate and the calibration check
passes through it — then a CONTINUE is bench-gaming, and the honest
verdict is HELD pending a decorrelated bench (the pre-reg must name this
branch).
