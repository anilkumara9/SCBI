## 4. Mathematical license

[THEOREM] (forward-mode AD exactness): for a C^1 map f, `jvp(f, (y,), (u,))` returns Df(y)·u exactly, up to floating-point roundoff, by the chain rule on the executed computation graph. It is not a difference quotient; no discretization parameter exists in its definition.

[PROOF-OF-DIFFERENCE] EXP128's estimator S_l(ε) satisfies lim_{ε→0} S_l(ε) = S_l^*(u) for each fixed probe (g_l is C^1). EXP128 measured S_l(ε) at ε = 1e-3 and required S_l(ε) ≈ S_l^* via the linearity check; the data refuted the approximation (saturation remainder dominated — LOG-4736 §1). The JVP apparatus computes **the limit object S_l^* directly** — the very quantity EXP128's estimator was trying to approximate. The failure mode "the remainder dominates the estimate" is not avoided by tuning; it is **inexpressible** in AD, which has no remainder term. An apparatus with no ε cannot be "ε retuning in disguise": retuning varies a parameter the new apparatus does not possess.

**Where the license breaks (the honest boundary):** the JVP exactly measures the *infinitesimal* gain. LOG-4736 §1 shows the linear regime ends below t* ≈ 5e-4, while any feasible injection budget is ~0.1–0.3 (EXP131's B = 1% of ‖y‖). The derivative characterizes a regime **3+ orders of magnitude below any intervention**. As a TARGETING map (which layer to inject at), the JVP field is the wrong object: the layer maximizing infinitesimal gain need not maximize finite-budget response, and the saturated regime — where injection actually operates — is invisible to it. **The license covers dynamics characterization (EXP127-class: "what does the frozen dynamics look like?"), NOT targeting.** EXP127's R = 8.24 is precedent that infinitesimal characterizations are publishable boundary facts. Any targeting claim in the downstream pre-registration → gate REJECT.
