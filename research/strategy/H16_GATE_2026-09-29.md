## The six mandatory items

### 1. Exact question

[HYPOTHESIS] Is the EXP111/EXP120 trained-head signal (layer 20,
final-token embeddings, frozen Pythia-410m) a *subject-position detector*
or an *entity-identity readout*? Test: the pinned H5 head protocol —
byte-verbatim head spec, split seed 20260929, layer-20 extraction site,
Δθ=0 backbone pin — on a newly constructed position-scrambled bench where
the label encodes a property of the **target entity's identity** and the
target's sentence position carries no information (scrambled across premise
slots, balanced within each label class).

### 2. Decision changed

Design note first: the label semantics must change for the test to be
discriminative. Bench 1/2 labels are y=1 iff A-first (the position feature
itself — H5 pre-reg §3.2; EXP120 §8). Bench 3 labels are y=1 iff the
highest-ranked entity belongs to domain-set D1 (vs D2) — a property of the
target's **identity** — with the target's position (first/middle/last)
balanced within each y class. What is carried verbatim is the H5 **protocol**
(head spec, split seed, extraction site, gates), not the label definition.
The construction lane pins the bench-3 label docstring independently
(Law #7; the EXP120 FIX-1 discipline).

- **CONTINUE (identity-readout interpretation):** test accuracy **≥ 20/30**
  (one-sided binomial p ≤ 0.0494 vs chance, the EXP111 bar) **AND** G < 0.30
  (G = acc(F)−acc(T) over the flipped-word cells, carried verbatim) → the
  trained head extracts the target's identity with position decorrelated;
  the two-bench CONTINUE (EXP111/EXP120) gains a mechanistic foothold —
  the signal is not exhausted by a position detector.
- **KILL (identity interpretation):** test accuracy **≤ 18/30** (p ≥ 0.1808)
  or **G ≥ 0.30** → the identity reading is killed. EXP111/EXP120's
  **observations stand** (Law #8 — nothing retroactively un-happens) but
  their CONTINUE is **reclassified as a position-detector correlation**
  under Law #11's two-layer tagging (observation vs interpretation); any
  mechanistic use of the signal in the program is retired.
- **HELD:** 19/30 with G < 0.30 → underpowered zone; no rerun, no rescue
  (the EXP106/EXP111 no-rescue discipline).

### 3. Cheapest test

One 60-item bench-3 authored independently (new domains, new verb families,
four 15-item cells per the EXP107 discipline: position × flipped, see §6),
60 layer-20 final-token embedding extractions on CPU (~30–60 min, Δθ=0,
backbone hash pinned before/after per the EXP111 §6 discipline), then
EXP111's pinned head protocol on the archived embeddings — seed 20260929,
stratified 30/30 split, `LogisticRegression(l2, C=1.0, lbfgs,
max_iter=1000, random_state=20260929)`, no sweeps. $0 CPU. No generation
anywhere in the design (the EXP108/EXP109/EXP115/EXP116 hard constraint
carries over); no plan text, no tokenization-identity surface (EXP119's
break has no purchase); reads only at layer 20 where EXP111/EXP120 are
bound (EXP118's early-layer degeneracy never entered).

### 4. Mathematical license

[OBSERVATION] EXP111: 24/30 (one-sided p ≈ 0.0014), G=0.0000 — a real
above-chance signal on bench 1 with no template-detector signature.
EXP120: 25/30 (exact one-sided p=0.0001624), G=−0.2000 — the signal
replicates on an independently constructed decorrelated bench. The
position cue is the **only surviving confound** named by both verdict
reviews' narrow readings (EXP120 §8: "a CONTINUE means the head extracts
*position* on a second bench"). [OBSERVATION] EXP111 §7 layer-resolved
descriptive context: the signal is decodable at L4 (24/30, G=−0.1333),
L8 (30/30, G=0.0000), L12 (29/30, G=0.0667), L16 (30/30, G=0.0000) —
diffuse across layers with no template-detector signature at any layer
(all |G| ≤ 0.1333 << 0.30; descriptive-only, ~18.5% family-wise FPR),
consistent with a low-level positional/structural feature rather than a
localized semantic readout. [PROPOSITION] The EXP107 builder's registered
honest limitation: "the flip also moves the target from sentence-subject
to sentence-object position, so a collapsed score kills the EXP106
transfer reading under EITHER a word-template cue or a subject-position
cue; the test does not identify which surface cue the head used." H16 is
the identified successor that names the confound the EXP107 discipline
registered but could not resolve. [HYPOTHESIS] Under identity-readout the
same pinned protocol scores ≥20/30 with G<0.30 on the scrambled bench;
under position-detector it collapses. The license is one-shot — the pinned
split seed, head spec, and bars carry over byte-verbatim; no sweeps, no
per-layer tuning (Law #7), no seed re-rolls.

### 5. Quantitative prediction (directions only — no outcome licensed)

- **Under identity-readout:** test accuracy ≥ 20/30 with G < 0.30 — the
  same bars as bench 1/2, because the bars are properties of the protocol,
  not the bench; the pure-position detector scores ≈30/60 on bench 3 by
  the position-scramble gate (below the bar).
- **Under position-detector:** test accuracy collapses toward chance
  (≈15/30, below the 20/30 bar) — the position cue the head would use is
  decorrelated from the label by construction — or the EXP107
  template-detector signature reappears (G ≥ 0.30). Both death modes are
  pre-registered.
- **Power:** n=30 test items; the bar clears α=0.05 one-sided.
  Fine-grained comparisons between bench-1/2 and bench-3 accuracies are
  NOT licensed (unlicensed cross-bench effect sizes — the H15 lesson).

### 6. Breaking point

**Bench construction is the breaking point.** The construction lane must pass
ALL of these before any head is trained; any failure → **apparatus-void**:
no metric read, no experiment number minted, the hypothesis recorded
**UNTESTED, not killed**:

1. **Independent item authoring:** new entity domains, new verb families,
   no shared template strings with bench 1 or bench 2, no reuse of
   bench-1/bench-2 items (Law #9 theater — explicitly barred).
2. **Decorrelation checks pass on bench 3 alone:** four balanced 15-item
   cells (position × flipped); G-C1 pure-template detector ≤ 36/60;
   **position-scramble gate:** a pure-position detector (predicts the label
   from the target's sentence position alone) scores ≤ 36/60 — the new
   gate that makes the test discriminative.
3. **G-C2** entity-rank correlation vs EXP107 builder records: |Pearson|
   ≤ 0.10 (design check; feeds no bench-1/2 labels or embeddings into
   analysis — Law #13).
4. **G-C3** template-decomposition pin: QUESTION_SUFFIX byte-exact,
   deterministic rebuild — pass.
5. **Law #7 own label pin:** the builder documents how the label is derived
   from the item record (target-identity property under the new bench's own
   documented construction rule — not inherited from bench 1/2's y=A-first
   comment); per-item records carry the fields; pinning independently
   verifiable from the builder code. Test labels used only for the final
   registered evaluation.

A further apparatus breaking point, registered for the execution pre-reg:
determinism re-fit pass fails or train/test ID overlap → RUN-INVALID
(the EXP111 §3.4/§3.3 discipline).

## Law #13 — independent apparatus, not a repair

Bench 3 is an independent apparatus. Nothing in H16 re-reads bench-1 or
bench-2 data, re-fits on bench-1/2 embeddings, re-cuts the fixed EXP111/120
splits, or re-scores archived test predictions (Law #9). The head is fit
fresh on bench 3's own pinned labels. This is a new hypothesis under a new
apparatus, not a repair of EXP111/EXP120 (Law #4).
