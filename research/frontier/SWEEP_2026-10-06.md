## 2. RSI / intelligence-explosion axis — first primary-source milestone

- **Weco AIDE²: "Recursive self-improvement of AI research agents" (VERIFIED primary PDF, arXiv:2609.26457; dated 2026-09-23; authors Srikanth, Zhao, Xu, Wu, Jiang — Weco AI).** Bi-level loop at the harness layer: an outer-loop agent rewrites the inner-loop research agent's code; candidates are graded on private held-out data the inner agent never sees; fixed per-task dollar budgets force algorithmic (not compute) wins. One autonomous 8-day run: 100 outer steps, ~90% of proposals rejected, **seven successive improvements** (new search policy, context-compression memory mechanisms). The strongest discovered agent matches or exceeds AIDEhuman — a production research agent built over two years of human R&D, among the strongest on FML-Bench — on four held-out benchmarks spanning ML engineering, heuristic algorithm engineering, and out-of-distribution weather forecasting. Unoptimized-for bonus: reward-hacking rate on a held-out GPU-kernel family fell **55% → 32%** (primary numbers), below the human-engineered agent's 39%. **The honest negative (VERIFIED, primary text):** when a discovered agent was installed as the outer-loop improver, "its performance in that role cannot be decisively distinguished from the strong baseline" — i.e., Level 1 (net positive) demonstrated, Level 2 (ignition) NOT claimed. Summit relevance: HIGH with the standard fence — this is harness-layer automation-of-R&D, not weight-level RSI and not frozen-model cognition. The YC harness panel (Sept 7, REPORTED via explainx.ai) showed the same model scoring ~30%→95% on ARC-AGI by harness alone — a large share of "self-improvement" is scaffolding engineering.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.26457

A data-generation self-improvement loop, distinct from AIDE²'s code-rewriting loop. Vendor-adjacent claim, single secondary source.
