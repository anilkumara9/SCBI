- PAPER / arXiv:2610.06945v1 / "Beyond the Linear Representation Hypothesis: Non-Linear Activation Steering in Text-to-Image Models" (submitted Oct 3, 2026; VERIFIED via arxiv.org abs page). Butt, Skierś, Van De Weijer, Deja. Activations prompted for intermediate visual attributes rarely fall along the straight direction between endpoints; KANSteer models concept traversal as a curve through intermediate states, using Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks to give a one-dimensional interpretable coordinate whose learned functions define the trajectory. Across concepts and text-to-image diffusion transformers, activation trajectories deviate substantially from straight lines, and curved steering fits closer and traverses intermediate attributes more smoothly than linear steering. Summit relevance: HIGH (adjacent) — a published positive arguing linear directions are insufficient for steering even concept traversal; it rhymes with SCBI's steering-skeptic stance (decodable ≠ steerable) and with the inertness of our killed linear-static lanes (EXP094). Caveats: text-to-image diffusion transformers, not LLM decision-making — a steering positive there does not rehabilitate our frozen-Pythia kills; it sharpens the boundary question: what can be steered where, and with what trajectory class (the curved-traversal idea vs our static vectors is a design input for any revived steering lane).
- PAPER / arXiv:2610.01595 / "Before It Fades: Reinforcing Temporal Representations at Inference Time in VideoLLMs" (submitted Oct 1, 2026; VERIFIED via arXiv PDF + github.com/Youngwoo-git/Before-It-Fades repo; pending since Oct 4 sweep — now cleared). Shin, Ro, Kim, Kim (NeurIPS 2026). Temporal divergence vector τₗ per layer: temporal information peaks at intermediate layers then fades before output. Temporal Activation Injection extracts per-input τ_steer at the profile peak and reinjects into subsequent layers following the measured decay — training-free, last-token-only, β the only free hyperparameter. Reports +3.2 AoTBench / +2.2 TempCompass / +2.0 TVBench over baseline; stacks on top of ArrowRL (+2.1/+1.5/+0.9), confirming orthogonality to weight-level optimization; code posted. Summit relevance: HIGH (adjacent) — exactly our lanes' experimental shape: measure the per-input signal profile, inject at the peak, schedule by the measured decay. STEAL candidates: per-input vector extraction (τ_steer, not a global direction), profile-guided injection schedule, the ArrowRL-orthogonality test as the standard way to show activation-level methods add over trained baselines. Honest asymmetry (unchanged from Oct 4): the positive lives in VideoLLMs' mid-network temporal signal, which our killed lanes were not; Pythia-410m static-geometry ΔM=0 stands.
