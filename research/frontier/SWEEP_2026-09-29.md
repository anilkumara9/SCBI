## 1. Steering / decodability / representation reading

- **arXiv:2609.31401 — "Decodable In-Context State and Model Output Across Training"** (Ravulapalli, Chadha; submitted 2026-09-25, v1): tracks probe accuracy, model output, and steering response across Pythia pretraining/post-training checkpoints. Probe accuracy rises during pretraining while probe-guided steering benefit grows from negligible; and — key line — "an information-theoretic counterexample explains why decodability on errors alone cannot establish discarded output information." **SIGNIFICANT: independent theoretical complement to SCBI's empirical Decodability Gap** (decode success ≠ causal influence; "decodes at 6.7× chance; steers zero decisions"). Converging evidence, same direction as 2609.06951 (LOG-4499). Affected decision: belongs in v84 related work (v83 is a frozen release candidate per LOG-4512).
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31401
- **arXiv:2609.31603 — "User Model Extraction via Belief Self-Distillation"** (Holmov, Huang, Bykov, Akata; submitted 2026-09-25, v1): "bridges linear and causal probing by learning a compact user representation that can be both decoded and written back into the model… Unlike conventional probing, BSD isolates not only information present in activations, but a state whose causal role can be directly tested." Claims stronger interventions than matched hidden-state steering for user-belief manipulation (incl. refusal). Relevance: **adjacent/watch, NOT a threat** — BSD is a *learned* read-write representation (self-distilled, no external annotations), a different intervention class from SCBI's static decode-direction injection; SCBI's boundary claim covers static directions only. Nothing in it claims label-free autonomous steering of decisions via a decoded direction.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31603
- **arXiv:2609.31122 — "LocUS: Head Selection and Subspace Projection for Targeted Activation Steering"** (Tallini et al.; submitted 2026-09-25, v1): grounds activation steering in the model's output-vocabulary subspace; restricts the transform to a property-specific linear subspace and a sparse attention-head subset; matches/beats baselines on toxicity/sentiment/sycophancy while touching <6% of parameters. Relevance: **neutral/adjacent** — a supervised steering-method improvement (contrastive data, label-informed); no autonomous-steering claim.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31122
- **arXiv:2609.30405 — "Adaptive Multi-Value Control in LLMs via Causal Activation Steering"** (AIMES; Bhattacharjee, Tandon; submitted 2026-09-24, v1): adaptive multi-value steering with intermediate-layer vocabulary readouts as online observers; "multi-value controllability varies across both value combinations and intervention locations" — a variability-of-control observation in the same family as SCBI's boundary theme. Relevance: **neutral/adjacent** — optimizer/observer-informed, supervised values; no label-free claim.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30405
- **arXiv:2609.27593 — "Hidden not Deleted: How Networks Suppress Entangled Features"** (Samanta, Singh, Chaudhuri; submitted 2026-09-23, v1): linear concept erasure assumes separable subspaces; under dense superposition, erasure destroys both features of an antipodal pair; networks converge to "mirror"/"shadow" circuit solutions that leave the erased feature recoverable via a single scalar patch. Relevance: **adjacent/neutral** — superposition geometry, not steering; consistent with SCBI's entanglement caution, no claim interaction.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27593

## 2. Caught up (submitted before the window, missed by LOG-4499's queries — labeled honestly)

- **arXiv:2609.25366 — "From Decorative to Load-Bearing: Task Difficulty Shapes the Causal Role of Chain-of-Thought"** (Jia, Mu; submitted 2026-09-21; **accepted to TMLR, September 2026**): "Linear probes on hidden states separate silent bypass, self-correction, and error propagation, but additive activation steering provides limited causal control, flipping only about 25% of error-propagation cases at best. Behavioral mode is readable but not reliably controllable." **SIGNIFICANT: peer-reviewed converging evidence for the Decodability Gap** — readability ≠ controllability, from a published venue. Affected decision: v84 related work.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25366
- **arXiv:2609.21662 — "When Steering Fails in Latent Reasoning: A Latent-to-Language Transition Gap"** (Huang, Qi; submitted 2026-09-18, v1): task information remains identifiable in continuous thoughts, yet steering continuous thoughts moves language generation far less than steering explicit CoT — "an intervention effect in latent space fails to transfer to language generation." Another decode≠steer instance, this time at the latent/language interface. Relevance: **adjacent converging evidence** (neutral-to-supportive).
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21662
- **arXiv:2609.22850v2 — "Same Outcome, Different Readout: What Does a Steerable Valence Direction in LLMs Represent?"** (Li et al.; v2 2026-09-22): "Decodability and successful activation steering do not, by themselves, establish what an internal direction represents." Relevance: **adjacent** — the decode-vs-representation gap, same theme family.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22850
