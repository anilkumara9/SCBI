## 2. Steering / activation engineering / mechanistic interpretability

- **arXiv:2609.06951 — "Steering Interference Reflects the Model's Defaults, Not the Behavior Directions"** (Malla, Choi, Choi; v3 2026-09-25): across 24 behaviors × 10 instruction-tuned models, steering interference is decided by the model, not the steered behavior — a steer relaxes the model toward behaviors it already favors (refusal, sycophancy, poeticism). Key line: **"all 24 behaviors are linearly decodable, but only 20 change what the model writes."** A content-free direction matched only in vector norm moves the same behaviors in the same order as real steers. **This is direct external replication of SCBI's core thesis** (EXP092 L11 decode success vs EXP104 HOLD: information presence ≠ routable decision influence; "decodes at 6.7× chance; steers zero decisions").
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06951v1
- **arXiv:2609.07037 — "Disentangling Steering Vectors"** (Hiramatsu et al., submitted 2026-09-07): trains a dedicated SAE on instance-level steering vectors to split composite difference-in-means directions into semantically consistent basis vectors with mutually distinguishable steering effects. A constructive response to the entanglement problem — adjacent to SCBI's per-item geometry work.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07037v1
- **arXiv:2609.14151 — "Signatures of Steerability in Activation Space"** (Bhattarai, Alhanai, 2026-09-12): simple separation metrics strongly correlate with downstream steerability across settings, even after controlling for layers/datasets — practical diagnostics for *when* steering vectors are likely to work. Same family as SCBI's LOG-4338 degeneracy/constant-data gate: a feasibility precondition, not a steering mechanism.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14151v1
- **arXiv:2609.30218 — "Minimally Invasive Steering of Language Models"** (Entesari et al., 2026-09-24): MISVO — pre-logit steering optimized against the local Fisher-KL geometry of the frozen model's token distribution; analytic gradient via the frozen LM head; best mean reward in 6/7 model–task settings (1B–14B models). Note: reward-optimization of a steering vector is **not** autonomous steering in SCBI's sense (it is test-time reward optimization, a label/optimizer-informed method) — file under adjacent, not competing.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30218v1

## So what for SCBI

The single most SCBI-relevant finding is arXiv:2609.06951 (Steering Interference Reflects the Model's Defaults): an independent group, on 24 behaviors × 10 models, landed on SCBI's exact boundary claim — linear decodability does not imply steerability, and interference is a property of the model, not the direction. This strengthens the v83 paper's central sentence ("decodes at 6.7× chance; steers zero decisions") from a single-lab result toward a replicated field observation, and it belongs in the paper's related-work section as converging evidence. Nothing in this sweep threatens an SCBI claim or licenses new work: the adjacent steering papers (disentanglement via SAE, separability diagnostics, MISVO) are all label/optimizer-informed or feasibility-gated methods — none claims autonomous steering, and the scoreboard's "label-free capability race tied at zero" headline survives the window unchanged. The stale OpenAI open-weights re-report is a reminder to date-verify every "new release" before it touches the scoreboard.
