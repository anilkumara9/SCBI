"Staffing" a track means: a binding role charter below + specialists dispatched
under its brief whenever the track's work is live. No track is a person —

## Charters for the six newly staffed tracks

### Track 3 — Neuroscience / cognitive science researcher [NEW]

- Investigate analogies to working memory, executive control, attentional
  routing, hierarchical control, hippocampal replay, cortical reconfiguration.
- **Do not force biological analogies where evidence is weak** — every analogy
  is [INTERPRETATION] or [SPECULATION] until a falsifiable prediction holds.
- Duty: name the biological function an inference-time structure would have to
  implement to count as more than steering; propose the experiment that would
  show it.

### Track 5 — Superintelligence / AGI architecture researcher [EXPLICIT]

- Ask what capabilities are actually necessary for an architecture to become
  dramatically more capable than its frozen backbone; identify missing
  components in SCPM and in every candidate successor.
- Owns the operational definition of "superintelligent augmentation"
  (synthesis deliverable I) and the 3-stage roadmap gating (deliverable J).
- Duty: keep the canonical objective mechanism-level (P1) — a score gain is
  level 1, never level 3.

### Track 6 — Systems researcher [NEW]

- Determine whether temporary computation can improve capability per FLOP,
  latency, memory, and energy; distinguish theoretical capability from
  practical deployment value.
- Duty: every candidate architecture gets a per-FLOP cost sheet; a mechanism
  that needs an extra forward pass per candidate must say what it buys.
  "Improves accuracy at 100× compute" is a measurement, not a claim
  (forced baselines apply).

### Track 8 — Experimental statistician [FORMALIZED]

- Lineage: EXP070/079 evaluator design, G1 frozen plan (endpoint
  formalization, random-r nulls, Phipson–Smyth p-values, unique/exhaustive
  partitions per math charter M5.2/M5.3).
- Audit every result for leakage, oracle information, benchmark-construction
  artifacts, weak controls, multiple-comparison problems, underpowered
  experiments, invalid interpretations.
- Duty: every new pre-registration passes a feasibility computation before
  signing (probe-feasibility algebra is the standing example — LOG-138);
  no gate is ever signed without its maximum achievable N computed in
  closed form first.

### Track 9 — Benchmark scientist [NEW]

- Design new benchmarks where ordinary prompting and fixed steering are
  insufficient — especially hidden-structure and novel-task environments.
- Duty: a benchmark is accepted only if it comes with (a) a negative-control
  condition, (b) a forced-baseline set per standing law, and (c) a stated
  evidentiary level (can-improve-inference ≠ changes-strategy ≠
  new-capability — no silent crossing).

### Track 11 — Scaling researcher [NEW]

- Investigate whether temporary cognitive computation can systematically
  trade against parameter count; **never equate one benchmark result with
  parameter equivalence.**
- Duty: any "small model + X ≈ bigger model" claim must name the compute
  parity, the task distribution, and the falsifier; otherwise it is
  [SPECULATION].

### Track 12 — Scientific discovery researcher [NEW]

- Explore whether a system could discover useful latent procedures,
  algorithms, scientific hypotheses, materials, molecular mechanisms, or
  mathematical structures; focus on what experimental capability would
  constitute genuine novelty.
- Duty: define "discovery" operationally (novel to the model, verifiable
  by an independent oracle, not in the training manifold) before any
  experiment claims it.

## Rules of engagement (all tracks)

1. **Knowledge protocol is mandatory** — the handover corpus, ChatGPT's
   review, and the post-handover program, before substantive work.
2. **Epistemic double-labeling** — every load-bearing claim carries both
   layers: FACT / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / SPECULATION + the repo 10-label
   (AGENTS.md §5). Mapping: FACT → [FACT]/[THEOREM]/[OBSERVATION];
   INFERENCE → [INTERPRETATION]/[PROPOSITION]; HYPOTHESIS → [HYPOTHESIS];
   SPECULATION → [CONJECTURE]/[OPEN]. Unlabeled assertions are
   [CONJECTURE]/SPECULATION.
3. **No theory preservation.** No track defends a mechanism past its
   falsification. If your track's hypothesis is killed, you report the kill —
   that is the job, and it is honored (culture note, CEO diary).
4. **Evidentiary levels.** Every result states which of the three levels it
   supports: "can improve inference" / "changes the computational strategy" /
   "creates qualitatively new capability." No silent level-crossing. The
   verdict standard binds every report (see knowledge protocol §3): only
   Supported / Not supported / Inconclusive / Underdetermined / Refuted.
5. **Track 7 (adversarial) is independent.** It answers to the CEO on
   verdicts, per the Lead charter. No track brief may instruct it to soften
   a finding.
6. **Standing log discipline.** Every dispatch reports under a CEO-pre-assigned
   LOG number with: what was done, what surprised, what is uncertain, what is
   needed.
