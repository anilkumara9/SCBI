# Track-7 Independent Review Checklist — the double bar

*Canonical 2026-09-23, LOG-218 (top-lab amendment). Every Law #14 review
grades BOTH bars. A proposal that clears rigor but fails ambition is sent
back with specific instructions to swing bigger — the reviewer names what
belief-change would license a re-review.*

Reviews are adversarial, not editorial: the reviewer's job is to falsify
the proposal before the world does, and the verdict binds the program.

## The RIGOR bar (unchanged practice, now canonical)

- **R1 — Pre-registration integrity.** Bars pre-registered, non-vacuous,
  kill criteria bite (reviewer recomputes the arithmetic). No silent
  hypothesis shift (Law #4); design changes take a new number.
  *Flatness-bar lint (LOG-228):* every "demonstrably flat" / no-effect
  conjunct must be one-sided with the direction stated, the interval
  family named, and the bite table recomputed from the *named
  implementation* and independently reproduced — bare one-sided wording
  does not prevent name/number divergence (D1 class defect).
  *Bite-table-diff discipline (LOG-229):* any revision touching an
  interval bar must carry canonical (N, s) points recomputed from the
  named implementation, and the re-check diffs the table against the
  bundle's implementation AND an independent reproduction before SIGN —
  self-consistency (bundle testing text against its own code) is not
  independent reproduction.
- **R2 — Mathematics.** Every lemma, bound, and derivation recomputed
  from primary sources by the reviewer — never trusted from the drafter.
  Assumption inventory complete; each assumption discharged or honestly
  disclosed, and undischarged assumptions cap the license grade (§A.3).
- **R3 — Verdict-table exhaustiveness (M5.2).** Every outcome cell mapped
  to exactly one of the five permitted verdicts (Supported / Not
  supported / Inconclusive / Underdetermined / Refuted). Banned language
  ("interesting", "promising", "elegant", "worth another experiment")
  absent from verdict positions.
- **R4 — Evidence hygiene.** Δθ=0 guards, zero data leakage, controls
  that could have produced the same number, epistemic labels on every
  load-bearing claim (FACT/INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS/SPECULATION + 10-label).
- **R5 — Law #15.** Four answers on record and honest: precise question,
  decision changed (KILL/CONTINUE/PIVOT), cheapest path with pass count,
  mathematical license.
- **R6 — Jurisdiction.** No inherited authority, no smuggled battery, no
  scope overclaim (no deciding §H7, no overriding sibling experiments).

## The AMBITION bar (new, LOG-218)

- **A1 — Belief-change test.** The proposal names the belief it could
  overturn and the observation that would overturn it. The reviewer asks:
  *"could this change what anyone believes?"* If the honest answer is no —
  if the best possible outcome is a footnote — the proposal FAILS and is
  sent back. Merely incremental work does not start.
- **A2 — Steelman.** The proposal contains the strongest counterargument
  to itself, argued at full strength — then answers it or concedes it.
  Reviews GRADE the steelman. A weak steelman fails the review, same as a
  weak license.
- **A3 — First principles.** No argument from authority, analogy, or
  "the literature suggests" without a mechanistic or mathematical
  reduction underneath. "Because X lab does it" is never a reason.
- **A4 — Quantitative prediction.** Direction + magnitude + breaking
  point, in writing, before compute. A hypothesis that cannot state what
  number would prove it wrong is a mood, not a hypothesis.
- **A5 — Non-incrementality.** Corpus search required: the proposal is
  not a minor variant of a dead or live corpus item without a new
  discriminating question. "We never tried α=3" is not a question.

## Kill experiments and the ambition bar

Kill gates (K1, EXP082, the LOG-208 sequence) are not incremental: their
best outcome — a clean kill — decides the program's direction (what the
program believes about the mechanism family; whether K3's bite dissolves;
whether GPU spend is licensed). A falsification that changes the
program's beliefs passes A1. The ambition bar gates what we START next
and what gets re-activated from backlog — never a reason to stop a
signed battery mid-execution (signed artifacts are immutable, Law #13).
