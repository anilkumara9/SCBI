## 0. Binding question block

**Precise question [HYPOTHESIS].** EXP100's L11 mean-gate never fired (0/60 → untestable, LOG-4441) and EXP101's L11 max-gate fired (16/60) and was Refuted (step 6, LOG-4448) — the injection-layer relevance hypothesis is twice-closed. Does the killed L11 task direction move decisions when injection is gated on **readout-adjacent attentional relevance** — injected at L11 only where **mean-over-heads L20 attention** from the final prompt token to the entity span exceeds the pre-registered threshold τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i), computed from the current forward pass? [HYPOTHESIS]

**Decision it changes.** **KILL:** later-layer-gated movement < 6 flips at coverage ≥ 10% with the gated random control sub-threshold → externally-computed attention gating is closed as a rescue family; the conditional license narrows to self-computed gates and beyond. A KILL's evidential weight is the test arm's flatness across the *fired* items (F1, binding LOG-4436, carried). **CONTINUE:** gated flips ≥ 6 AND McNemar-exact (doubling) p < 0.01 AND gated random control flat AND stratum consistency AND coverage ≥ 10% → the first conditional causal signal at L11 under later-layer relevance; the program's next lane is self-conditioned intervention. **PIVOT:** the gate fires on < 10% of items (untestable in this form) or the gated random control moves with the task arm (attentional fragility, nonspecific). All outcomes pre-registered; exactly one will be earned.

**Cheapest falsifying test.** 60 EXP092-B items × 3 arms (B, gated-T, gated-R under the identical live L20 mean-gate) = 180 passes maximum (unfired items skip the injection passes by deterministic copy — §2.1), +60 reserve → 240 expected, 500 hard cap. CPU, $0. White-box forward pass with read-only attention hooks on the B pass only (EXP100/EXP101 settled preflight: SDPA emits no weights; block-input pre-hooks; attention math recomputed offline from the model's own submodules; within-backend eager bit-exactness proof required pre-run for the L20 attention math); frozen weights; Δθ=0.

**Quantitative prediction (pre-registered bars).** Gated-T-vs-B flips ≥ 6 AND McNemar-exact (doubling) p < 0.01 AND gated-R flat (< 6 flips AND |ΔM| < 0.10) AND stratum consistency (§2.5) AND gate coverage ≥ 10% → CONTINUE. Gated-T < 6 flips with coverage ≥ 10% and gated-R sub-threshold → KILL. Coverage < 10% → PIVOT (untestable form). Gated-R ≥ 6 → PIVOT (fragility), regardless of the task arm.

**Breaking point (sharp, four levels).** (i) Gate: coverage < 10% → the design declares itself untestable (PIVOT), never KILL — a KILL is never manufactured from a gate that fires nowhere. (ii) Strength: the single pre-registered ratio is 0.38 (α=8.0, realized 0.3792 per EXP096); above it the question becomes S6-1's super-license territory, not this design's — no grid, no interpolation. (iii) Degeneracy: the L11 direction set is frozen (EXP094's constructor); if the constructor re-hash mismatches or the regenerated degeneracy signature deviates from 0.7923 by > 0.05 → HOLD (the pipeline changed). (iv) **No more externally-computed attention gates:** S6-3c is the last externally-computed attention-gate variant licensed in this sequence (mean-at-L11 → max-at-L11 → mean-at-L20). If the L20 mean-gate collapses (<10%), the next move is one of: max-at-L20 (the named S6-3d candidate), a new threshold family (F5 re-opens), or self-computed gates — each its own pre-registration and Law #14 review. A fourth externally-computed attention-gate tweak is pre-emptively unlicensed by this draft. A KILL here closes externally-computed attention gating as a rescue family.

**The belief it tests (named):** "the direction is real but the relevance read was at the wrong layer" — overturned by a later-layer-gated signal (the program goes self-conditioned), deepened by a later-layer-gated KILL (the conditional license narrows to self-computed gates and beyond). [INTERPRETATION]

### 0.1 Why this is not "secretly S6-3/S6-3b" (the distinction Law #14 must confirm)

S6-3/S6-3b operationalized relevance at the **injection layer** (L11): mean- and max-over-heads attention from the final prompt token to the entity span. S6-3c operationalizes relevance at a **readout-adjacent layer** (L20) while injecting at the killed layer (L11). The gate's *source* changes and the relevance claim's *locus* changes: S6-3c tests whether the model's later, decision-shaping attention state is the right relevance signal for the same killed direction — a genuinely different mechanism claim, not a re-aggregation. The threshold constant is byte-identical (2×); what changed is the read layer, i.e. *where* relevance is measured. A KILL here does not re-kill S6-3b's gate (refuted at L11) — it kills the later-layer-read variant. A CONTINUE is a different, readout-adjacent claim, not a resurrection of the injection-layer gates. The injected direction, injection layer, ratio, and bench are deliberately the *killed* ones, as in S6-3/S6-3b. [INTERPRETATION]

The LOG-4407 bar is re-cleared exactly as S6-3/S6-3b cleared it: the bar reserves static per-item L11 experiments; the L20 mean-gate is a live function of the current forward pass's activations (computed from the B pass via read-only hooks), inside the "attention-state-gated, dynamic" carve-out. The read layer is not the injection layer — the carve-out operates at the gating-mechanism level, which is dynamic here. [INTERPRETATION]

### 0.2 The layer choice (stated for Law #14 to attack)

**Chosen: L20, mean-over-heads, τ = 2·(E_i/N_i) unchanged.** Three independent legs:

1. **License-bearing [OBSERVATION]:** L20 is the only later layer with a program record of causal movement — EXP098's L20-only 6 flips (b=0, c=6, p=0.03125, sub-bar observation, LOG-4423); EXP099's L20 fragility finding (L20 6 vs W20 7, control-outmoves-task, LOG-4434). No other later layer has any movement record. The choice is from the license record, not a guess.
2. **Readout-adjacency without terminality [INTERPRETATION]:** L20 sits 4 blocks below the final block — close enough that its attention plausibly shapes the readout distribution, far enough that it is not the terminal decision read. The falsification-first question is whether relevance *anywhere decision-shaping* rescues the direction.
3. **Conservative-first ordering [INTERPRETATION]:** in a NEW hypothesis space (later-layer read), the conservative operationalization (mean) is tried first — Law #14's LOG-4443 ordering principle (conservative mean first, permissive max second; the S6-3→S6-3b order was endorsed as "disciplined exhaustion").

**Rejected alternatives:** **L23 (final block)** — rejected: maximal readout proximity maximizes the Law #7 leakage attack (risk #1); the final block's attention is dominated by the decision-position read, making "entity relevance" nearly a decision proxy. **L12–L19** — rejected: no license-bearing evidence at any of them; choosing one would be a guess, not a licensed choice. **Max-at-L20** — rejected for this wave: the permissive aggregation in the new space; named as the S6-3d candidate if the L20 mean-gate collapses, not smuggled into this design. **Later-layer read + new threshold family** — rejected: re-opens the F5 principledness fight with no warrant while the closed-form family is untested at the new layer.

**The no-peeking bright line (binding on this lane).** No S6-3c parameter is a function of any measurement from EXP094–101: the threshold is byte-identical to the adjudicated constant; the layer is chosen from the program's license record (EXP098/099 movement observations), not from any attention measurement — **no L20 attention masses have been observed anywhere in this program's record.** The EXP098 L20 observation is a *movement* observation, not a gate-calibration measurement; no tuning surface exists. Law #14 should verify this distinction explicitly. [INTERPRETATION]

**F3 prose discipline (updated for the new layer).** The read is "later-layer (L20) attention from the final prompt token" — never "readout query," never "decision attention." The self-consistency rationale (gate at the injection layer) is explicitly dropped and replaced: the new rationale is readout-adjacent relevance — if attentional relevance rescues the direction anywhere, it is in the layers whose attention most directly shapes the readout distribution. This is the honest rationale change; Law #14 should confirm it is not verbal.

### 0.3 The gate-determinism note (no S6-4-style obstruction here)

[FACT] The L20 mean-gate is a deterministic function of the frozen baseline pass (frozen weights Δθ=0 + frozen items + pinned environment + read-only hooks): same item → same mass_i^{L20,mean} → same firing decision, every re-run. No L20-mean-gated run has ever been executed, so the first execution is the experiment, not a replication — there is no prior observation to be circular about. (The obstruction would bite only if a *second* L20-mean-gated run on the same bench were later framed as a "confirmation" — that framing is pre-emptively disallowed here.) [INTERPRETATION]

## 2. Design (frozen model, Δθ = 0; ratio 0.38; L20-mean-gated attention injection at L11)

### 2.1 Arms (pre-registered)

| Arm | Injection | Gate | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | none (read-only attention hooks on L20 block inputs) | — | baseline — single run per item; the gate is computed from this pass |
| gated-T | d_i^(11) @ 0.38·‖h_i^(11,B)‖₂, iff L20 mean-gate fires | live (§2.2) | **discovery test** — the later-layer-relevance causal question |
| gated-R | g_i^(11), norm-tied per fired item to gated-T's δ, iff L20 mean-gate fires (identical live gate) | live (§2.2) | fragility discriminant + liveness instrument |

**Unfired items (pre-registered):** on items where the L20 mean-gate does not fire, the injection passes are **skipped** and the arm decisions are B's decisions by deterministic copy (identical input + identical frozen weights → identical output; the run log asserts the firing vector and the copies). This is exact, not an approximation. Flips can therefore only arise on fired items.

**{d_i^(11)} (frozen reuse).** EXP094's frozen per-item L11 task-contrast directions; constructor code hash-pinned `5e03f206dd907b52baa1ab6d5c9954c709d1b47d143c83f9aa85caa81917f361`; pre-run re-hash, mismatch → HOLD. Functional pin: regenerated degeneracy signature must reproduce 0.7923 within ±0.05, else HOLD. Anchored to **baseline** L11 norms ‖h_i^(11,B)‖₂ (never the perturbed norm). The injection layer is unchanged — only the gate's read layer moved.

**{g_i^(11)} (fresh random control).** Per-item Gaussian directions, normalized and **norm-tied per fired item** to gated-T (‖g_i^(11)‖ == ‖δ_i^(11)‖ — assertions on all fired rows; the EXP096 double-scale bug class). Fresh pre-registered seed, distinct from every prior R stream including EXP101's (311019) — no numeric value appears in this draft (the S6-3b convention Law #14 accepted).

**Single-B design (frozen choice).** B is byte-identical across arms — 60 B + ≤120 injection = **180 passes maximum**. The attention read is hook overhead on the B pass (no extra passes).

### 2.2 The L20 mean-gate (pre-registered, §0.2)

For item i: N_i = prompt tokens, E_i = entity-span tokens (bench annotation; step 0 asserts 1 ≤ E_i < N_i, E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5, and span excludes the final token, for all 60 items). Let A^{(20,h)}_i[q_i → t] be the L20 attention weight from the final prompt token q_i to span token t in head h (recomputed offline from the B pass's captured L20 block inputs through the model's own submodules — EXP100/101's settled preflight: SDPA emits no weights; the within-backend eager bit-exactness proof is required pre-run for the L20 attention math, exactly as LOG-4441/4448 audited). mass_i^{L20,mean} = (1/H) Σ_h Σ_{t ∈ span_i} A^{(20,h)}_i[q_i → t] — the **readout-adjacent** relevance signal. τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i) — **unchanged from S6-3/S6-3b**. Gate fires_i ⟺ mass_i^{L20,mean} ≥ τ_i. Coverage = (#fired)/60. The gate derivation is frozen: no outcome-adaptive tuning exists in this design (there is no tuning surface — τ is closed-form and the read layer is a discrete pre-registered choice).

**F3 prose discipline (updated, §0.2).** The read is "later-layer (L20) attention from the final prompt token" — never "readout query," never "decision attention." The self-consistency rationale (gate at the injection layer) is explicitly dropped, replaced by readout-adjacent relevance.

**No extra audit columns (deliberate).** Unlike S6-3b's mean-field audit column, S6-3c persists **no** extra attention aggregations — the gate's own mass only. The L11-max comparability counterfactual is constructible from EXP101's bundle; re-persisting it would re-open the S6-3b F2 attack surface for no licensed use. No verdict, worksheet cell, or report may cite any non-gate attention quantity.

**Non-perturbation proof (pre-registered, carried):** the hooks are read-only observers on the B pass only (now capturing L20 block inputs); the empirical proof is step 0's B-agreement gate (60/60 vs the archived EXP094 B vector, produced by unhooked passes). Any hook-induced perturbation → HOLD, never a verdict. EXP100/101's bit-exactness proof procedure is the required pre-run instrumentation check for the L20 attention math.

### 2.5 Statistics (pre-registered)

- **McNemar exact, doubling convention** (EXP094's convention; b = wrong→right, c = right→wrong) on paired decisions: gated-T-vs-B **[discovery]**, gated-R-vs-B, gated-T-vs-gated-R [diagnostic-only — Law #9 hygiene]. All contrasts on the full 60-item paired set (unfired items contribute concordant pairs — correct: no injection, no possible flip).
- **ΔM** per EXP093's paired logit-margin convention; **Tango 95% CIs** on paired differences (descriptive).

- **Stratum consistency [DEFINITION, carried from S6-4 v2 §2.4]:** let s_agg = sign(c − b) for the aggregate contrast. For each stratum s ∈ {A-first, C-first} with (b_s, c_s): consistent iff sign(c_s − b_s) = s_agg, or (b_s + c_s) < 2 (uninformative — not a contradiction). A stratum with ≥2 flips in the opposite direction fails consistency → the design cannot CONTINUE (routes to otherwise-PIVOT).
- **Correctness:** margin > 0 (strict), EXP094's convention.
- **Coverage tripwire:** coverage ≥ 10% (≥6/60 fired) → testable; < 10% → PIVOT (untestable form — §3 step 2b). (EXP096 §2.4 pattern; EXP100's step-2b precedent.)
- **Liveness instrument rule:** (gated-T flips ≥ 1) OR (gated-R flips ≥ 1) → regime is LIVE → evaluate. Both 0 with coverage ≥ 10% → FROZEN → Underdetermined (no verdict licensed). Asymmetric rule carried: a moving test arm routes LIVE.
- **Sanity gates:** zero-variance decisions on any injection arm → RUN-INVALID for that arm's contrasts; ties/60 > 5% on any arm → RUN-INVALID for that arm.

### 4.1 Degeneracy analysis of every diagnostic (LOG-4338 lesson — constant-data unit tests, pre-registered)

Each diagnostic states what it returns on degenerate input. A diagnostic that can manufacture a positive from degenerate input is disqualified; all below fail safe:

1. **McNemar exact (doubling).** Constant input (b = c = 0): p = 1.0000 exactly → fails safe toward KILL, **cannot** manufacture CONTINUE. Adversarial input (b=60, c=0): p ≈ 1.73×10⁻¹⁸.
2. **ΔM paired difference.** Zero-variance input → ΔM = 0 exactly → cannot clear DELTA_BAR = 0.10 → fails safe.
3. **Flip-rate.** No flips → 0. Safe.
4. **The liveness instrument (gated-T ≥ 1 OR gated-R ≥ 1) — the dangerous direction.** (a) Truly-frozen regime: all arms 0 → FROZEN → Underdetermined — correct, never KILL. (b) Noise-unlocked regime: a single control noise flip with test arm 0 → LIVE → step-6 KILL fires (coverage ≥ 10% required — a noise-unlocked regime with a collapsed gate routes to step-2b PIVOT, not KILL). **Disclosed asymmetry** (S6-4 §4.1-item-4 carried): the ≥1 bar is deliberately weak to avoid manufacturing Underdetermined from freeze-ambiguity; the KILL's weight is the test arm's flatness across the *fired* items, not the bar's height. (F1)
5. **The coverage tripwire — the load-bearing analysis, re-verified for the L20 read.** Degenerate input (uniform attention at L20 everywhere): mass_i^{L20,mean} = E_i/N_i < 2·E_i/N_i = τ_i for all i → coverage 0% → step 2b PIVOT (untestable) — fails safe, **never** manufactures CONTINUE or KILL. (The L20 mean-gate's degenerate behavior is layer-independent: uniform attention makes every head's span-mass equal E_i/N_i.) Adversarial input (τ tuned to fire everywhere): coverage 100% → step-2c vacuous-gate flag — the design reduces to a static test, but the licensed positive still requires the full step-5 conjunct (≥6 flips, p<0.01, control flat, strata consistent); the gate alone cannot manufacture a false positive. A gate that fires nowhere cannot manufacture a KILL (step 2b short-circuits before step 6). The tripwire fails safe in every direction.
6. **The gate-source audit (step 2a).** Degenerate input (executor derives the gate from an injection pass): audit assertion fails → HOLD — fails safe toward diagnosis, never toward a verdict.
7. **The B-agreement hook check (step 0ii).** Degenerate input (hooks perturb activations): B decisions deviate from the archived vector → HOLD. Fails safe toward diagnosis. Adversarial input (executor disables hooks to pass agreement): then no L20 attention masses exist → step 0iii/2a assertions fail → HOLD. The check cannot be passed without a genuine read-only L20 attention read.
8. **gated-R flatness — the dangerous direction (S6-4 §4.1-item-7 carried).** Flatness is a CONTINUE-*enabling* conjunct. True neutralization: (a) a truly frozen regime yields test arm 0 too, so the significance conjunct fails — the automatic neutralization path; (b) control 0 with significant directional test arm routes LIVE, and a CONTINUE there fires correctly — task-geometry-specific movement where matched-norm random injection under the identical L20 mean-gate moves nothing is the *strongest* evidence the gate found. **CONTINUE requires the full conjunction** (significant AND control flat AND strata consistent AND coverage ≥ 10%); no conjunct alone licenses anything.
9. **Stratum consistency (carried definition).** Degenerate input (all flips in one stratum, other stratum 0 flips): the empty stratum is uninformative (<2 flips), not contradictory → consistent. Cannot manufacture inconsistency from lopsided-but-unidirectional data. Adversarial input (flips split against the aggregate sign): fails consistency → blocks CONTINUE → fails safe toward PIVOT, never toward a false positive.

### 4.2 Probe-feasibility + power algebra (EXP079 lesson — proved, not asserted; recomputed by this lane 2026-09-28)

- **McNemar minimum at α = 0.01:** b=0, c=7 → p = 0.015625, n.s.; b=0, c=8 → p = 0.0078125 < 0.01 ✓. The discovery bar needs **≥8 directional flips** (b=0 case); the ≥6-flip movement gate is necessary-but-insufficient. Maximum achievable = 60 ≥ 8. **No structural infeasibility.**
- **CONTINUE-reachability (F1, binding LOG-4436, carried):** the licensed positive requires ≥8 directional flips, and flips can only arise on fired items (unfired items contribute concordant pairs by deterministic copy, §2.1) — so the positive is mathematically unreachable at coverage < 8/60 (13.3%). In the coverage window **[10%, 8/60)**, the only available verdicts are: **KILL** (gated-T < 6, gated-R sub-threshold, LIVE); **PIVOT** (gated-R ≥ 6 — matched-control fragility or control-outmoves-task); **Underdetermined** (FROZEN). A KILL in this window rests on ~6–7 injected items (0/6 → 95% upper bound 39.3% on the gated-conditional flip rate), not on flatness across 60 items. The KILL in the window stays licensed (the alternative — Underdetermined-by-construction in a plausible world — is worse), but its weakness is honestly accounted rather than rhetorically inflated.
- **F2 operating condition re-proved for the L20 read:** mass_i^{L20,mean} is a convex combination of attention weights (each head's weights sum to 1 over all tokens), so mass_i^{L20,mean} ≤ 1 always; τ_i ≤ 1 ⟺ 2·E_i/N_i ≤ 1 ⟺ E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5 — identical to S6-3/S6-3b. The layer change does not move the operating condition. Step 0 asserts E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5 for all 60 items.
- **Single discovery contrast:** gated-T-vs-B at family-wise 0.01 — no Bonferroni (one contrast; stated, not hidden). Controls and diagnostics are not discovery tests — disclosed, not hidden.
- **KILL feasibility:** needs gated-T < 6 at coverage ≥ 10% — satisfiable (the null predicts ~0–3; EXP096's differently-gated T moved 3/21 at this ratio).
- **CONTINUE feasibility:** needs ≥8 directional gated-T flips with the control flat — satisfiable under the hypothesis; demanding by design (the bar does not move).
- **Coverage-tripwire feasibility:** 6/60 = 10% — satisfiable. **Honest note:** L20 mean-attention firing behavior is unobserved anywhere in the program record — the tripwire may fire either way; that is its purpose, not a design defect.
- **Low-coverage asymmetry (disclosed):** at coverage near the 10% floor, the CONTINUE branch demands ≥6 flips among ~6–10 fired items — a high gated-conditional flip rate. The design is therefore **KILL-favoring at low coverage**; the asymmetry is declared here, not discovered later, and it points in the falsification-first direction.
- **Sweep sensitivity floor:** at N=60, the discovery bar (≥8 directional flips) implies a minimum detectable directional flip rate of θ ≈ 0.133. Effects below this are in the blind spot — disclosed, not hidden. **Post-exclusion N:** the count-bars (≥6, ≥8) apply to the post-exclusion paired set (§3 step 1) — the verdict lane recomputes and reports the realized N, the sensitivity floor (8/N_realized), and the coverage-conditioned floor 8/F (F = fired items).
- **Noise specificity:** under a noise flip rate θ_noise = 0.03 (EXP098's L11-only moved 3/60), P(≥6 flips | N=60) = 0.0091 — the movement gate has <1% false-alarm rate against this noise model; P(<6) = 0.9909, so the KILL branch is well-powered against noise.
- **Delivery bands** [0.285, 0.475] per (fired item, arm): feasible by construction (EXP095/096/098/100/101 machinery).
- **Budget:** 180 + 60 = 240 expected ≤ 500 hard cap. **Replication gate** (B = 36/60 per-item agreement): feasible — deterministic CPU forward passes demonstrated across EXP094–101.
- **Conclusion:** every pre-registered bar is reachable by construction. If Law #14's independent recomputation of this algebra disagrees, the run does not proceed.

## 6. Known design risks — what Law #14 should try to break (falsification-first)

Ranked by how badly each would wound the licensed verdicts:

1. **Law #7 readout-proximity (the lead risk).** The gate reads L20 attention — closer to the readout than L11's. **Attack:** L20 attention to the entity span may correlate with the correct answer (the model "knows" the answer and attends to the entity) — gating on it could be a near-decision proxy, in which case a CONTINUE is uninterpretable and a KILL is unsurprising. **This lane's defenses:** (a) the gate is a scalar threshold on entity-span attention mass — it never sees the label, the margin, the decision, or the answer; (b) the identical gate applies to the matched random control, so any answer-correlation advantage accrues symmetrically to both arms — a CONTINUE still requires directional task-arm movement with the control flat; (c) the gate is a deterministic function of the frozen B pass, fixed before any injection pass runs; (d) the bench strata are the bench pin, fixed before inspection. **Honest residual:** the attack is real at the correlation level — this is the risk most likely to wound the design's interpretability. Law #14 must confirm the no-leakage argument explicitly, as it did for S6-3's risk #4 (LOG-4436).
2. **"Readout neighbor" as decision proxy (the most fatal framing).** If the L20 read is nearly a decision proxy, the design tests nothing about *relevance* — it tests whether injecting where the model is "about to be right" moves it. **Attack:** does this make a CONTINUE uninterpretable regardless of §5's conjunction? **Counter:** the gate sees no label/margin/decision; the identical-gate control symmetrizes any proxy advantage; the step-5 conjunction (≥6 directional flips, p<0.01, flat control, strata consistent) still demands task-geometry-specific movement. But the attack is real — Law #14 should rule on whether the L20 read is too close to the decision to license a CONTINUE interpretation, or whether the control structure answers it.
3. **Why L20 and not another later layer.** The license-bearing leg is EXP098's sub-bar L20-only 6-flip observation (LOG-4423). **Attack:** is a sub-bar observation enough of a license for the read layer? **Counter:** it is the only later-layer movement record in the program — the alternative is an unlicensed guess; L23 is rejected for maximal leakage (risk #1) and L12–L19 for having no record at all. Attack whether the L20 choice is principled or convenient.
4. **Why mean and not max at L20.** The conservative-first ordering (Law #14's LOG-4443 principle): mean is tried first in the new hypothesis space. **Attack:** is the ordering principle being used to launder a second predictable collapse — EXP100's L11 mean-gate fired 0/60, and the L11 record "suggests" mean won't fire? **Counter:** the L11 record does not transfer to L20 — attention distributions shift across layers, and L20-mean masses are unobserved anywhere in the record (§0.2 bright line). The tripwire handles a collapse honestly (2b PIVOT); max-at-L20 is the named S6-3d candidate, not smuggled into this design. Attack whether the non-transfer claim holds.
5. **The no-peeking bright line for the layer choice.** No L20 attention masses exist anywhere in the program record; the layer is chosen from the license record (EXP098/099 movement observations). **Attack:** is "chosen from the license record" outcome-blind — EXP098's L20 observation is itself an outcome (6 flips)? **Counter:** it is a *movement* observation, not a gate-calibration measurement — no S6-3c parameter is a function of any attention mass. The license standard is the absence of a tuning surface, not the chooser's ignorance (LOG-4443's binding adjudication). Law #14 should verify the distinction explicitly.
6. **τ-principledness at the new layer.** F5's three conditions (LOG-4436) re-verified for the L20 read: (i) the constant is unchanged and closed-form; (ii) fail-safe miscalibration (too selective → 2b PIVOT, too permissive → 2c flag; the gate alone cannot manufacture the step-5 conjunction); (iii) the verdict never hinges on the constant. The constant is load-bearing for testability, not validity — exactly as adjudicated.
7. **Ungated α=8.0 at L11 was never tested (carried).** If gated-T moves, the design attributes it to gating via the EXP096 comparison (3/21 at ratio 0.3792 under a different gate). **Counter (carried):** adding a fourth ungated arm would change the licensed 3-arm design; it is named here as the cheapest follow-up, not smuggled in. Attack whether the 3-arm design is under-controlled without it.
8. **The low-coverage KILL-favoring asymmetry (§4.2, carried).** At coverage near 10%, CONTINUE demands ≥6 flips among ~6–10 fired items. **Attack:** does this make the CONTINUE branch decorative at low coverage — is the design secretly a KILL machine when the gate is selective? The lane's counter: the asymmetry is declared pre-run and points in the falsification-first direction; a CONTINUE at low coverage would be an extraordinarily strong signal. Attack whether the declared asymmetry is sufficient disclosure or a design defect (same attack as S6-3 risk #3 — the answer had better be the same).
