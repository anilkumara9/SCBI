## TIER 0 — Gates (run first; minutes of compute; de-risk everything behind them)

### G1 — Weight-only QK/OV projection-energy audit of the null-space mechanism [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Experiment-Corpus agent's idea (corpus suggests, never tested).
**Question.** Do the failed relational contrast directions project near-zero energy
into downstream attention heads' QK subspaces while the rescuing bridge projects
large energy — the central prediction of the handover's Core Causal Null-Space
Theorem (THEORY §3.3), which all 66 ledger experiments *assume* but none measured,
and which EXP067's Stage-A halt left unexamined?
**Mechanism (math).** For each head h in layers l+1..L downstream of the L20
injection point: build the QK projector P_QK from [W_Q; W_K] column space
(d×d, d=1024); compute e_QK(v) = ‖P_QK v‖/‖v‖ for
v ∈ {B_agg, v̂_k, mean bridge(x), B_perp, B_wrong, 100 random unit r}.
Repeat for the OV-path projector. Positive control: the bridge (known causal).
Endpoint: e_QK(B_agg) vs e_QK(bridge) against the random-r null (exact permutation
p); pre-register "near-null-space" as e_QK(B_agg) < 5th percentile of random-r
AND e_QK(bridge) > 95th percentile.
**Kill criterion.** e_QK(B_agg) ≥ e_QK(bridge) → the null-space mechanism is dead;
the §3.3 theorem's central sentence is false; the boundary's mechanistic
explanation must be replaced (same audit extends to P_OV and MLP input matrices).
**Cost.** $0 GPU — CPU linear algebra on weights + ~170KB archived vectors;
seconds to minutes. [ESTIMATE] < 0.01% of weekly quota.
**Why first.** Either verdict re-routes Phase 2: confirms → Phase 2 gains its
first direct evidence + a designable objective (maximize QK-projection energy);
kills → the entire QK-subspace operator program (incl. the lit-corpus idea below)
is stood down before another GPU minute is spent on it.
**Roadmap phase:** Phase 2 (Solve the Causal Interchangeability Gap) — as its
missing premise-check. **Novelty:** N1. **Ledger:** zero hits — never run.

## TIER 1 — Capability bets (the actual shots at making the model more capable)

### C-A — Cross-item donor-bridge transfer: does the bridge generalize? [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Experiment agent Class 4 (its top capability pick).
**Question.** Is the bridge's +10–23pp rescue relation-general or item-specific?
Apply a bridge direction built from *labeled donor items* (entity-disjoint, same
relation template) to unlabeled test items — the first test of whether the
program's one working intervention family has transferable structure, and the
path to label-free-at-test-time gains (donor bank from labeled support).
**Design.** C1 baseline · C2 oracle self-bridge (positive control) · C3
same-relation donor-centroid bridge (primary) · C4 different-relation donor
(negative) · C5 random-target donor (content control) · C6 random direction.
Endpoint: McNemar exact, N=60. **Pre-register the entity-similarity-leak
confound** (audit Finding 4: bridge ≈ near-direct logit steering): C3's win is
licensed as transfer only if it survives donor↔test token-overlap stratification;
if "transfer" tracks donor-target ↔ test-option overlap, it is logit steering,
not relational transfer.
**Kill criterion.** ΔM_C3 ≤ 0 (p ≥ 0.05) while C2 rescues → the bridge is
item-specific; the transfer hope for this intervention family is dead.
**Cost.** 2,700 passes ≈ 2 min [ESTIMATE] + 1,440 ≈ 65s layer-sweep companion
(causal locus of the bridge). **Roadmap phase:** Phase 4 (transferable
intervention = autonomous-capability primitive). **Novelty:** N1.
**Ledger:** ADJACENT not killed — EXP058 (LOG-066) killed zero-shot transfer of
*internal-state inferred bases* across disjoint domains (ΔM −6.7pp, p=0.8906),
a different mechanism family and granularity; the pre-reg must cite and
distinguish it.

### C-B — Self-bridge fixed-point iteration (target-free, ≤5 passes) [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Theory C5 ⊃ Experiment Class 1 (same family — merged here; the
experiment agent's top-1-only amplification control is adopted as C5 in the
design).
**Mechanism.** Φ(s;x) = normalize(w_{t̂_1(h+αs)} − w_{t̂_2(h+αs)}), iterate
s_{k+1} = Φ(s_k;x), s_0 = 0, k ≤ 5 or ‖s_{k+1}−s_k‖ < ε. The model's own output
geometry is both search substrate and intervention — a dynamical system in
unembedding-row-difference space. Law #7 clean (outputs only, no labels).
Cost: ≤5 forward passes vs the loop's ~245.
**Why it could win.** If the model's top-2 geometry already encodes the answer
and only needs amplification to decision, no search is needed at all — ~50×
compute reduction over the loop with a strictly more causal substrate.
**Kill criterion.** (i) ΔM ≤ 0 (p ≥ 0.05) → the self-bridge does not amplify to
correct decisions; (ii) informative mechanism verdict: signed ledger c > b →
the bridge's power is *entirely label-derived* — retires a whole mechanism
class; (iii) non-convergence on >50% of items → ill-posed dynamical system.
**Confound control (pre-registered):** a win is licensed as "bridge structure"
only if it beats the top-1-only amplification arm; if top-1-only ≈ fixed-point,
the effect is confidence amplification, not contrastive structure (the
AMPLIFICATION ILLUSION).
**Cost.** 2,880 passes ≈ 2.2 min [ESTIMATE]. **Roadmap phase:** Phase 4 (the
cheapest fully-autonomous target-free adaptation primitive). **Novelty:** N1
(delta: iterated *direction* in unembedding-difference space, not sampled
outputs like Wang et al. self-consistency). **Ledger:** zero hits.

### C-C — Bridge-amplified chain-of-thought (step-local bridges across generation) [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Theory C4.
**Mechanism.** 2-step generation per 2-hop item (step 1: intermediate entity;
step 2: final answer). At each step t, inject the step-local bridge at l*:
h^{(t)}_{l*} ← h^{(t)}_{l*} + α_t b̂^{(t)}, with b̂^{(t)} recomputed per step from
live logits. Closed-loop *across generation steps* — step-1's intervention
changes the generated intermediate, which changes step-2's context. Distinct
from A-LQR's within-step controller and from C1's within-pass positions.
**Why it could win.** Relational reasoning is sequential — hop 1 must resolve
before hop 2 uses it; a single readout tilt cannot fix a wrong hop-1 binding
that hop 2 then compounds. This is the only candidate that intervenes on the
*reasoning trajectory* rather than the final readout — the closest thing here
to a genuine capability (not measurement) gain.
**Sequenced kill (Law #4 compliant).** Label-informed ceiling FIRST:
ΔM_step-cascade ≤ ΔM_single-final-bridge (paired McNemar p ≥ 0.05) while the
single bridge rescues → hop-local repair adds nothing; the target-free
self-bridge variant is NOT attempted. If the ceiling survives, the target-free
variant's kill is ΔM ≤ 0 (p ≥ 0.05). Hop-1 vs hop-2 ablation localizes any gain.
**Most likely secret failure (steelman):** distribution shift — the step-1
intervention breaks step-2's CoT coherence; errors compound (c > b on the signed
ledger). A significant *harm* finding is the informative version of the null.
**Cost.** ~7 conditions × 60 × 2 steps ≈ 840+ passes ≈ <2 min [ESTIMATE].
**Roadmap phase:** Phase 4 (autonomous multi-step self-improvement) with Phase-2
mechanism core. **Novelty:** N1 (delta vs Self-Refine: representation-space
per-step injection, not text-space prompting). **Ledger:** zero mechanism hits.

### M1 — Multi-layer synchronized bridge cascade [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Theory agent's §1 idea (corpus suggests, never tested).
**Question.** Does injecting the per-item output bridge simultaneously at

**Design.** C1 unintervened; C2 bridge at L20 (α=0.5); C3 bridge at {18,20,22}
simultaneously (fresh injection per layer, budget NOT conserved); C4 L18 only;
C5 L22 only; C6 B_wrong multi-layer (discriminant); C7 random multi-layer.
**Kill.** ΔM_C3 ≤ ΔM_C2 (paired p ≥ 0.05) while C2 rescues → no cascade synergy.
**Cost.** 420 passes ≈ <30 min [ESTIMATE]. **Phase:** 2. **Novelty:** N1.
**Ledger delta:** EXP017-A killed only *conserved-budget rank-2 projector*
coordination — different mechanism, era, and budget semantics; cited with delta.

### M2 — Token-compounded bridge cascade (multi-position schedule) [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Theory C1.
**Mechanism.** Inject the bridge at entity-slot + answer positions within one
forward pass at l*: h_{l*,p_j} ← h_{l*,p_j} + α_j b̂(x). Every prior program
intervention was last-token-only; the p_1 injection alters entity-slot
representations that downstream attention then routes — converts the bridge
from readout tilt into *computation repair*.
**Kill.** On the pre-registered bridge-resistant subset: ΔM_multi-pos ≤ 0
(p ≥ 0.05) → the bridge's failures are not positional; globally
ΔM_multi-pos ≤ ΔM_single (paired p ≥ 0.05) → no gain. Signed ledger per
position-subset ablation localizes any effect — including significant *harm*
(entity injection scrambling attention routing), which is information.
**Cost.** ~420 passes [ESTIMATE]. **Phase:** 2. **Novelty:** N1 (orthogonal axis
to M1: multi-position one layer vs multi-layer one position). **Ledger:** zero
hits for position-variant interventions.

### M3 — QK/OV-projection-subspace-restricted alignment (Workstream 2, never executed) [CONJECTURE]

**Source:** Literature-Corpus agent's idea (handover §6.2 Workstream 2).
**Question.** Does a static internal contrast basis (B_agg), aligned *within* the
row-spaces of downstream attention projections instead of the ambient residual
stream, recover nonzero causal rescue on novel vocabularies?
**Mechanism.** U = orth(concat_h [row(W_V^{(h)}), row(W_O^{(h)} W_V^{(h)})]);
(i) projection-restricted injection b_rest = UU^T B_agg (norm-matched);
(ii) subspace-restricted Procrustes R(x) = UṘU^T, Ṙ ∈ O(r) — the rank-2-fit
pathology is structurally avoided because the operator is *defined* in the
low-rank downstream space. Parameter-free, per-instance, label-free, Δθ=0.
**Kill.** b = 0/19 at α ∈ {0.5, 1.0} for both arms while the output bridge
rescues ≥5/19 → the handover's QK/OV-alignment fix is dead for the static basis;
Phase 2 must rely on the adaptive loop, not better static alignment.
**Cost.** ~840 passes on pythia-160m ≈ <1h T4 [ESTIMATE]. **Phase:** 2 (its
mandated first step). **Novelty:** N1. **Ledger:** distinct from EXP017
(different substrate) and sprint-1 Idea 7 (bridge-into-S, not
basis-into-downstream-subspace).
**SEQUENCING:** runs only after G1 — if the weight audit kills the null-space
mechanism, this experiment's premise dies with it.

## The kill list (what died in this sprint and why)

1. **Per-token B_agg compounding** (Experiment Class 2 as specified) — KILLED.
   The 0×K=0 argument is decisive: compounding multiplies a per-step effect, and
   the measured per-step effect of the v̂ family is ~0. Boundary science on an
   exhausted family. The *compounding* idea survives only in C-C's bridge form.
2. **Any new hidden-state candidate search/selection before EXP079 re-measures
   the ceiling** — KILLED (standing DOA ruling, Experiment agent). The v̂ family
   is exhausted (EXP065/066/077/078 nulls; EXP070 oracle ΔM=+0.00pp over that
   pool, probe uninformative). EXP068 stays unbuilt.
3. **Margin-shift / confidence as endpoint or unvalidated evaluator objective** —
   KILLED (Law #9; B_wrong p=1.2e-08 with b=c=0). Pre-register a confidence-oracle
   control for any future evaluator.
4. **Label-informed bridge presented as a method** — KILLED as a proposal class
   (Law #7); it remains a positive control / ceiling probe only.
5. **Naive self-critique loops** — KILLED by the 2026 literature (AVI: naive
   self-correction degrades 0.712→0.697). Any loop proposal must name its error
   signal and beat AVI-style veracity, not a strawman.
6. **"Online per-instance optimization" as a novelty claim in general** — KILLED
   by ∇-Reasoner (ICLR 2026). Our residual claim is strictly the *target-free,
   gradient-free, activation-space* variant.
7. **"Per-instance adaptive activation steering" as a novelty claim** — KILLED
   by SVF + STARS (both 2026). Residual: steering-for-capability with a
   target-free evaluator and terminator (the full closed loop), which neither has.
