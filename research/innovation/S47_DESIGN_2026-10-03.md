## 1. Exact question

**[HYPOTHESIS]** On the 60-item decision bench, does the signal-eigenvalue count of the residual-stream covariance — eigenvalues outside the Marchenko–Pastur bulk — differ between correct and failed items, with correct items showing more signal eigenvalues?

### Operational definition of the observable

**[DEFINITION]** For item i, layer l (l = 1…24, Pythia-410m), let X_{i,l} ∈ ℝ^{n_i×d} be the residual-stream activations over the n_i input positions (d = 1024), centered per dimension. Form the sample covariance Σ_{i,l} = X_{i,l}^T X_{i,l} / n_i (equivalently the n_i×n_i Gram — same nonzero spectrum; the pre-reg pins the cheaper construction).

**Marchenko–Pastur null [THEOREM as mathematics].** For a noise matrix with iid entries, the empirical spectral distribution of the sample covariance converges to the MP law with aspect ratio γ = d/n_i: bulk supported on [λ_−, λ_+] with λ_± = σ²(1 ± √γ)² (plus an atom at 0 when γ > 1, which holds here since n_i < d typically). **[INTERPRETATION]** Eigenvalues strictly above λ_+ are *signal* — directions of coherent low-rank structure the noise model cannot explain.

**Signal-eigenvalue count [DEFINITION]:** s_{i,l} := #{eigenvalues of Σ_{i,l} > λ_+(γ_i, σ̂²_{i,l})}, where σ̂²_{i,l} is the noise-variance estimate (estimation method pinned in pre-reg — e.g., σ̂² fit from the bulk median against the theoretical MP median at γ_i; the design requires the method to be fixed before any data is seen).

**Per-item statistic [DEFINITION]:** s_i := max_l s_{i,l} — the maximum signal-eigenvalue count over layers. This directly operationalizes the backlog's Law #15 prediction (medians of signal counts). The J3 sketch's transition-layer l_c (first layer where the count jumps by ≥3 sustained 2 layers) is a pre-registered *secondary* diagnostic.

**Grouping:** items are correct/failed per the baseline's answers on the 60-item bench (baseline = the committed EXP140 B0 greedy records, reused exactly as in EXP141; correctness from bench labels — see §5 for the Law #7 statement).

**Why this is a different object from the four dead signals:**
- vs the margin (TESTA): the margin is *decision-local* — top-2 logit gap at the answer token in the decision pass. The spectral observable is *computation-global* — covariance structure of the residual stream over all positions, across all layers. It measures the coherence of the computation, not the sharpness of the decision.
- vs free energy (EXP140): F is trajectory-integrated surprise density over a *generated rationale*. The spectral observable needs no rationale — it reads the residual stream of the decision pass itself.
- vs P(True) self-evaluation (EXP141): â_self is a *metacognitive judgment* in a second forward pass. The spectral observable is a *physical measurement* of the first pass — no judgment, no second pass, no elicitation.
- vs verbalized/honesty self-report (EXP125): no self-report of any kind.

**[INTERPRETATION — the honest limit]** All four distinctions are structural, but the observable is still computed from the same frozen model on the same bench family. The dangerous possibility: TESTA's position bias *is itself* a coherent low-rank component — a biased policy is, after all, a consistent direction. If so, failed items (where the bias struck) could show *more* signal eigenvalues, and the licensed direction (correct > failed) fails. The permutation test in the licensed direction is the breaking instrument for exactly this: a wrong-direction effect yields one-sided p ≈ 1 → KILL. The design does not assume the bias is incoherent; it bets the bet and names the instrument that collects.

## 4. Mathematical license

### 4.1 The MP null and the coherence-emergence conjecture

**[THEOREM as mathematics]** The Marchenko–Pastur law: for X ∈ ℝ^{n×d} with iid entries of variance σ², as n,d → ∞ with d/n → γ, the empirical spectral distribution of XX^T/n converges almost surely to the MP distribution — bulk on [σ²(1−√γ)², σ²(1+√γ)²], with an atom at 0 of mass 1−1/γ when γ > 1. Eigenvalues outside the bulk are, under the null, impossible — their presence rejects pure noise.

**[CONJECTURE as mechanism]** Correct reasoning = emergence of a coherent low-rank component in the residual stream. When the model "settles" into a coherent computation — the pieces of the item locking into a consistent representation — a few directions carry most of the variance, and the spectrum shows outliers above the MP edge. Failed items, where the computation never coheres (the model waffles between framings, or the bias fires without integrating the item's evidence), stay closer to the noise bulk. This is the *phase-transition* framing: coherence as an order parameter with a sharp onset at some layer l_c.

**[INTERPRETATION]** The MP null is a *counting rule*, not a true data-generating model — activations are not iid noise, so the bulk will never match MP exactly and there will always be some outliers (attention structure, positional encodings, the residual stream's own anisotropy). The design is explicit about this: the falsification does not come from "is MP exactly right" but from the *label permutation test* — do correct items show more outliers than failed items, beyond what the grouping explains? The MP construction gives a principled, pre-registered counting rule; the permutation test is the statistical instrument.

### 4.2 Why the spectral observable might survive TESTA's inversion — head-on

**[INTERPRETATION]** TESTA's mechanism: position bias makes the model lean first-listed; the margin (decision-local sharpness) is calibrated to the biased *decision policy*. Three reasons the spectral observable is a different bet:
1. **Coherence, not sharpness.** The margin measures how peaked the final decision is. The spectrum measures whether the *computation leading there* was low-rank-coherent. A confidently-wrong decision (high margin, wrong answer) can sit atop either a coherent or an incoherent residual stream — the observable is orthogonal to the margin's axis by construction.
2. **Global, not local.** The margin lives at one token position in one layer. s_i integrates over all positions and (via the max) all layers. A position bias that corrupts one decision token need not reorganize the whole stream's covariance.
3. **Unsupervised by construction.** The observable is computed with no reference to the decision at all — it cannot "track the decision policy" the way the margin does, because it never sees the decision.

**[INTERPRETATION — the honest limit]** Reason 3 cuts both ways: an observable blind to the decision is also blind to correctness — the bet is precisely that coherence *correlates* with correctness anyway. And the sharpest counter-argument is stated in §1: the bias itself may be the coherent component, in which case failed items show *more* signal and the licensed direction fails. The permutation test in the licensed direction collects this bet: wrong-direction significance → one-sided p ≈ 1 → KILL. The license is a [CONJECTURE]; the permutation test is the breaking instrument.

### 4.3 Quantitative prediction and breaking points

**Prediction (the candidate's, from the J3 sketch):** median(s_correct) − median(s_failed) ≥ 2, permutation p < 0.05 one-sided.
**Breaking points (binding):** p ≥ 0.05 → KILL the observational claim, intervention never attempted; p < 0.05 but Δ_s < 2 → HELD.

## 5. Calibration/validation design (Law #7 firewall)

**[FACT]** This is a purely observational test: there is no candidate score, no selection, no trigger, no revision, no ranking. Bench labels define the correct/failed groups in the primary permutation test — that *is* the validation. The Law #7 firewall is trivially intact because there is nothing labels could leak into beyond the pre-registered grouping.

**The validation IS the experiment.** Unlike S4-5 (whose free-energy signal needed a separate G3 calibration gate before any selection claim) and S4-6 (whose â_self needed G2/G3 before any revision claim), S4-7's observable makes no downstream claim on its own authority — its *only* claim is the group difference, which is tested directly against labels in the primary analysis. There is no separate calibration gate because there is no separate use of the signal. The permutation test is the named breaking instrument (§1, §4.2).

**Pre-registered validation procedure (Stage-1 CPU pilot, one-sided in the licensed direction — correct > failed):**
- **V1 (primary):** permutation test on Δ_s = median(s_correct) − median(s_failed), 100,000 permutations, seed pinned in pre-reg, bar p < 0.05.
- **V2 (robustness):** the same test on the mean difference (sensitive to tail items); and a per-layer version (which layers carry the difference, if any) — reported, not binding.
- **V3 (l_c diagnostic):** the J3 transition-layer rule (≥3 new outliers sustained 2 layers) — distribution of l_c existence/position across groups, reported, not binding.

## 8. Prior art (searched 2026-10-03; arXiv API rate-limited at design time — full-text verification is a pre-reg-stage follow-up)

1. **Martin & Mahoney — RMT analysis of DNN weight spectra [REPORTED, not independently verified this lane].** The random-matrix-theory program for deep learning (WeightWatcher): trained weight matrices show MP bulk + signal outliers; the outlier structure tracks training quality and generalization. **Closest methodological prior art.** Distinctions: (a) their object is *weight* matrices (static, post-training); S4-7's object is *activation* covariances at inference time; (b) they correlate spectra with generalization across models — S4-7 correlates per-item spectra with per-item correctness within one frozen model. The MP-bulk-plus-outliers counting rule is inherited; the grouping question is new.
2. **Neural collapse — Papyan, Han & Donoho (2020) [REPORTED, not independently verified this lane].** Terminal-phase training geometry: class means collapse to a simplex ETF, within-class covariance → 0. **Conceptual prior art** for "coherence has a spectral signature." Distinctions: (a) a *training-time* phenomenon; S4-7 is inference-time; (b) about class structure, not per-item correctness.
3. **Intrinsic dimensionality of representations — Aghajanyan et al. [REPORTED, not independently verified this lane].** Low intrinsic dimension of the fine-tuning parameter manifold. **Adjacent** but distinct: parameter-space vs activation-space, fine-tuning vs inference.

**What was NOT found (honest negative):** no prior art was found applying MP-based signal-eigenvalue counting to per-item *inference-time* activation covariances grouped by correctness. If the pre-reg-stage full-text search finds it, the novelty read updates.

**Novelty read:** likely **N1 (known combination)** — MP spectral analysis is established (Martin & Mahoney), per-item correctness grouping is new. The differentiator is the observational question itself, asked with falsification-first bars on this bench/backbone. The design claims none of the prior art's territory and cites it.

## 9. Risks and honest limitations

1. **Honest prior is against the signal** (§0): four internal-signal failures (TESTA anti-calibration, EXP140 null, EXP141 kill, EXP125 kill) all point the same way. The spectral observable is a genuinely different family, but the prior says internal correctness-signed signals don't exist here.
2. **The MP null is a counting rule, not a true model** (§4.1): activations are not iid noise. The bulk will never match MP exactly; the σ̂² fit absorbs some misspecification, but a bad fit could systematically bias counts. The pre-reg must pin the σ̂² estimator and a bulk-fit diagnostic (reported, not binding) so a fit failure is visible rather than silent.
3. **The bias-coherence counter-argument** (§1, §4.2): the position bias may itself be the coherent low-rank component. The licensed direction could be exactly backwards. The permutation test collects this by killing.
4. **n_i vs d:** with short prompts, n_i may be small (tens of positions), making the covariance estimate noisy and the MP asymptotics shaky. The pre-reg should report the n_i distribution and, if n_i is too small for stable spectra, the pilot reports HELD with reason rather than a spurious KILL — this is a measurement-validity guard, pre-registered.
5. **The intervention sharp edge** (§6.i): amplifying the top-signal-PC is directional manipulation. The observational license does not launder it; the intervention gate must face the killed-lanes audit directly.
6. **Bench specificity:** all bars are on the 60-item decision bench at Pythia-410m. No claim about other benches/backbones is licensed under any branch.
