[HYPOTHESIS]

**Decision it changes.** **KILL:** later-layer-max-gated movement < 6 flips at coverage ≥ 10% with the gated random control sub-threshold → externally-computed attention gating is closed as a rescue family — **the terminal verdict for the whole family**; the conditional license narrows to self-computed gates and beyond. ### 0.1 Why this is not "secretly S6-3/S6-3b/S6-3c" (the distinction Law #14 must confirm)

S6-3/S6-3b operationalized relevance at the **injection layer** (L11): mean- and max-over-heads attention from the final prompt token to the entity span. S6-3c operationalized relevance at a **readout-adjacent layer** (L20, mean). S6-3d operationalizes relevance at the **same new read layer with the permissive aggregation** (L20, max) — the other discrete choice along the aggregation axis of the new hypothesis space. The gate's *source* and the relevance claim's *locus* both moved once (S6-3c); this wave moves the *aggregation* within that new locus: it tests whether entity relevance at L20 lives in specialized heads — sparse head-specific relevance — rather than in the head-average that EXP102's mean read washed out. A KILL here does not re-kill S6-3c's mean gate (already collapsed to step 2b) — it closes the max gate at L20. A CONTINUE is a sparse-head, readout-adjacent claim — not a resurrection of the injection-layer gates or of the washed-out mean read. The injected direction, injection layer, ratio, and bench are deliberately the *killed* ones, as in S6-3/S6-3b/S6-3c. [INTERPRETATION]

The LOG-4407 bar is re-cleared exactly as S6-3/S6-3b/S6-3c cleared it: the bar reserves static per-item L11 experiments; the L20 max-gate is a live function of the current forward pass's activations (computed from the B pass via read-only hooks), inside the "attention-state-gated, dynamic" carve-out. The read layer is not the injection layer — the carve-out operates at the gating-mechanism level, which is dynamic here. [INTERPRETATION]

### 0.2 The read choice (stated for Law #14 to attack)

**Chosen: L20, max-over-heads, τ = 2·(E_i/N_i) unchanged.** Three independent legs:

1. **Disciplined exhaustion [INTERPRETATION]:** Law #14's LOG-4443 endorsed principle — "conservative mean first, permissive max second" — was applied to the L11 space (S6-3 → S6-3b) and is now applied to the L20 space (S6-3c → S6-3d). Max was the permissive second choice at L11 (licensed and Refuted, EXP101); it is the permissive second choice at L20. The ordering is a stated convention, not a measurement: max-at-L20 was named as the S6-3d candidate in S6-3c's rejected alternatives, in EXP101's verdict's exhaustion accounting, and in LOG-4455's breaking point (iv) — never smuggled in.
2. **Testability precedent for the aggregation [OBSERVATION]:** max-over-heads under the 2×-uniform family has already fired — 16/60 at L11 (EXP101) — so the aggregation itself is testable under the adjudicated constant; and mean-at-L20 collapsed (1/60, EXP102). These are *firing observations*, not gate-calibration measurements; no parameter of this design is a function of them.
3. **Sparse-head relevance hypothesis [INTERPRETATION — weaker-motivated, stated honestly]:** if entity relevance at L20 lives in a few specialized heads (e.g. a head tracking the entity mention), the head-average read of EXP102 washes it out — the mean is diluted by heads attending elsewhere, while the max-head read captures it. This is a real mechanism-hypothesis change from S6-3c (distributed vs sparse relevance), honestly weaker-motivated than the S6-3c layer-license: it rests on a plausible property of late attention, not on a program observation. The alternative — that the collapse is a property of the threshold family itself, not of the aggregation — is the falsification-first rival (LOG-4455's re-opened question), and the tripwire adjudicates it: another 2b collapse routes PIVOT, not a re-tuned threshold.

**Rejected alternatives:** **Mean-at-L20** — rejected: already tried (EXP102, collapsed to step 2b); re-running it would be a duplicate, not a variant. **Median / top-k / weighted head-aggregations** — rejected: per LOG-4443's binding adjudication, {mean, max} exhaust the natural discrete head-aggregations; a third aggregation tweak is the pre-emptively unlicensed move (S6-3b's F2 fence extended). **L11 re-tunes** — rejected: both L11 cells are closed (PIVOT-untestable, Refuted). **Later-layer read + new threshold family** — rejected: re-opens the F5 principledness fight with no warrant while the closed-form family is untested at the new aggregation. **L12–L19 / L23 with max** — rejected: no license-bearing evidence at any of them; L23 maximizes the Law #7 leakage attack (risk #1); choosing one would be a guess.

**The no-peeking bright line (binding on this lane).** No S6-3d parameter is a function of any measurement from EXP094–102: the threshold is byte-identical to the adjudicated constant; the layer is carried from S6-3c's license record; the aggregation is the second discrete choice of LOG-4443's endorsed convention, pre-named as S6-3d. **Verified by search (not asserted): no L20-max attention masses exist anywhere in this program's record** — the three gate CSVs in `experiments/runs/*/out/per_item_gate.csv` carry only `mass` (L11 mean, EXP100), `mass_max`/`mass_mean` (L11, EXP101), and `mass_L20_mean` (EXP102); a grep for L20-max mass columns across the runs and research trees returns zero hits (2026-09-28). The EXP098 L20 observation and the EXP101/102 firing observations are *movement/firing* observations, not gate-calibration measurements — no tuning surface exists. The license standard is the absence of a tuning surface, not the chooser's ignorance (LOG-4443's binding adjudication). Law #14 should verify this distinction explicitly. [INTERPRETATION]

**F3 prose discipline (carried, §0.2).** The read is "later-layer (L20) attention from the final prompt token" — never "readout query," never "decision attention." The rationale is now sparse head-specific relevance at the readout-adjacent layer (a rationale change from S6-3c's distributed mean read, honestly disclosed as weaker-motivated).

## 2. Design (frozen model, Δθ = 0; ratio 0.38; L20-max-gated attention injection at L11)

### 2.1 Arms (pre-registered)

| Arm | Injection | Gate | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | none (read-only attention hooks on L20 block inputs) | — | baseline — single run per item; the gate is computed from this pass |

**Unfired items (pre-registered):** on items where the L20 max-gate does not fire, the injection passes are **skipped** and the arm decisions are B's decisions by deterministic copy (identical input + identical frozen weights → identical output; the run log asserts the firing vector and the copies). This is exact, not an approximation. Flips can therefore only arise on fired items.

**{d_i^(11)} (frozen reuse).** EXP094's frozen per-item L11 task-contrast directions; constructor code hash-pinned `5e03f206dd907b52baa1ab6d5c9954c709d1b47d143c83f9aa85caa81917f361`; pre-run re-hash, mismatch → HOLD. The injection layer is unchanged — only the gate's read changed.

### 2.2 The L20 max-gate (pre-registered, §0.2)

For item i: N_i = prompt tokens, E_i = entity-span tokens (bench annotation; step 0 asserts 1 ≤ E_i < N_i, E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5, and span excludes the final token, for all 60 items). Let A^{(20,h)}_i[q_i → t] be the L20 attention weight from the final prompt token q_i to span token t in head h (recomputed offline from the B pass's captured L20 block inputs through the model's own submodules — EXP100/101/102's settled preflight: SDPA emits no weights; the within-backend eager bit-exactness proof is required pre-run for the L20 attention math, exactly as LOG-4441/4448/4455 audited). mass_i^{L20,max} = max_h Σ_{t ∈ span_i} A^{(20,h)}_i[q_i → t] — the **sparse-head** relevance signal. τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i) — **unchanged from S6-3/S6-3b/S6-3c**. Gate fires_i ⟺ mass_i^{L20,max} ≥ τ_i. Coverage = (#fired)/60. The gate derivation is frozen: no outcome-adaptive tuning exists in this design (there is no tuning surface — τ is closed-form and the read layer and aggregation are discrete pre-registered choices).

**Structural fact (provable, no measurement needed):** mass_i^{L20,max} ≥ mass_i^{L20,mean} for every item (the max of a set dominates its mean) — so the single EXP102 mean-fired item (`exp092_elemental_3hop_0`) fires under the max gate necessarily; coverage ≥ 1/60 = 1.67% is structurally guaranteed, and any item where L20-mean firing was near-miss (margin small) plausibly fires. The step-2b tripwire therefore cannot collapse *harder* than EXP102 did. [FACT]

**F3 prose discipline (carried, §0.2).** The read is "later-layer (L20) attention from the final prompt token" — never "readout query," never "decision attention."

**No extra audit columns (deliberate).** Unlike S6-3b's mean-field audit column, S6-3d persists **no** extra attention aggregations — the gate's own mass only. The L20-mean comparability counterfactual is constructible from EXP102's bundle; re-persisting it would re-open the S6-3b F2 attack surface for no licensed use. No verdict, worksheet cell, or report may cite any non-gate attention quantity.

**Non-perturbation proof (pre-registered, carried):** the hooks are read-only observers on the B pass only (capturing L20 block inputs); the empirical proof is step 0's B-agreement gate (60/60 vs the archived EXP094 B vector, produced by unhooked passes). Any hook-induced perturbation → HOLD, never a verdict. EXP100/101/102's bit-exactness proof procedure is the required pre-run instrumentation check for the L20 attention math.

### 2.5 Statistics (pre-registered)

- **McNemar exact, doubling convention** (EXP094's convention; b = wrong→right, c = right→wrong) on paired decisions: gated-T-vs-B **[discovery]**, gated-R-vs-B, gated-T-vs-gated-R [diagnostic-only — Law #9 hygiene]. All contrasts on the full 60-item paired set (unfired items contribute concordant pairs — correct: no injection, no possible flip).
- **ΔM** per EXP093's paired logit-margin convention; **Tango 95% CIs** on paired differences (descriptive).
- **DELTA_BAR = 0.10; ALPHA = 0.01** — **one** discovery contrast (gated-T-vs-B); no Bonferroni (single contrast — stated, not hidden). **Movement gate: ≥ 6 flips** (program standard; necessary but not sufficient — §4.2: significance needs ≥ 8 directional flips).
- **Stratum consistency [DEFINITION, carried from S6-4 v2 §2.4]:** let s_agg = sign(c − b) for the aggregate contrast. For each stratum s ∈ {A-first, C-first} with (b_s, c_s): consistent iff sign(c_s − b_s) = s_agg, or (b_s + c_s) < 2 (uninformative — not a contradiction). A stratum with ≥2 flips in the opposite direction fails consistency → the design cannot CONTINUE (routes to otherwise-PIVOT).
- **Correctness:** margin > 0 (strict), EXP094's convention.
- **Coverage tripwire:** coverage ≥ 10% (≥6/60 fired) → testable; < 10% → PIVOT (untestable form — §3 step 2b). (EXP096 §2.4 pattern; EXP100's/EXP102's step-2b precedent.)
- **Liveness instrument rule:** (gated-T flips ≥ 1) OR (gated-R flips ≥ 1) → regime is LIVE → evaluate. Both 0 with coverage ≥ 10% → FROZEN → Underdetermined (no verdict licensed). Asymmetric rule carried: a moving test arm routes LIVE.
- **Sanity gates:** zero-variance decisions on any injection arm → RUN-INVALID for that arm's contrasts; ties/60 > 5% on any arm → RUN-INVALID for that arm.

### 4.1 Degeneracy analysis of every diagnostic (LOG-4338 lesson — constant-data unit tests, pre-registered)

Each diagnostic states what it returns on degenerate input. A diagnostic that can manufacture a positive from degenerate input is disqualified; all below fail safe:

1. **McNemar exact (doubling).** Constant input (b = c = 0): p = 1.0000 exactly → fails safe toward KILL, **cannot** manufacture CONTINUE. Adversarial input (b=60, c=0): p ≈ 1.73×10⁻¹⁸.
2. **ΔM paired difference.** Zero-variance input → ΔM = 0 exactly → cannot clear DELTA_BAR = 0.10 → fails safe.
3. **Flip-rate.** No flips → 0. Safe.
4. **The liveness instrument (gated-T ≥ 1 OR gated-R ≥ 1) — the dangerous direction.** (a) Truly-frozen regime: all arms 0 → FROZEN → Underdetermined — correct, never KILL. (b) Noise-unlocked regime: a single control noise flip with test arm 0 → LIVE → step-6 KILL fires (coverage ≥ 10% required — a noise-unlocked regime with a collapsed gate routes to step-2b PIVOT, not KILL). **Disclosed asymmetry** (S6-4 §4.1-item-4 carried): the ≥1 bar is deliberately weak to avoid manufacturing Underdetermined from freeze-ambiguity; the KILL's weight is the test arm's flatness across the *fired* items, not the bar's height. (F1)
5. **The coverage tripwire — the load-bearing analysis, re-verified for the L20-max read.** Degenerate input (uniform attention at L20 everywhere): every head's span-mass = E_i/N_i, so mass_i^{L20,max} = E_i/N_i < 2·E_i/N_i = τ_i for all i → coverage 0% → step 2b PIVOT (untestable) — fails safe, **never** manufactures CONTINUE or KILL. (The L20 max-gate's degenerate behavior is layer-independent: uniform attention makes every head's span-mass equal E_i/N_i.) Adversarial input (τ tuned to fire everywhere): coverage 100% → step-2c vacuous-gate flag — the design reduces to a static test, but the licensed positive still requires the full step-5 conjunct (≥6 flips, p<0.01, control flat, strata consistent); the gate alone cannot manufacture a false positive. A gate that fires nowhere cannot manufacture a KILL (step 2b short-circuits before step 6). The tripwire fails safe in every direction.
6. **The gate-source audit (step 2a).** Degenerate input (executor derives the gate from an injection pass): audit assertion fails → HOLD — fails safe toward diagnosis, never toward a verdict.
7. **The B-agreement hook check (step 0ii).** Degenerate input (hooks perturb activations): B decisions deviate from the archived vector → HOLD. Fails safe toward diagnosis. Adversarial input (executor disables hooks to pass agreement): then no L20 attention masses exist → step 0iii/2a assertions fail → HOLD. The check cannot be passed without a genuine read-only L20 attention read.
8. **gated-R flatness — the dangerous direction (S6-4 §4.1-item-7 carried).** Flatness is a CONTINUE-*enabling* conjunct. True neutralization: (a) a truly frozen regime yields test arm 0 too, so the significance conjunct fails — the automatic neutralization path; (b) control 0 with significant directional test arm routes LIVE, and a CONTINUE there fires correctly — task-geometry-specific movement where matched-norm random injection under the identical L20 max-gate moves nothing is the *strongest* evidence the gate found. **CONTINUE requires the full conjunction** (significant AND control flat AND strata consistent AND coverage ≥ 10%); no conjunct alone licenses anything.
9. **Stratum consistency (carried definition).** Degenerate input (all flips in one stratum, other stratum 0 flips): the empty stratum is uninformative (<2 flips), not contradictory → consistent. Cannot manufacture inconsistency from lopsided-but-unidirectional data. Adversarial input (flips split against the aggregate sign): fails consistency → blocks CONTINUE → fails safe toward PIVOT, never toward a false positive.

### 4.2 Probe-feasibility + power algebra (EXP079 lesson — proved, not asserted; algebra unchanged from S6-3c v2 §4.2)

**Explicit statement: the §4.2 algebra is unchanged from S6-3c v2 §4.2.** The design moves the aggregation from mean to max, but nothing in the power algebra is a function of the aggregation: N=60, α=0.01, the McNemar doubling convention, the noise model, and the count-bars are identical. This lane recomputed nothing — the algebra below is carried verbatim, flagged for Law #14's independent verification:

- **McNemar minimum at α = 0.01:** b=0, c=7 → p = 0.015625, n.s.; b=0, c=8 → p = 0.0078125 < 0.01 ✓. The discovery bar needs **≥8 directional flips** (b=0 case); the ≥6-flip movement gate is necessary-but-insufficient. Maximum achievable = 60 ≥ 8. **No structural infeasibility.**
- **CONTINUE-reachability (F1, binding LOG-4436, carried):** the licensed positive requires ≥8 directional flips, and flips can only arise on fired items (unfired items contribute concordant pairs by deterministic copy, §2.1) — so the positive is mathematically unreachable at coverage < 8/60 (13.3%). In the coverage window **[10%, 8/60)**, the only available verdicts are: **KILL** (gated-T < 6, gated-R sub-threshold, LIVE); **PIVOT** (gated-R ≥ 6 — matched-control fragility or control-outmoves-task); **Underdetermined** (FROZEN). A KILL in this window rests on ~6–7 injected items (0/6 → 95% upper bound 39.3% on the gated-conditional flip rate), not on flatness across 60 items. The KILL in the window stays licensed (the alternative — Underdetermined-by-construction in a plausible world — is worse), but its weakness is honestly accounted rather than rhetorically inflated.
- **F2 operating condition re-proved for the L20-max read:** each head's attention weights sum to 1 over all tokens, so each head's span-mass ≤ 1; the max over heads ≤ 1 always. Hence τ_i ≤ 1 ⟺ 2·E_i/N_i ≤ 1 ⟺ E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5 — identical to S6-3/S6-3b/S6-3c. The layer and aggregation changes do not move the operating condition. Step 0 asserts E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5 for all 60 items.
- **Single discovery contrast:** gated-T-vs-B at family-wise 0.01 — no Bonferroni (one contrast; stated, not hidden). Controls and diagnostics are not discovery tests — disclosed, not hidden.
- **KILL feasibility:** needs gated-T < 6 at coverage ≥ 10% — satisfiable (the null predicts ~0–3; EXP096's differently-gated T moved 3/21 at this ratio).
- **CONTINUE feasibility:** needs ≥8 directional gated-T flips with the control flat — satisfiable under the hypothesis; demanding by design (the bar does not move).
- **Coverage-tripwire feasibility:** 6/60 = 10% — satisfiable. Structural floor: mass_i^{L20,max} ≥ mass_i^{L20,mean} everywhere ⇒ coverage ≥ 1/60 (the EXP102 fired item fires necessarily) — the tripwire cannot collapse harder than EXP102 did. **Honest note:** L20 max-attention firing behavior is unobserved anywhere in the program record — the tripwire may fire either way; that is its purpose, not a design defect.
- **Low-coverage asymmetry (disclosed):** at coverage near the 10% floor, the CONTINUE branch demands ≥6 flips among ~6–10 fired items — a high gated-conditional flip rate. The design is therefore **KILL-favoring at low coverage**; the asymmetry is declared here, not discovered later, and it points in the falsification-first direction.
- **Sweep sensitivity floor:** at N=60, the discovery bar (≥8 directional flips) implies a minimum detectable directional flip rate of θ ≈ 0.133. Effects below this are in the blind spot — disclosed, not hidden. **Post-exclusion N:** the count-bars (≥6, ≥8) apply to the post-exclusion paired set (§3 step 1) — the verdict lane recomputes and reports the realized N, the sensitivity floor (8/N_realized), and the coverage-conditioned floor 8/F (F = fired items).
- **Noise specificity:** under a noise flip rate θ_noise = 0.03 (EXP098's L11-only moved 3/60), P(≥6 flips | N=60) = 0.0091 — the movement gate has <1% false-alarm rate against this noise model; P(<6) = 0.9909, so the KILL branch is well-powered against noise.
- **Delivery bands** [0.285, 0.475] per (fired item, arm): feasible by construction (EXP095/096/098/100/101/102 machinery).
- **Budget:** 180 + 60 = 240 expected ≤ 500 hard cap. **Replication gate** (B = 36/60 per-item agreement): feasible — deterministic CPU forward passes demonstrated across EXP094–102.
- **Conclusion:** every pre-registered bar is reachable by construction. If Law #14's independent recomputation of this algebra disagrees, the run does not proceed.

## 6. Known design risks — what Law #14 should try to break (falsification-first)

Ranked by how badly each would wound the licensed verdicts:

1. **Law #7 readout-proximity (the lead risk, carried and sharpened).** The gate reads L20 attention — closer to the readout than L11's. **Attack:** L20 attention to the entity span may correlate with the correct answer (the model "knows" the answer and attends to the entity) — gating on it could be a near-decision proxy, in which case a CONTINUE is uninterpretable and a KILL is unsurprising. **New sharp edge for max:** a max-head read may be *more* decision-proxy-like than the mean read — a single head could be the decision-position head, and firing on its strongest span attention is closer to "inject where the model is about to be right" than the head-average was. **This lane's defenses:** (a) the gate is a scalar threshold on a single head's entity-span attention mass — it never sees the label, the margin, the decision, or the answer; (b) the identical gate applies to the matched random control, so any answer-correlation advantage accrues symmetrically to both arms — a CONTINUE still requires directional task-arm movement with the control flat; (c) the gate is a deterministic function of the frozen B pass, fixed before any injection pass runs; (d) the bench strata are the bench pin, fixed before inspection. **Honest residual:** the attack is real at the correlation level — this is the risk most likely to wound the design's interpretability. Law #14 must confirm the no-leakage argument explicitly, as it did for S6-3's risk #4 (LOG-4436) and S6-3c's risk #1 (LOG-4450).
2. **The F1 audit's role under max.** The decision-proxy audit (fired-vs-unfired B-accuracy split, §5 item 8) is the licensed vocabulary for stating whether the max gate behaved as a near-decision proxy — descriptive only, no routing consequence. In EXP102 the audit showed 1/1 fired vs 35/59 unfired — a suggestive-but-degenerate observation (one fired item). Under the max gate the audit has a chance to be informative (coverage ≥ several items). Law #14 should rule whether a strongly answer-correlated audit profile (fired accuracy ≫ unfired) should weaken a CONTINUE's prose beyond the step-5 wording, or whether the audit must remain purely descriptive. This lane keeps it descriptive — anything else would re-introduce a tuning surface.
3. **Why L20-max and not another cell.** The layer leg is carried from S6-3c (license record: EXP098's sub-bar L20-only 6 flips; readout-adjacency without terminality; L20 sits 3 blocks below the final block L23 — F2's correction carried). The aggregation leg is LOG-4443's endorsed convention (conservative mean first, permissive max second). **Attack:** is a sub-bar observation enough of a license for the read layer — and is "permissive second" a principled choice or a polite way of trying the other thing when the first collapsed? **Counter:** the aggregation choice is discrete and pre-named (S6-3d), not data-driven; the alternative is to leave a licensed cell unexhausted. Law #14 should rule whether the exhaustion argument (3/4 cells closed) is as principled as the original layer argument.
4. **"Is the family really exhausted?" (the terminality attack).** The draft claims {mean,max}×{L11,L20} exhausts the externally-computed attention-gate space. **Attack:** the aggregation-exhaustion argument is airtight (LOG-4443's binding adjudication: mean+max exhaust the natural discrete head-aggregations), but the *layer*-exhaustion argument is thinner — it rests on the license record (L11: injection layer; L20: the only later layer with a movement record), not on a closed discrete space. A median-at-L20 or top-k-at-L20 is excluded by the aggregation fence; but why not max-at-L12? **Counter:** L12–L19 and L23 have no license-bearing evidence at all — choosing one is a guess, and the "last externally-computed attention-gate variant" fence (LOG-4450's adjudication (d), judged principled) pre-emptively unlicenses exactly this kind of hop. The residual is real and honestly stated: layer-exhaustion is license-record-exhaustion, not space-exhaustion. Law #14 should rule whether the terminality claim is warranted or whether it should be rephrased as license-exhaustion.
5. **The near-vacuous-gate risk (new for max).** The max gate is the permissive aggregation — it may fire near-everywhere, routing to the step-2c vacuous-gate flag: the design collapses to a static test at ratio 0.38, and a CONTINUE would carry the weakened "gating unattributed" prose. **Counter:** max-at-L11 fired 26.7% (EXP101) — the aggregation is not vacuous-by-construction; the 2c flag exists precisely to record the collapse honestly rather than hide it. The tripwire handles a vacuous gate as honestly as it handles a collapsed one. Attack whether a near-100% firing max gate makes this design a disguised static experiment under EXP096's "no further static per-item L11 experiments licensed" bar — the lane's answer: the carve-out is the gating *mechanism* (a live function of forward-pass activations), not the firing rate; a vacuous gate is a recorded interpretation caveat, not a license violation.
6. **No-peeking for the aggregation choice.** No L20-max attention masses exist anywhere in the program record (verified by search, §0.2). The aggregation is the second discrete choice of LOG-4443's endorsed convention. **Attack:** max was chosen *because* mean collapsed at L20 — is that outcome-tuning with extra steps? **Counter:** motivation-by-observation is distinguished from calibration-by-observation (LOG-4443's binding standard, re-applied by LOG-4450 §2a): no design *parameter* is a function of the collapse — τ is byte-identical, the aggregation is a discrete pre-named choice, the firing rule is closed-form. There is no tuning surface. Law #14 should verify the distinction explicitly.
7. **τ-principledness at the new aggregation.** F5's three conditions (LOG-4436) re-verified for the L20-max read: (i) the constant is unchanged and closed-form; (ii) fail-safe miscalibration (too selective → 2b PIVOT, too permissive → 2c flag; the gate alone cannot manufacture the step-5 conjunction); (iii) the verdict never hinges on the constant. The aggregation change moves the *read head-selection*, not the constant — the principledness fight about the number stays settled.
8. **Ungated α=8.0 at L11 was never tested (carried).** If gated-T moves, the design attributes it to gating via the EXP096 comparison (3/21 at ratio 0.3792 under a different gate). **Counter (carried):** adding a fourth ungated arm would change the licensed 3-arm design; it is named here as the cheapest follow-up, not smuggled in.
