## 0. Binding question block

**Precise question [HYPOTHESIS].** EXP100's mean-over-heads 2×-uniform gate never fired (0/60, LOG-4441) — the attentional-relevance hypothesis is untestable in that form, not killed. Does the killed L11 task direction move decisions when injection is gated on **existential** attentional relevance — injected only where the **maximum over heads** of mid-stack (L11) attention from the final prompt token to the entity span exceeds the pre-registered threshold τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i), computed from the current forward pass? [HYPOTHESIS]

**Decision it changes.** **KILL:** dynamically-gated movement < 6 flips at coverage ≥ 10% with the gated random control sub-threshold → the direction is not "real but misapplied" under either tested relevance operationalization; the conditional license narrows to truly dynamic mechanisms (self-computed gates and beyond). A KILL's evidential weight is the test arm's flatness across the *fired* items — in the [10%, 13.3%) coverage window this is ~6–7 injected items (0/6 → 95% upper bound 39.3% on the gated-conditional flip rate), not flatness across 60 items (F1, binding LOG-4436, carried). **CONTINUE:** gated flips ≥ 6 AND McNemar-exact (doubling) p < 0.01 AND gated random control flat AND stratum consistency AND coverage ≥ 10% → the first conditional causal signal at L11; the program's next lane is self-conditioned intervention. **PIVOT:** the gate fires on < 10% of items (coverage collapse → untestable in this form — the existential operationalization is exhausted too) or the gated random control moves with the task arm (attentional fragility, nonspecific). All outcomes pre-registered; exactly one will be earned.

**Cheapest falsifying test.** 60 EXP092-B items × 3 arms (B, gated-T, gated-R under the identical live max-gate) = 180 passes maximum (unfired items skip the injection passes by deterministic copy — §2.1), +60 reserve → 240 expected, 500 hard cap. CPU, $0. White-box forward pass with read-only attention hooks on the B pass only (EXP100's settled preflight: SDPA backend, block-input pre-hooks, weights recomputed offline from the model's own submodules, bit-exactness proof required pre-run); frozen weights; Δθ=0.

**Quantitative prediction (pre-registered bars).** Gated-T-vs-B flips ≥ 6 AND McNemar-exact (doubling) p < 0.01 AND gated-R flat (< 6 flips AND |ΔM| < 0.10) AND stratum consistency (§2.5) AND gate coverage ≥ 10% → CONTINUE. Gated-T < 6 flips with coverage ≥ 10% and gated-R sub-threshold → KILL. Coverage < 10% → PIVOT (untestable form). Gated-R ≥ 6 → PIVOT (fragility), regardless of the task arm.

**Breaking point (sharp, four levels).** (i) Gate: coverage < 10% → the design declares itself untestable (PIVOT), never KILL — a KILL is never manufactured from a gate that fires nowhere. (ii) Strength: the single pre-registered ratio is 0.38 (α=8.0, realized 0.3792 per EXP096); above it the question becomes S6-1's super-license territory, not this design's — no grid, no interpolation. (iii) Degeneracy: the L11 direction set is frozen (EXP094's constructor); if its regenerated degeneracy signature deviates from 0.7923 by > 0.05 → HOLD (the pipeline changed). (iv) **No aggregation-hopping:** S6-3b is the last aggregation variant licensed under the 2×-uniform threshold family. If the max-gate also collapses (<10%), the existential-relevance operationalization is exhausted at this threshold — the next move must change the threshold family or the read layer, each requiring fresh pre-registration and Law #14 review. A third aggregation tweak is pre-emptively unlicensed by this draft.

**The belief it tests (named, LOG-4408):** "the direction is real but statically misapplied" — overturned-by a gated signal (the program goes self-conditioned), deepened-by a gated KILL (the conditional license narrows to truly dynamic mechanisms). [INTERPRETATION]

### 0.1 Why this is not "secretly S6-3/EXP100" (the distinction Law #14 must confirm)

S6-3's gate operationalized relevance as **average** concentration: mean-over-heads mass ≥ 2× uniform — "the layer as a whole is entity-focused." S6-3b operationalizes relevance as **existential** concentration: max-over-heads mass ≥ 2× uniform — "there exists a head through which the layer selectively attends to the entity." These are different mechanism hypotheses about what "the model attending" means: S6-3 tested diffuse relevance; S6-3b tests channeled relevance. The threshold constant is byte-identical (2×); what changed is the aggregation, i.e. the relevance claim's quantifier (∀-average vs ∃-head). A KILL here does not re-kill S6-3's exact gate (which died untestable, not refuted) and a CONTINUE is a different, weaker-threshold existential claim — not a resurrection of the mean-gate. The injected direction, layer, ratio, and bench are deliberately the *killed* ones, as in S6-3. [INTERPRETATION]

### 0.2 The re-gated no-peeking construction (the load-bearing answer to EXP100's verdict)

[FACT] EXP100's binding verdict (LOG-4441) leaves the τ-principledness and no-peeking questions re-opened for any re-gating: "each requiring its own pre-registration and Law #14 review, since any of them re-opens the τ-principledness and no-peeking questions this verdict leaves untouched." This draft answers both before any run.

**What changed and what did not.** The threshold τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i) is **unchanged — byte-identical to the Law #14-adjudicated constant** (LOG-4436, F5 binding: licensable as a fail-safe pre-registered heuristic). No numeric threshold was moved. What changed is the **aggregation**: mass_i^max = max_h Σ_{t ∈ span_i} A^{(11,h)}_i[q_i → t] replaces mass_i^mean = (1/H) Σ_h (·). Gate fires_i ⟺ mass_i^max ≥ τ_i.

**Why max-over-heads (stated for Law #14 to attack).** Three independent legs, none of which is fitted to data: (1) **Structural [FACT]:** max_h ≥ mean_h always, strictly unless all heads are equal — the max-gate is uniformly more permissive than the mean-gate on every item, by construction, with no data examined. (2) **Pre-named alternative [FACT]:** S6-3 v2 §2.2 explicitly considered and rejected max-over-heads as "the less conservative option" that "would fire the gate more often" — S6-3b selects among pre-registered discrete design alternatives; it does not invent a new parameter. (3) **Published qualitative observation [OBSERVATION, LOG-4441]:** EXP100's mean-masses ran below the uniform baseline on all 60 items — a 1×-uniform mean-gate would plausibly also collapse, so merely lowering the multiple (candidate (a)) risks a second predictable PIVOT; changing the aggregation is the honest next move. **Rejected alternatives:** (a) lower multiple of uniform — rejected: risks re-collapsing on the same aggregation (per the LOG-4441 observation) and re-opens the "why this multiple" principledness fight from zero; (b)-as-tweaked-further — rejected by the §0 breaking-point (iv) no-aggregation-hopping fence; (c) later-layer relevance read — rejected for this wave: it changes the hypothesis more substantially (readout-adjacent relevance vs injection-layer relevance, breaking F3's frozen self-consistency rationale) and re-opens EXP100's settled instrumentation story at a new layer; it is the named S6-3c candidate if S6-3b collapses.

**The no-peeking bright line (binding on this lane).** EXP100's observed mass distribution (mass_i ∈ [0.0396, 0.0746], LOG-4441) was **not used to set any parameter of this design**: the threshold is unchanged from the adjudicated constant, and the aggregation switch is a discrete choice among pre-named alternatives motivated by the structural max≥mean fact and the published qualitative observation — not by fitting to the mass histogram. Any parameter that is a function of EXP100's measurements would be a peeking surface; no S6-3b parameter is. The draft states this so Law #14 can verify it: the checkable claim is that τ_i = 2·E_i/N_i appears here exactly as in S6-3 v2, computable from the bench annotation alone. [INTERPRETATION]

**Fail-safe re-proof (F5's three conditions, re-answered for the new gate).** (i) No peeking surface: the constant is unchanged and closed-form; the aggregation is a discrete pre-named choice — there is no tuning surface. (ii) Fail-safe miscalibration: too selective → step 2b PIVOT (never KILL/CONTINUE); too permissive → step 2c vacuous-gate flag, and the gate alone cannot manufacture the step-5 conjunction (which still demands ≥6 flips, p<0.01, flat control, strata). (iii) The verdict never hinges on the aggregation: miscalibration produces PIVOT-untestable or a weak KILL, never a false CONTINUE. The aggregation is load-bearing for *testability*, not *validity* — exactly as F5 adjudicated for the constant. [INTERPRETATION]

**Operating condition (F2 re-proved).** mass_i^max is an attention mass, so mass_i^max ≤ 1 always; τ_i ≤ 1 ⟺ E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5 — identical to S6-3. Step 0 asserts E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5 for all 60 items (violation → HOLD — the bench annotation is outside the gate's operating range).

### 0.3 The gate-determinism note (no S6-4-style obstruction here)

## 2. Design (frozen model, Δθ = 0; ratio 0.38; max-gated attention injection at L11)

### 2.1 Arms (pre-registered)

| Arm | Injection | Gate | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | none (read-only attention hooks attached) | — | baseline — single run per item; the gate is computed from this pass |
| gated-T | d_i^(11) @ 0.38·‖h_i^(11,B)‖₂, iff max-gate fires | live (§2.2) | **discovery test** — the existential-relevance causal question |
| gated-R | g_i^(11), norm-tied per fired item to gated-T's δ, iff max-gate fires (identical live gate) | live (§2.2) | fragility discriminant + liveness instrument |

**Unfired items (pre-registered):** on items where the max-gate does not fire, the injection passes are **skipped** and the arm decisions are B's decisions by deterministic copy (identical input + identical frozen weights → identical output; the run log asserts the firing vector and the copies). This is exact, not an approximation. Flips can therefore only arise on fired items.

**{d_i^(11)} (frozen reuse).** EXP094's frozen per-item L11 task-contrast directions; constructor code hash-pinned `5e03f206dd907b52baa1ab6d5c9954c709d1b47d143c83f9aa85caa81917f361`; pre-run re-hash, mismatch → HOLD. Functional pin: regenerated degeneracy signature must reproduce 0.7923 within ±0.05, else HOLD. Anchored to **baseline** L11 norms ‖h_i^(11,B)‖₂ (never the perturbed norm).

**{g_i^(11)} (fresh random control).** Per-item Gaussian directions, normalized and **norm-tied per fired item** to gated-T (‖g_i^(11)‖ == ‖δ_i^(11)‖ — assertions on all fired rows; the EXP096 double-scale bug class). Fresh pre-registered seed, distinct from every prior R stream including EXP100's.

**Single-B design (frozen choice).** B is byte-identical across arms — 60 B + ≤120 injection = **180 passes maximum**. The attention read is hook overhead on the B pass (no extra passes).

### 2.2 The max-gate (pre-registered, §0.2)

For item i: N_i = prompt tokens, E_i = entity-span tokens (bench annotation; step 0 asserts 1 ≤ E_i < N_i, E_i/N_i ≤ 0.5, and span excludes the final token, for all 60 items). Let A^{(11,h)}_i[q_i → t] be the L11 attention weight from the final prompt token q_i to span token t in head h (recomputed offline from the B pass's captured block-11 inputs through the model's own submodules — EXP100's settled preflight: SDPA emits no weights; the within-backend eager bit-exactness proof is required pre-run, exactly as LOG-4441 audited). mass_i^max = max_h Σ_{t ∈ span_i} A^{(11,h)}_i[q_i → t] — the **existential** relevance signal: the most entity-focused head's mass. τ_i = 2·(E_i/N_i) — **unchanged from S6-3**. Gate fires_i ⟺ mass_i^max ≥ τ_i. Coverage = (#fired)/60. The gate derivation is frozen: no outcome-adaptive tuning exists in this design (there is no tuning surface — τ is closed-form and the aggregation is a discrete pre-registered choice).

**Why max-over-heads is the relevance operationalization here (frozen rationale, F3 discipline).** The hypothesis is about L11 processing: if *any* head's computation at L11 is selectively entity-focused, the layer's local computation contains an entity-attending channel — and the injection lands in that layer. Mean-over-heads dilutes a genuinely attending head with non-attending heads and can report "not attending" while the model, through one head, is attending. Max-over-heads is the existential quantifier over the layer's attention channels: relevance as "there exists an entity-attending head." This is a weaker, more permissive claim than S6-3's average-concentration claim — honestly so, and stated as such. The prose never says "readout query": the read is mid-stack (L11) attention from the final prompt token, exactly as F3 repaired.

**Mean-mass audit field.** `per_item_gate.csv` persists mass_i^mean alongside mass_i^max (same forward pass, free) — the gate fires on max only; the mean column exists for the gate-integrity audit ONLY: the verdict lane may use it solely to verify (a) the fired ⟺ mass_i^max ≥ τ_i consistency on all 60 rows and (b) the structural mean-fire ⟹ max-fire implication (same τ). It is not a second gate; no mean-field-derived coverage, flip, or counterfactual quantity may be cited.

**Non-perturbation proof (pre-registered, carried):** the hooks are read-only observers on the B pass only; the empirical proof is step 0's B-agreement gate (60/60 vs the archived EXP094 B vector, produced by unhooked passes). Any hook-induced perturbation → HOLD, never a verdict. EXP100's bit-exactness proof procedure is the required pre-run instrumentation check.

### 2.5 Statistics (pre-registered)

- **Stratum consistency [DEFINITION, carried from S6-4 v2 §2.4]:** let s_agg = sign(c − b) for the aggregate contrast. For each stratum s ∈ {A-first, C-first} with (b_s, c_s): consistent iff sign(c_s − b_s) = s_agg, or (b_s + c_s) < 2 (uninformative — not a contradiction). A stratum with ≥2 flips in the opposite direction fails consistency → the design cannot CONTINUE (routes to otherwise-PIVOT).
- **Correctness:** margin > 0 (strict), EXP094's convention.
- **Coverage tripwire:** coverage ≥ 10% (≥6/60 fired) → testable; < 10% → PIVOT (untestable form — §3 step 2b). (EXP096 §2.4 pattern; EXP100's step-2b precedent.)

- **Sanity gates:** zero-variance decisions on any injection arm → RUN-INVALID for that arm's contrasts; ties/60 > 5% on any arm → RUN-INVALID for that arm.

Fenced to max-gated per-item L11 injection at ratio 0.38 on EXP092-B — never to "all dynamic mechanisms," "all aggregations," or "all layers." *(Maps to: Refuted.)*
7. **Otherwise → PIVOT** *(maps to: Not supported for the directional claim)*, with named sub-cases: (a) gated-T ≥ 6 but p ≥ 0.01 or gated-R not flat or strata inconsistent (movement without licensed significance); (b) the step-2c vacuous-gate caveat attaches to whichever verdict fired above (weakened dynamic claim — recorded, not re-litigated).

Five permitted verdicts only (Supported / Not supported / Inconclusive / Underdetermined / Refuted); the parenthetical mappings bind each step to its verdict. No banned language. ### 4.1 Degeneracy analysis of every diagnostic (LOG-4338 lesson — constant-data unit tests, pre-registered)

Each diagnostic states what it returns on degenerate input. A diagnostic that can manufacture a positive from degenerate input is disqualified; all below fail safe:

1. **McNemar exact (doubling).** Constant input (b = c = 0): p = 1.0000 exactly → fails safe toward KILL, **cannot** manufacture CONTINUE. Adversarial input (b=60, c=0): p ≈ 1.73×10⁻¹⁸.
2. **ΔM paired difference.** Zero-variance input → ΔM = 0 exactly → cannot clear DELTA_BAR = 0.10 → fails safe.
3. **Flip-rate.** No flips → 0. Safe.
4. **The liveness instrument (gated-T ≥ 1 OR gated-R ≥ 1) — the dangerous direction.** (a) Truly-frozen regime: all arms 0 → FROZEN → Underdetermined — correct, never KILL. (b) Noise-unlocked regime: a single control noise flip with test arm 0 → LIVE → step-6 KILL fires (coverage ≥ 10% required — a noise-unlocked regime with a collapsed gate routes to step-2b PIVOT, not KILL). **Disclosed asymmetry** (S6-4 §4.1-item-4 carried): the ≥1 bar is deliberately weak to avoid manufacturing Underdetermined from freeze-ambiguity; the KILL's weight is the test arm's flatness across the *fired* items, not the bar's height. (F1, carried)
5. **The coverage tripwire — the load-bearing analysis, re-verified for the max-gate.** Degenerate input (uniform attention everywhere): mass_i^max = E_i/N_i < 2·E_i/N_i = τ_i for all i → coverage 0% → step 2b PIVOT (untestable) — fails safe, **never** manufactures CONTINUE or KILL. (Note: the max-gate's degenerate behavior is identical to the mean-gate's here — uniform attention makes every head's mass equal E_i/N_i.) Adversarial input (τ tuned to fire everywhere): coverage 100% → step-2c vacuous-gate flag — the design reduces to a static test, but the licensed positive still requires the full step-5 conjunct; the gate alone cannot manufacture a false positive. A gate that fires nowhere cannot manufacture a KILL (step 2b short-circuits before step 6). The tripwire fails safe in every direction.
6. **The gate-source audit (step 2a).** Degenerate input (executor derives the gate from an injection pass): audit assertion fails → HOLD — fails safe toward diagnosis, never toward a verdict.
7. **The B-agreement hook check (step 0ii).** Degenerate input (hooks perturb activations): B decisions deviate from the archived vector → HOLD. Fails safe toward diagnosis. Adversarial input (executor disables hooks to pass agreement): then no attention masses exist → step 0iii/2a assertions fail → HOLD. The check cannot be passed without a genuine read-only attention read. (EXP100's LOG-4441 audit of this check's residual stands: a zero-decision perturbation is possible in principle; the agreement check is the licensed proof.)
8. **gated-R flatness — the dangerous direction (S6-4 §4.1-item-7 carried).** Flatness is a CONTINUE-*enabling* conjunct. True neutralization: (a) a truly frozen regime yields test arm 0 too, so the significance conjunct fails — the automatic neutralization path; (b) control 0 with significant directional test arm routes LIVE, and a CONTINUE there fires correctly — task-geometry-specific movement where matched-norm random injection under the identical max-gate moves nothing is the *strongest* evidence the gate found. **CONTINUE requires the full conjunction** (significant AND control flat AND strata consistent AND coverage ≥ 10%); no conjunct alone licenses anything.
9. **Stratum consistency (carried definition).** Degenerate input (all flips in one stratum, other stratum 0 flips): the empty stratum is uninformative (<2 flips), not contradictory → consistent. Cannot manufacture inconsistency from lopsided-but-unidirectional data. Adversarial input (flips split against the aggregate sign): fails consistency → blocks CONTINUE → fails safe toward PIVOT, never toward a false positive.

### 4.2 Probe-feasibility + power algebra (EXP079 lesson — proved, not asserted; recomputed by this lane 2026-09-28)

- **McNemar minimum at α = 0.01:** b=0, c=7 → p = 0.015625, n.s.; b=0, c=8 → p = 0.0078125 < 0.01 ✓. The discovery bar needs **≥8 directional flips** (b=0 case); the ≥6-flip movement gate is necessary-but-insufficient. Maximum achievable = 60 ≥ 8. **No structural infeasibility.**
- **CONTINUE-reachability (F1, binding LOG-4436, carried):** the licensed positive requires ≥8 directional flips, and flips can only arise on fired items (unfired items contribute concordant pairs by deterministic copy, §2.1) — so the positive is mathematically unreachable at coverage < 8/60 (13.3%). In the coverage window **[10%, 8/60)**, the only available verdicts are: **KILL** (gated-T < 6, gated-R sub-threshold, LIVE); **PIVOT** (gated-R ≥ 6 — matched-control fragility or control-outmoves-task); **Underdetermined** (FROZEN). A KILL in this window rests on ~6–7 injected items (0/6 → 95% upper bound 39.3% on the gated-conditional flip rate), not on flatness across 60 items. The KILL in the window stays licensed (the alternative — Underdetermined-by-construction in a plausible world — is worse), but its weakness is honestly accounted rather than rhetorically inflated.
- **Single discovery contrast:** gated-T-vs-B at family-wise 0.01 — no Bonferroni (one contrast; stated, not hidden). Controls and diagnostics are not discovery tests — disclosed, not hidden.
- **KILL feasibility:** needs gated-T < 6 at coverage ≥ 10% — satisfiable (the null predicts ~0–3; EXP096's differently-gated T moved 3/21 at this ratio).
- **CONTINUE feasibility:** needs ≥8 directional gated-T flips with the control flat — satisfiable under the hypothesis; demanding by design (the bar does not move).
- **Coverage-tripwire feasibility:** 6/60 = 10% — satisfiable; the max-gate is uniformly more permissive than the adjudicated mean-gate (max ≥ mean on every item, §0.2), so the tripwire's 10% floor is *easier* to clear than in EXP100 — if it still collapses, the existential operationalization is genuinely exhausted at this threshold family (§0 breaking point iv).
- **Low-coverage asymmetry (disclosed):** at coverage near the 10% floor, the CONTINUE branch demands ≥6 flips among ~6–10 fired items — a high gated-conditional flip rate. The design is therefore **KILL-favoring at low coverage**; the asymmetry is declared here, not discovered later, and it points in the falsification-first direction.
- **Sweep sensitivity floor:** at N=60, the discovery bar (≥8 directional flips) implies a minimum detectable directional flip rate of θ ≈ 0.133. Effects below this are in the blind spot — disclosed, not hidden. **Post-exclusion N:** the count-bars (≥6, ≥8) apply to the post-exclusion paired set (§3 step 1) — the verdict lane recomputes and reports the realized N, the sensitivity floor (8/N_realized), and the coverage-conditioned floor 8/F (F = fired items).
- **Noise specificity:** under a noise flip rate θ_noise = 0.03 (EXP098's L11-only moved 3/60), P(≥6 flips | N=60) = 0.0091 — the movement gate has <1% false-alarm rate against this noise model; P(<6) = 0.9909, so the KILL branch is well-powered against noise.
- **Delivery bands** [0.285, 0.475] per (fired item, arm): feasible by construction (EXP095/096/098/100 machinery).
- **Budget:** 180 + 60 = 240 expected ≤ 500 hard cap. **Replication gate** (B = 36/60 per-item agreement): feasible — deterministic CPU forward passes demonstrated across EXP094–100.
- **Conclusion:** every pre-registered bar is reachable by construction. If Law #14's independent recomputation of this algebra disagrees, the run does not proceed.
