## Binding defects (must be fixed before SIGN)

**E1 — Power numbers computed for n=380/level; the registered bench provides n=190/level.** Both drafts. The design (§6) and pre-reg (§2) state "n = 380 per hop level, carried from EXP149's verified power table (at d = 0.30–0.36: power 0.79–0.88 for δ = +0.10, one-sided α = 0.05)." But the registered bench (§5.1: 4 strata × 190 items = 760) gives the transitive primary **190 items per hop level** (transitive-2hop: 190; transitive-3hop: 190). EXP149's table was computed for 380/level and does not transfer. Independent recomputation at n=190 (SE(θ) = √[(d−net²)(2/190)], net = 0.20):

| (d, net) | True SE(θ) at n=190 | True power (δ=+0.10, 1-sided α=0.05) | Drafts claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| (0.30, 0.20) | 0.0523 | 0.61 | 0.0370 / 0.86 |
| (0.32, 0.20) | 0.0543 | 0.58 | 0.0384 / 0.83 |
| (0.36, 0.20) | 0.0580 | 0.53 | 0.0410 / 0.79 |
| (0.30, 0.25) | 0.0500 | 0.64 | 0.0354 / 0.88 |

The design is powered at ≈ 0.53–0.64 for its own quantitative prediction, not 0.79–0.88. Law #2: power constants must be computed for the registered bench, not carried from a different bench. **Fix:** recompute §2's power table and every power claim (design §6, pre-reg §§1.4/2) for n_T2 = n_T3 = 190; correct the "n = 380 per hop level" design-value statement to the bench's actual per-level n.

**E2 — Reverse dose-response P(KILL) carried at the wrong n.** Pre-reg §2 (and design §6): "under true δ = −0.10 (reverse dose-response), P(KILL fires) ≈ 0.77." That value was computed for n=380. At n=190: (d, net) = (0.30, 0.20) → Φ(0.10/0.0523 − 1.96) = Φ(−0.048) ≈ **0.48**; (0.36, 0.20) → Φ(0.10/0.0580 − 1.96) = Φ(−0.237) ≈ **0.41**. **Fix:** replace ≈0.77 with the recomputed band ≈0.41–0.48 wherever the reverse-KILL operating characteristic appears.

**E3 — The Step-0 n-raise rule is unsatisfiable against the fixed bench.** Pre-reg §2's registered power rule computes power with the design value n=380: at the bench's actual n=190 the true power is ≈0.53–0.64 < 0.80, so the rule would demand raising n to ≈400/level — but the bench is registered fixed at 190/stratum (§5.1) and cannot supply more items. No outcome is registered for "required n exceeds bench size" (the D5 carry covers guard (g)/(h) breach, not bench-size breach). **Fix:** rewrite the Step-0 power rule to reference the bench's actual n (n_T2 = n_T3 = 190); register the bench-size outcome explicitly — if the Step-0 registered power at the bench n is < 0.80, the study STOPS at design cost, logged HELD (the bench cannot supply the registered power bar). **CEO decision required:** the 0.80 bar vs the ≈0.55 achievable power is a registered design choice — the CEO may instead accept the lower bar by registered amendment, or restructure (e.g., a transitive-only 2×380 bench, which would drop the compositional pilot under guard (g)). The repair lane prescribes the mechanical registration; the bar decision is the CEO's.

**E4 — §3's frozen decision bars vs §4's decision table: two unreconciled KILL/CONTINUE definitions.** Pre-reg §3 carries EXP148's per-level KILL conjunction ("95% Wald CI for net δ̂=(b−c)/n entirely below +0.10") and CONTINUE ("one-sided p < 0.05 AND net δ̂ ≥ +0.10") "applied to the transitive primary," while the binding §4 table defines the primary KILL as "95% CI for (δ̂_T3 − δ̂_T2) entirely ≤ 0" and CONTINUE as "CI entirely above 0 AND transitive-3hop S−C one-sided p < 0.05."
