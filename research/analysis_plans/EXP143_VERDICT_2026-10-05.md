- The median-split low-spread selector would have **hurt** accuracy (Δ=−0.042): selecting low-spread items underperformed the complement. No selection experiment is licensed — nothing to rescue.
- This is consistent with the pre-registered winner's-curse pre-mortem (§7.1): the discovery |ρ|=0.49 was plausibly inflated, and at true |ρ|≈0.30 the design was only ~65% powered. The CI+MDE reporting prevents misreading: the data are compatible with a small-or-zero true effect, not with the discovery-sized effect.
- The τ=0.7 sampling-artifact caveat (§7.3) stands as a live alternative explanation for the discovery result: spread may have tracked a sampling artifact of the discovery view's branch construction rather than a replicable signal. No mechanistic claim is licensed either way (instrumental reading only, per the plan).
