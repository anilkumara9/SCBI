## 5. Reading

[INTERPRETATION] The J7 hypothesis — that explicit in-context WRITE/READ discipline
beats budget-matched chain-of-thought on multi-hop reasoning at Δθ=0 — is supported
at decision-relevant scale (+22pp, p = 2.3e-07). This is the program's first
capability entry: the first experiment in which a frozen-model intervention
*adds* capability rather than mapping a boundary.

[INTERPRETATION] Two honest caveats. First, the C arm's absolute accuracy (0.1889)
is low: budget-matched CoT underperforms direct answer on this bench under
first-token scoring. S beats C decisively, but S (0.4111) does not beat B (0.6611).
The discipline gain is over *reasoning-heavy* CoT specifically — consistent with
the J7 framing (discipline vs architecture for reasoning), but the result does not
show scratchpad beating the best available baseline. Second, the compositional
strata sit near ceiling for B (0.96–0.98), so the pooled S−C comparison leans on
transitive items for discordant pairs; the effect is directionally consistent
across strata, which mitigates but does not eliminate this concern.

[INTERPRETATION] Licensed follow-ups (per §4 CONTINUE branch): hop dose-response
(does the S advantage grow with hop count?), cross-family generality (bounded by
EXP145 — a new family needs its own pre-reg). The v102 paper's "zero capability
entries" claim is now stale and must be revised in the next paper version.
