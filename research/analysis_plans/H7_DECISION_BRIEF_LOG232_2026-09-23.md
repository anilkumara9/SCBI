## 2. Options

**REVERSE** — restore the bridge as a positive control for autonomous-mechanism questions.
Ruled out. A positive control for *autonomous* steering must be label-free; the bridge is label-informed by construction (Q1, Supported). No verdict, including K1-exoneration, changes how the vector was built. Reversal would also require ignoring the adopted Law #7 operational rule. **Not honest; not available.**

**KEEP (verbatim)** — demotion stands exactly as LOG-204 stated it, tilt-artifact layer included.
Ruled out. The tilt account was a stated layer of the demotion's grounds (the "mandatory and irreversible" branch was conditioned on K1 confirming tilt). K1/EXP082 killed it (both directional tilts 0/60 on every run). Retaining a killed ground to justify a status would be theory preservation — the named primary failure mode. **Not honest; not available.**

**NARROW** — demotion stands, grounds reduced to Q1 alone; tilt layer retired; rescue mechanism marked open.
This is the only option consistent with the evidence:
- The bridge remains **"rescue control (known-answer direction), NOT a mechanism control"**; its positive-control status for autonomous-mechanism questions stays **REVOKED** — on the Law #7 letter violation alone, which is sufficient and permanent.
- The tilt-artifact suspicion is **retired as a demotion ground** (K1/EXP082 killed it). The demotion must not be cited as resting on readout tilt.
- The EXP070 LOG-110 license ("the apparatus can detect causal effects") stands, now tilt-clean: the +10pp official rescue (6/0, p=0.03125) is a label-informed readout-path effect whose mechanism is **open** — the live candidates are the relational (t−f) readout effect (still label-informed) and downstream transformation (S3-8's ×1.8, Supported). Steerability remains NOT licensed (Q2's negative half survives).
