# SCBI Top-Talent Team Charter — 2026-09-28

*Standing program asset. Authority: direct founder order 2026-09-28
("make sure team top talents, researchers, coders, mathematicians, whatever
needs"). Companion to research/TEAM_ROSTER_2026-09-23.md (LOG-196) and
research/EXPERT_TRACKS.md (LOG-140), which it extends — it does not replace
them. The 12-track researcher core stands as chartered. This document adds
the elite talent the founder explicitly named and sets the hiring bar for
every track. Status: defined while the program is archived; activates on
dispatch when the program resumes. Charters persist; specialists are
ephemeral.*

## 1. The top-talent bar (applies to every track)

"Top talent" here is not a résumé — it is a behavioral standard, enforced
in every dispatch brief:

- **Researchers:** falsification-first. A top researcher returns a kill of
  their own hypothesis with the same pride as a positive. No
  theory-preservation, no metric cherry-picking (Laws #8, #9, #14), no
  silent hypothesis shifts (Law #4). Every claim carries its
  kill criterion before it carries its conclusion.
- **Coders:** reproducibility-or-it-didn't-happen. A top coder ships
  bundles that run first-try on a fresh machine, pins every seed, hashes
  every weight file before and after (Law #13), and makes every number
  traceable to a logged artifact. A silent failure is a disqualification.
- **Mathematicians:** proof-grade or nothing. A top mathematician earns
  every "clearly," hunts counterexamples before writing QED, and never
  lets a hand-wave cross into a protocol, paper, or verdict. The
  MATH_STANDARDS_CHARTER is the floor, not the ceiling.
- **All tracks:** knowledge protocol complete before substantive work;
  epistemic double-labeling on every load-bearing claim (AGENTS.md §5);
  the challenge + idea obligation on every dispatch; log discipline —
  what was done, what surprised, what is uncertain, what is needed.

## 3. New tracks — the founder's explicit order

### Track 13 — Research engineering (elite coders) [NEW]

- **Role definition:** the lab's builders. Implementation was previously
  staffed ad hoc per dispatch; it is now a standing track with its own
  charter and bar.
- **Owns:** experiment executables, Kaggle/GPU bundles, evaluator
  harnesses, determinism infrastructure (seed pinning, environment
  manifests, Δθ=0 runtime guards, weight-hash verification), artifact
  lineage (every number → logged file).
- **Bar lineage:** EXP070's 18/18 evaluator tests, the EXP094 verdict-
  repair (a scoring-script bug fabricated a verdict; the repair caught
  it) — the track exists so that class of failure becomes structurally
  impossible.
- **May not touch:** hypothesis or protocol design (it builds what
  others specify; a builder who designs the experiment cannot be its
  independent check), result interpretation, pre-registration content.
- **Hiring order: FIRST of the new tracks.** Every future experiment —
  CPU or GPU — needs its executables before anything else moves.

### Track 14 — Mathematicians (proof-grade) [NEW]

- **Role definition:** the lab's proof engine. Track 1 formalizes;
  Track 14 proves and audits proofs.
- **Owns:** proofs, lemmas, bounds (Procrustes-failure lineage and
  everything after it), audit of every mathematical claim entering a
  paper, protocol, or verdict; enforcement of the MATH_STANDARDS_CHARTER
  at dispatch time, not review time.
- **May not touch:** empirical claims beyond what the math licenses
  (it certifies the license; it does not spend it), implementation.
- **Hiring order: SECOND of the new tracks.** Pairs with Track 1 on
  theory and with Track 8 on statistical formalism — the two places
  where an unlicensed step does the most damage.

## 4. "Whatever needs" — on-call supporting talent (not standing tracks)

Standing law: no vanity roles. These are chartered on-call roles,
staffed per milestone, not standing tracks:

- **Figure/visualization craft** — the founder's pixel-level standard
  (400 DPI inspection, zero overlaps, 8pt minimum, grayscale-safe).
  Attaches to Track 13 on paper/figure milestones.
- **Scientific paper craftsman** — human voice + mathematical clarity;
  the v82 humanization pass is the bar. Staffed per paper milestone.
- **Data steward** — artifact lineage, manifest integrity, bundle
  inventories. Attaches to Track 13 on execution milestones.

If any on-call role's workload becomes continuous, the CEO charters it
as a track — it is not promoted by its occupant's request.

## 5. Staffing discipline (unchanged law, restated for the new tracks)

1. **No track licenses its own claim.** Licensing runs through the
   §G/§H program gates and the five verdict categories — never through
   a role's say-so. This binds Tracks 13–14 exactly as it binds 1–12.
2. **Track 7 (adversarial) is independent of the new tracks.** It
   reviews Track 13's harnesses and Track 14's proofs; it does not
   co-author them.
3. **Track 8 holds its veto** on pre-registration signing — now with
   Track 14 as its formal partner and Track 13 as its executability
   check (a feasible design with an unrunnable bundle is not signed).
4. **The mentor is outside the roster.** Unchanged.
5. **Staffing happens on dispatch.** Defining this charter staffs
   nothing; when the program resumes, each dispatch brief carries the
   §1 bar for its track, and the brief is rejected if it doesn't.

## 6. Mentor-objection pre-emption

1. Two new tracks, each mapped to a demonstrated program need (the
   EXP094 scoring bug; the proof-audit load on Track 1) — not vanity.
2. Track 13 cannot design what it builds; Track 14 cannot spend the
   license it certifies — structural independence, not politeness.
3. The bar in §1 is behavioral and checkable per dispatch; "top talent"
   is audited in the work product, never asserted in the brief.
