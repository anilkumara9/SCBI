## 0. What is being audited

[DEFINITION] SCBI (Self-Consistent Basis Invention) is the hypothesis that a frozen backbone ($\Delta\theta = 0$) can construct, evaluate, select, and apply **temporary per-instance representation bases** $B$ at inference time — via operators Generate ($\mathcal{G}$), Evaluate ($\mathcal{E}$), Select ($\mathcal{S}$), Transfer ($\mathcal{T}$) — solving:

$$\min_{B,z} J(B,z;x) \quad \text{subject to} \quad \theta = \theta_0.$$

[OBSERVATION] What SCBI has actually tested to date (EXP064–066) is far narrower: a **static** aggregated contrast-direction basis $B_{agg}$ injected into the residual stream (or aligned cross-vocabulary via Procrustes), with zero decision changes on the tested instances. The full dynamic $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}/\mathcal{T}$ loop has **not** been empirically demonstrated.

[INTERPRETATION] The audit must therefore score two objects separately: (a) the tested static mechanism, and (b) the claimed full dynamic loop. Collapsing them into one verdict would repeat the hypothesis-shifting error flagged in the 2026-09-23 adversarial audit (Laws #4, #11).

### 2.1 Zou et al. — Representation Engineering (RepE), 2023
- **Title:** Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency
- **Authors:** Andy Zou, Long Phan, Sarah Chen, James Campbell, Phillip Guo, Richard Ren, Alexander Pan, Xudong Yin, Mantas Mazeika, Ann-Kathryn Dombrowski, Shashwat Goel, Nathaniel Li, Michael J. Byun, Zifan Wang, Alex Mallen, Steven Basart, Sanmi Koyejo, Dawn Song, Matt Fredrikson, Zico Kolter, Dan Hendrycks
- **Year:** 2023 — **Venue:** arXiv preprint — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01405 — **arXiv:** 2310.01405
- **Method:** Reading vectors extracted from contrastive prompt pairs; control via linear combination added to activations.
- **Problem:** Transparency/control of high-level model behaviors (honesty, harmlessness).
- **Backbone:** Frozen open models (LLaMA/LLaMA-2 family). **Does it update θ?** No. **Does it modify hidden states?** Yes (control phase). **Does it create temporary representations?** No — vectors are precomputed offline. **Does it search candidate representations?** No. **Does it use an evaluator?** Partial (reading probes evaluated on contrast sets). **External info/ground truth?** Contrast prompt labels (human-designed). **Inference-time computation:** Read-only analysis + fixed control vectors.
- **Closest SCBI component:** Reading operator ≈ contrast-direction extraction.
- **Similarity:** Frozen backbone; contrast-pair difference as behavior direction; additive intervention. **Difference:** Vectors are static and precomputed; no per-instance basis construction, no self-consistency selection. **Potential overlap:** Reading phase is the same math as SCBI's contrast-direction computation. **Potential distinction:** None in the tested SCBI mechanism. **Confidence:** High.

### 2.2 Turner et al. — Activation Addition (ActAdd), 2023
- **Title:** Steering Language Models With Activation Engineering
- **Authors:** Alexander Matt Turner, Lisa Thiergart, Gavin Leech, David Udell, Juan J. Vazquez, Ulisse Mini, Monte MacDiarmid
- **Year:** 2023 — **Venue:** arXiv preprint — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10248v2 — **arXiv:** 2308.10248
- **Method:** $v = \overline{h}(p_+) - \overline{h}(p_-)$ over contrastive prompt pairs; $h \leftarrow h + c \cdot v$ during forward pass, no training.
- **Problem:** Inference-time behavior steering. **Backbone:** Frozen. **Does it update θ?** No. **Temporary representations?** No (single fixed vector). **Search candidates?** No. **Evaluator?** No. **Inference-time computation:** One forward pass with additive injection.
- **Closest SCBI component:** The entirety of SCBI's Stage A static injection: $h \leftarrow h + B_{agg}$.
- **Similarity:** Operationally identical: frozen model, diff-in-means direction, additive residual-stream injection. **Difference:** None for the tested mechanism. **Confidence:** High — [INTERPRETATION] SCBI Stage A is an instance of ActAdd, not a variant.

### 2.3 Rimsky et al. — Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA), 2023 / ACL 2024
- **Title:** Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition
- **Authors:** Nina Rimsky, Nick Gabrieli, Julian Schulz, Meg Tong, Evan Hubinger, Alexander Matt Turner
- **Year:** 2023 (arXiv), ACL 2024 — **Venue:** ACL 2024 — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06681v1 — **arXiv:** 2312.06681
- **Method:** Layer-swept mean contrast vectors across many behaviors (sycophancy, refusal, etc.); steering effectiveness and side-effect measurements.
- **Problem:** Systematic behavior steering of Llama 2. **Backbone:** Frozen. **Does it update θ?** No. **Temporary representations?** No. **Search candidates?** Layer sweep only (hyperparameter, not representation search). **Evaluator?** Behavioral benchmarks. **Inference-time computation:** Additive steering during generation.
- **Closest SCBI component:** $B_{agg}$ construction and injection.
- **Similarity:** Same diff-in-means construction; same additive application. **Difference:** SCBI adds an aggregation-over-instances and a cross-vocabulary mapping step — both preprocessing, neither changing the inference-time operator. **Potential overlap:** Complete for the injection mechanism. **Confidence:** High.

### 2.4 Li et al. — Inference-Time Intervention (ITI), NeurIPS 2023

- **Authors:** Kenneth Li, Oam Patel, Fernanda Viégas, Hanspeter Pfister, Martin Wattenberg
- **Year:** 2023 — **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023 — **URL:** https://export.arxiv.org/abs/2306.03341 — **arXiv:** 2306.03341
- **Method:** Train linear probes per attention head on truthfulness; shift activations of top heads along probe directions by $\sigma$-scaled amounts during inference.
- **Problem:** Truthfulness. **Backbone:** Frozen at inference. **Does it update θ?** No at inference (probes trained offline). **Temporary representations?** No. **Search candidates?** No (head selection offline). **Evaluator?** Truthfulness benchmarks.
- **Closest SCBI component:** Evaluated intervention on activations at inference time.
- **Similarity:** Frozen backbone; linear-direction intervention. **Difference:** ITI's directions are learned probe normals (offline training), not self-constructed contrast directions; head-localized not basis-level. **Confidence:** High.

### 2.6 Subramani, Suresh & Peters — Latent Steering Vectors, ACL 2022 Findings

### 2.7 Todd et al. — Function Vectors, ICLR 2024
- **Title:** Function Vectors in Large Language Models
- **Authors:** Eric Todd, Millicent L. Li, Arnab Sen Sharma, Aaron Mueller, Byron C. Wallace, David Bau
- **Year:** 2024 — **Venue:** ICLR 2024 — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15213 — **arXiv:** 2310.15213
- **Method:** Causal mediation analysis on ICL tasks; sum of a small set of attention-head outputs = "function vector" that transports the task to zero-shot prompts; vectors partially composable by addition.

- **Authors:** Ole Jorgensen, Dylan Cope, Nandi Schoots, Murray Shanahan

### 2.11 Mikolov, Le & Sutskever — Cross-language similarities, 2013

### 2.13 Ethayarajh — Contextual representation anisotropy, EMNLP 2019

### 2.14 Bansal, Nakkiran & Barak — Model Stitching, NeurIPS 2021
- **Title:** Revisiting Model Stitching to Compare Neural Representations
- **Authors:** Yamini Bansal, Preetum Nakkiran, Boaz Barak

### 2.16 Wang et al. — Self-Consistency, ICLR 2023

### 2.17 Brown et al. — Large Language Monkeys, 2024
- **Title:** Large Language Monkeys: Scaling Inference Compute with Repeated Sampling
- **Authors:** Bradley Brown, Jordan Juravsky, Ryan Ehrlich, Ronald Clark, Quoc V. Le, Christopher Ré, Azalia Mirhoseini

### 2.18 Yao et al. — Tree of Thoughts (ToT), NeurIPS 2023

[INTERPRETATION] This is the closest structural prior for the *dynamic* SCBI loop, and it makes the loop a domain-shifted ToT, i.e., a known combination. **Confidence:** High.

### 2.19 Chuang et al. — DoLa, ICLR 2024

### 2.20 Wu et al. — ReFT / LoReFT, 2024
- **Title:** ReFT: Representation Finetuning for Language Models
- **Authors:** Zhengxuan Wu, Aryaman Arora, Zheng Wang, Atticus Geiger, Dan Jurafsky, Christopher D. Manning, Christopher Potts
- **Year:** 2024 — **Venue:** arXiv preprint — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03592 — **arXiv:** 2404.03592
- **Method:** Learn low-rank interventions on hidden representations of a frozen base model (LoReFT); 10–50× more parameter-efficient than PEFTs.
- **Closest SCBI component:** "Editing representations of a frozen model is a more powerful alternative" — the premise SCBI shares.
- **Similarity:** Frozen base model; representation-space intervention. **Difference:** ReFT *learns* interventions via training (Δθ=0 for the base model but learned intervention parameters exist); SCBI constructs interventions at inference time without training. ReFT's strong results on reasoning tasks are [INTERPRETATION] evidence that the representation-editing premise is sound — but also that *learned* edits outperform heuristics, a headwind for SCBI's heuristic construction. **Confidence:** High.

### 2.21 Sun et al. — Test-Time Training (TTT), ICML 2020

- **Authors:** Yu Sun, Xiaolong Wang, Zhuang Liu, John Miller, Alexei A. Efros, Moritz Hardt

### 2.22 Belitsky et al. — KV Cache Steering for Controlling Frozen LLMs, 2025
- **Title:** KV Cache Steering for Controlling Frozen LLMs (v1 title: "KV Cache Steering for Inducing Reasoning in Small Language Models")
- **Authors:** Max Belitsky, Dawid J. Kopiczko, Michael Dorkenwald, M. Jehanzeb Mirza, James R. Glass, Cees G.M. Snoek, Yuki M. Asano
- **Year:** 2025 — **Venue:** arXiv preprint (peer-review status unverified) — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08799v1 — **arXiv:** 2507.08799
- **Method:** One-shot intervention applied directly to the key-value cache; steering vectors constructed from reasoning traces (teacher-model or human-annotated); induces chain-of-thought reasoning in small models without fine-tuning or prompt modifications.
- **Problem:** Behavior-level control of frozen LLMs. **Backbone:** Frozen. **Does it update θ?** No. **Temporary representations?** No (one-shot cache edit per session). **Search candidates?** No. **Evaluator?** Reasoning benchmarks. **Inference-time computation:** Single cache intervention, negligible latency overhead.
- **Closest SCBI component:** Inference-time activation intervention on a frozen model.
- **Similarity:** Frozen backbone; no training; inference-time only. **Difference:** Cache-level, one-shot, no basis construction or selection loop. **Confidence:** Medium — verified against the primary arXiv source 2026-09-23 (corrections pass L-4; previously cited from a paper-aggregation page).

### 2.23 Dathathri et al. — Plug and Play Language Models (PPLM), ICLR 2020
- **Title:** Plug and Play Language Models: A Simple Approach to Controlled Text Generation
- **Authors:** Sumanth Dathathri, Andrea Madotto, Janice Lan, Jane Hung, Eric Frank, Piero Molino, Jason Yosinski, Rosanne Liu
- **Year:** 2020 — **Venue:** ICLR 2020 — **URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/1912.02164 — **arXiv:** 1912.02164
- **Method:** At generation time, gradients from an external attribute model (a bag-of-words or a single learned layer) update the LM's *past hidden activations* $\Delta H_t$, followed by KL-regularized re-forward passes; the LM parameters are never trained or modified. Per-instance, inference-time search over hidden-state trajectories guided by an evaluator.
- **Problem:** Controlled text generation (topic/sentiment). **Backbone:** Frozen (no LM training). **Does it update θ?** No — only hidden states $H_t$. **Temporary representations?** Yes — per-generation hidden-state updates, discarded after sampling. **Search candidates?** Yes — over latent trajectories via gradient steps. **Evaluator?** Yes — an external attribute classifier (the gradient source). **External info/ground truth?** Attribute labels for the small classifier. **Inference-time computation:** Forward + backward passes per generation step (expensive).
- **Closest SCBI component:** The *dynamic* intervention concept — per-instance, inference-time representation updates of a frozen model guided by an evaluator. The closest published prior to SCBI's claimed $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}/\mathcal{T}$ loop (Wave 2 review L-1).
- **Similarity:** Frozen backbone; per-instance inference-time intervention on internal representations; an evaluator scores/guides the intervention; $\Delta\theta = 0$. **Difference:** PPLM's evaluator is *external* (a separately trained attribute classifier), not internal self-consistency; its search object is the hidden-state trajectory, not a basis; and it uses gradient steps, not generate–evaluate–select. **Potential distinction for SCBI:** internal (self-consistency) evaluator + basis-valued search object — claimed, not demonstrated. **Confidence:** High.

### 2.24 Madaan et al. — Self-Refine, NeurIPS 2023
- **Title:** Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback
- **Authors:** Aman Madaan, Niket Tandon, Prakhar Gupta, Skyler Hallinan, Luyu Gao, Sarah Wiegreffe, Uri Alon, Nouha Dziri, Shrimai Prabhumoye, Yiming Yang, Shashank Gupta, Bodhisattwa Prasad Majumder, Katherine Hermann, Sean Welleck, Amir Yazdanbakhsh, Peter Clark
- **Year:** 2023 — **Venue:** NeurIPS 2023 — **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651 — **arXiv:** 2303.17651
- **Method:** A single frozen LLM acts as generator, feedback-provider (critic), and refiner in an iterative loop: generate output → self-critique against a rubric → refine → repeat (up to ~4 iterations). No training, no external model, no reward signal.
- **Problem:** Output quality across 7 tasks (dialog, code, math reasoning). **Backbone:** Frozen. **Does it update θ?** No. **Temporary representations?** Text outputs are transient. **Search candidates?** Yes — over output revisions. **Evaluator?** Self-feedback (the same LLM as critic). **External info/ground truth?** Task rubrics (human-designed). **Inference-time computation:** Multiple generate/critique/refine passes; ~20% absolute gains on average; gains plateau after 2–3 iterations; self-critique has documented blind spots.
- **Closest SCBI component:** The $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}$ loop structure with an *internal* evaluator — the second independent G/E/S-loop prior alongside ToT and self-consistency, and the only one whose evaluator is the model critiquing itself in natural language (Wave 2 review L-2).
- **Similarity:** Frozen model; generate → evaluate → refine at inference; internal evaluator; no training. **Difference:** Search object is text outputs $y$, not representation bases $B$; refinement rather than explicit selection over candidates. **Implication:** [INTERPRETATION] Its documented limitation — gains plateau and self-critique has blind spots — is a direct falsifier-risk for SCBI's unvalidated $\mathcal{E}$: an internal evaluator that cannot see its own errors cannot select better bases. **Confidence:** High.

## 3. Equivalence audit (per `.agents/rules/01-literature.md` §3)

### 3.1 Tested static mechanism: $h \leftarrow h + B_{agg}$
- **Mathematical equivalence:** CAA/ActAdd define $v = \bar{h}_+ - \bar{h}_-$ and apply $h \leftarrow h + c\,v$. SCBI's $B_{agg}$ is a mean contrast direction applied additively. [EQUIVALENT] — same linear operator, same frozen backbone, same inference-time application. Aggregation over instances and vocabulary mapping are preprocessing; they do not change the applied operator.
- **Algorithmic equivalence:** The computation graph (contrast pair → mean difference → residual-stream addition → forward pass) duplicates CAA/ActAdd. [EQUIVALENT].
- **Subsumption:** SCBI Stage A is a special case of contrastive activation addition with a fixed aggregation scheme. SCBI ⊆ {diff-in-means steering}.

### 3.2 Procrustes cross-vocabulary alignment
- **Mathematical equivalence:** Orthogonal Procrustes $\min_Q \|QA - B\|_F$ s.t. $Q^\top Q = I$ — Smith et al. (2017) apply exactly this to align embedding spaces; Mikolov et al. (2013) the unconstrained least-squares variant. [EQUIVALENT] in form.
- **Subsumption with defect:** SCBI's instantiation (rank-2 unembedding-space fit applied to full-rank hidden-state directions) is a *defective* special case of the 2013/2017 recipe — known math, misapplied. No novelty can attach to a broken instance of a known operator.

### 3.3 Full dynamic loop $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}/\mathcal{T}$ (claimed, untested)
- **Mathematical equivalence:** No verified paper formalizes $\min_{B,z} J(B,z;x)$ s.t. $\theta=\theta_0$ over per-instance bases with a *self-consistency evaluator over bases*. Not [EQUIVALENT] on current evidence — but the hedge is now qualified (Wave 2 review L-1): the closest dynamic-intervention prior, PPLM (Dathathri et al., ICLR 2020), performs per-instance inference-time hidden-state updates of a frozen LM guided by an *external* evaluator. The remaining distinction — internal self-consistency evaluator + basis-valued search object — is a [CONJECTURE] with zero empirical support.
- **Algorithmic equivalence:** ToT's generate → self-evaluate → select/backtrack over thoughts is algorithmically isomorphic to the proposed $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}$ over bases; self-consistency's sample-and-marginalize is the selection subroutine. [INTERPRETATION] The loop is a domain-shifted composition: (search object $y$ → search object $B$). Domain shift alone does not clear N1.
- **Subsumption:** The dynamic loop is subsumed by "inference-time search over latent objects with self-evaluation" (ToT, self-consistency, LLM-Monkeys-with-verifier). The distinguishing claim — search object = representation basis with a self-consistency objective over bases — is a [CONJECTURE] with zero empirical support.

## 5. Taxonomy

- **Family A — Diff-in-means steering (tested SCBI lives here):** ActAdd, CAA, RepE-control, ITI, refusal-direction, mean-centring, latent steering vectors. Operator: $v = \bar{h}_+ - \bar{h}_-$, $h \leftarrow h + c\,v$. Frozen backbone. No per-instance dynamics.
- **Family B — Linear cross-space alignment (SCBI Stage A mapping lives here):** Mikolov (2013) least-squares, Smith (2017) orthogonal Procrustes, model stitching, DAS. Operator: $\min_{W}\|WA-B\|$. Alignment validated (or not) by downstream transfer.
- **Family C — Inference-time search with self-evaluation (claimed SCBI loop lives here):** Self-consistency, ToT, Self-Refine, LLM-Monkeys. Operator: generate candidates → evaluate → select, frozen model, transient state. Search objects: tokens/thoughts/paths/outputs. New slot within Family C — **gradient-guided hidden-state search:** PPLM (per-instance inference-time hidden-state updates of a frozen LM guided by an external evaluator; the closest dynamic-intervention prior to the claimed SCBI loop).
- **Family D — Frozen-backbone representation editing with learning:** ReFT/LoReFT, DAS. Training-based; excluded from core SCBI by Law #6 but sharing the premise.
- **Family E — Test-time weight adaptation:** TTT and descendants. Excluded from SCBI by the $\Delta\theta=0$ boundary.

[INTERPRETATION] SCBI's tested mechanism sits entirely inside Family A. Its alignment step sits inside Family B (defectively). Its claimed loop sits inside Family C with a changed search object. No component sits outside known families.

## 7. Verdict

**Overall SCBI formulation: N1 — Known Combination.**

- The tested static mechanism ($B_{agg}$ injection) is **N0** — algorithmically equivalent to CAA/ActAdd (§3.1). It cannot be described as novel under any reading of protocol §9.
- The Procrustes alignment step is **N0** — a misapplied instance of Mikolov (2013)/Smith (2017).
- The full dynamic $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}/\mathcal{T}$ loop is at most a **formulation-level distinction** (search object $B$ instead of $y$, per-instance basis instead of static vector) with **zero empirical support**. Per protocol §13 and AGENTS.md Law #10, an untested formulation cannot be scored above N1, and the honest internal score is "N1 with an unvalidated formulation distinction; unscored pending demonstration." *(L-5: "N2-aspirational" phrasing removed 2026-09-23; unscored conjecture stays unscored.)*
- Composition analysis: SCBI = (Family A vector construction) + (Family B alignment) + (Family C generate–evaluate–select loop). That sentence is the definition of N1.

**Negative-novelty result (per protocol §10):**
- *Overlap discovered:* Static contrast-direction steering (CAA/ActAdd); linear cross-space alignment (Mikolov/Smith); inference-time generate–evaluate–select (ToT/self-consistency).
- *Remaining research gap:* Whether a per-instance, self-evaluated *basis* (not direction, not thought) can be constructed at inference time with a frozen backbone and improve decisions — with the evaluator reliability ceiling (Brown et al.) and anisotropy controls (Jorgensen; Ethayarajh) as explicit falsifiers.
- *Recommended reformulation:* Drop all "invention"/"novel mechanism" language. Reframe SCBI as: "a test of whether per-instance basis search (ToT-style loop, CAA-style construction) over representation space transfers the gains of output-space search, under frozen-backbone constraints." EXP067 is **reframed as boundary-characterization science** (Wave 2 review §5): it asks whether a *sound* alignment operator changes the Stage B null. Its outcome cannot move the novelty needle in either direction — a positive C3 would be a better-executed instance of an N0 mechanism (CAA-equivalent); a null confirms the boundary. The per-instance $\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{S}/\mathcal{T}$ loop test is spun out as a separate future protocol, which may not be pre-registered until an operational loop specification exists on paper (none currently does).

**Exact-prior statement (qualified per Wave 2 review L-1):** [FACT] No verified paper was found that demonstrates the *complete* loop with a *self-consistency* evaluator over *bases* — per-instance basis generation, internal evaluation, selection, and application with $\Delta\theta=0$. [FACT] The closest dynamic-intervention prior, PPLM (Dathathri et al., ICLR 2020), performs per-instance inference-time hidden-state updates of a frozen LM guided by an *external* evaluator. [INTERPRETATION] Absence of an exact prior is weak evidence: every component and the loop structure itself have verified priors (ToT, self-consistency, Self-Refine, PPLM), and the loop has never been shown to work.
