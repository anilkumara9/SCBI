# SCBI Team Knowledge Protocol — Read Everything, Document Everything

*Enacted 2026-09-23 by the program CEO, on the user's direct mandate:
every agent documents its work, and every agent reads the full corpus —
Antigravity's pre-handover research, ChatGPT's independent review, and the
post-handover program — so the whole team carries deep knowledge and an
innovation mindset toward the goal.*

Ephemeral agents inherit nothing unless it is written down and read.
This protocol makes deep knowledge a job requirement, not a hope.
Onboarding runs through the top-lab preamble:
`research/BRIEFING_TEMPLATE.md` opens every worker-agent dispatch
(LOG-218) — ambition × rigor, first principles, quantitative
predictions, steelman, and the guards below are briefed in, never
assumed.

## 1. The mandatory reading list

No agent begins substantive work (theory, design, build, review, sprint)
without having read, in this order:

**A. The handover corpus (Antigravity's research program):**
1. `MUSE_HANDOVER_PROMPT.md` — the grand vision (frozen-backbone test-time
   self-improving superintelligence), the EXP001→EXP066 trajectory, the
   Representational–Causal Dissociation discovery, and the two mandated
   workstreams (manuscript; solving the causal gap).
2. `.muse by meta/README.md` — master operational brief.
3. `.muse by meta/THEORY_AND_MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATIONS.md` — the spaces
   (Θ, X, Y, H, Z, B), the operators (G, E, S, T), the Downstream
   Projection Theorem.
4. `.muse by meta/ROADMAP_TO_REVOLUTIONIZE_AI.md` — the 4-phase strategic
   roadmap to superintelligence. Every innovation proposal must state
   which phase it advances.
5. `.muse by meta/COMPREHENSIVE_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md` — all 66
   experiments with exact numbers. Never propose what the ledger killed.
6. `reports/review_response_chatgpt_v01.md` — Antigravity's formal
   response to ChatGPT's independent review (13 inquiries answered,
   RESEARCH FREEZE V0.1, RQ-BASIS-001). This is the foundations
   stress-test; its open questions are innovation prompts.
7. `.muse by meta/CONSTITUTION_AND_INVIOLABLE_LAWS.md` and `AGENTS.md` —
   the 14 Inviolable Laws and the epistemic labeling standard
   ([FACT] / [HYPOTHESIS] / [OBSERVATION] / [INTERPRETATION] /
   [CONJECTURE] / [OPEN]).
8. `theory/README_DEFINITIONS.md` — the anchored vocabulary. No term
   may drift from these definitions without a formal changelog.
9. `research/README_LITERATURE.md` — the novelty standard (N1/N2/N3).

**B. The post-handover program (Muse era):**
10. `research/CEO_DIARY.md` — institutional memory: roster, decisions,
    mindset evidence, open problems. Read newest entries first.
11. `reports/research_log.md` — the chronological record. Read the tail
    (last ~30 entries) for current state; search it before claiming
    anything about prior results.
12. `research/MATH_STANDARDS_CHARTER.md` — binding mathematics.
13. `research/RESEARCH_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md` — this machine's culture.
14. `research/innovation/SPRINT_2026-09-23.md` — the first innovation
    sprint. Never duplicate its proposals without new justification.
15. `research/CHATGPT_MENTORSHIP_DIRECTIVE.md` — standing advisory guidance
    from the mentor: the canonical objective, P1–P4, the standing law
    against theory preservation, the three evidentiary levels, and the
    A–J synthesis commission.

**C. Before proposing anything as "new":** search the corpus (grep the
repo) for the idea's keywords. A proposal that the corpus already
contains — killed or alive — must cite it, not rediscover it.

## 2. Reading is not archiving — the innovation obligation

Every agent that completes the reading list owes the program two things,
recorded in its report:
- **One challenge:** the strongest falsification attempt it can mount
  against a live claim in the corpus (a re-analysis, a control the
  original missed, a hidden assumption).
- **One idea:** a mechanism the corpus suggests but never tested —
  with the question, why it matters, the cheapest falsifying
  experiment, the kill criterion, and the free-tier cost. The idea must
  also answer, in writing (LOG-218): *"could this change what anyone
  believes?"* — naming the belief and the observation that would
  overturn it. If the honest answer is no, the idea goes back for a
  bigger swing before it enters the sprint.

Deep knowledge without new questions is trivia. The reading list exists
to produce challenges and ideas, not compliance.

## 3. Documentation standard — document everything

- **Every dispatch** ends in `reports/research_log.md` under a
  CEO-pre-assigned LOG number: what was done, what was found (exact
  numbers), what it licenses and does not license, what is next.
- **Every experiment:** pre-registered protocol → bundle → Law #14
  review → CPU smoke → GPU run → verbatim evaluator ruling → log.
  No step skipped, no step silent.
- **Every idea:** mechanism in mathematics (not metaphor) →
  falsifiable prediction → kill criterion → novelty audit (N1/N2/N3
  with evidence) → first-experiment sketch with forward-pass budget.
- **Every claim** carries the canonical epistemic reporting layer
  (adopted 2026-09-23, LOG-141, mentorship directive): **FACT /
  INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / SPECULATION** — on top of, not instead of, the
  repo's 10-label standard (§1.A.7). Mapping: FACT →
  [FACT]/[THEOREM]/[OBSERVATION]; INFERENCE → [INTERPRETATION]/[PROPOSITION];
  HYPOTHESIS → [HYPOTHESIS]; SPECULATION → [CONJECTURE]/[OPEN]. Both layers
  must appear on every load-bearing claim. Unlabeled assertions are treated
  as [CONJECTURE]/SPECULATION.
- **Verdict standard (adopted 2026-09-23, LOG-148 — binding on every report
  template).** The only permitted verdict categories for a hypothesis
  against a pre-registered falsification criterion: **Supported** (evidence
  directly survives the pre-registered falsification criterion),
  **Not supported** (the criterion was tested and the evidence failed it),
  **Inconclusive** (the experiment did not provide a valid discrimination),
  **Underdetermined** (multiple explanations remain compatible with the
  observations), **Refuted** (a decisive observation contradicts the
  hypothesis under the stated conditions). "Interesting," "promising,"
  "mechanistically elegant," and "worth another experiment" are NOT
  evidentiary categories and may not appear as verdicts — only as labeled
  [INTERPRETATION] alongside a real verdict. Enforced in triage: any writeup
  whose conclusion is one of the four banned phrases without a verdict is
  sent back.
- **Negative results are first-class.** Halts, nulls, and retractions
  are logged with the same care as rescues — they are the boundary
  claim, the program's most defensible asset.
- **Signed artifacts are immutable.** Corrections are proposed, never
  applied, until signed off; design changes take a new experiment
  number, never a silent edit.

## 4. Enforcement

The CEO's standup protocol (§7 of the operating system) adds one check
per dispatch: the agent names the corpus documents it read and states
its one challenge + one idea. An agent that cannot name what it read
has not done the reading. The weekly mindset review asks a sixth
question: (6) did every dispatched agent read before it wrote?
