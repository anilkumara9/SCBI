# SCBI 30-Day Autonomous Campaign Charter

**Issued:** 2026-09-25, by direct founder order.
**Mandate:** Run the research lab 24/7 for 30 days (through 2026-10-25) with no human in the loop, working continuously toward the summit — frozen models that think at conscious, human level; superhuman intelligence. Full latitude on research direction. Do not stop. Do not idle.

## The two human gates (physical, not policy)

Everything else runs autonomously. Only these two require the founder:
1. **GPU execution** — the K2→EXP087 queue lives on the founder's Kaggle/Colab. The lab makes every queued experiment honestly launch-ready; the founder runs the notebooks.
2. **GitHub pushes** — commits are prepared locally after every cycle; each push needs a fresh founder-supplied token (used once, never stored).

## Lanes (all CPU, $0, always moving)

1. **GPU-queue readiness** — close every unresolved launch-chain gate so the queue is honestly ready, not nominally ready.
2. **Mechanism innovation** — generate and pre-register new mechanisms aimed at the summit question: *what discovery would have to be true for a frozen model to become far more cognitively capable through inference-time computation?* Summit filter is mandatory; elegance is not a license.
3. **Null re-mining** — every null, kill, and retraction is ore. Re-analyze all primary artifacts for overlooked structure with new mathematics.
4. **Frontier watch** — daily sweep of releases, architectures, features; adapt, never wholesale-steal; scoreboard stays current.
5. **Artifact integrity** — paper draft, CLLC, HQ, and the research log stay truthful and current.

## Non-negotiable law (the challenge does not suspend this)

- Pre-registered falsification before any claim. Signed protocols immutable. Independent Law #14 review with binding verdicts. Every null/kill/halt preserved. No untested code reaches the GPU queue. Primary artifacts outrank summaries.
- "Do whatever you want" licenses the full research space — including trainable probes as new pre-registered experiments — but never silent scope changes: a design change is a new experiment number, always.
- $0 spend. No external publication. No account/credential changes.

## Cadence

- **Every 2h:** next-wave dispatcher (existing) — if workers active, stand down; if idle and licensed CPU work exists, dispatch exactly one wave.
- **Daily ~07:00:** strategy review — assess campaign state, set the day's priorities, kill stale lines.
- **Every ~15 min:** HQ live monitor (truthful polling, not a stream).
- **Weekly:** founder briefing — verdicts, kills, and the honest scoreboard. Failures first.

## Scoreboard honesty

Week 0 (2026-09-25): zero autonomous capability entries; all static families 0.0pp; EXP091 KILL adopted (free-lunch falsified). The campaign succeeds by verdicts, not by activity. A month of clean kills is a successful month if the kills are real — but the summit demands a surviving mechanism, and the lab does not stop until one is found or the space is exhausted.
